⚡ Quick Revision Box (Unit 3)

◌ Not started
  1. ρ = m/V; 1 kg/m³ = 10⁻³ g/cm³.
  2. SG = ρ_substance/ρ_water; unitless; water at 4 °C (highest density).
  3. Stress = F/A (N/m²); strain = ΔL/L₀, ΔV/V₀, Δx/L₀ (dimensionless).
  4. Hooke's law: within elastic limit, stress ∝ strain.
  5. Young modulus Y = (F/A)/(ΔL/L₀), unit Pa.
  6. Steel = highest Young modulus among common metals (most elastic).
  7. Static equilibrium: ΣF = 0 and Στ = 0.
  8. Torque τ = F·r·sin θ (N·m); if perpendicular, τ = F·r.
  9. Seesaw balance: m₁g·x₁ = m₂g·x₂.
