3.4 Phrasal Verbs: "get"

The exact Unit 3 list (Activity 3.15) is:

Phrasal verbMeaningExample
get aheadsucceed / make progressHard work helps you get ahead.
get alongbe on friendly terms; manageI get along well with my classmates.
get around tofinally do something you postponedI finally got around to writing the essay.
get awayescape / leaveI need to get away from the city.
get backreturnI'll get back to you next week.
get back attake revenge onHe tried to get back at his rival.
get back toreturn to someone/contact laterI'll get back to you with the answer.
get behindfall behind; supportThey couldn't get behind the project.
get on bysurvive on what you haveThey get on by their monthly salary.
get downdepress; write downThe bad news got him down.
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: This unit's ten "get" phrasal verbs are exactly: get ahead, get along, get around to, get away, get back, get back at, get back to, get behind, get on by, get down. (More "get" verbs — get in, get off, get on, get out, get over, get over with, get through, get to — are taught separately in Unit 4; don't mix the two lists.)
