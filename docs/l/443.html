6A Listening Skills: What is Global Warming?

◌ Not started
  • Global warming = the increase in the average temperature of the earth's atmosphere because an increased amount of energy (heat) striking the earth from the sun is being trapped in the atmosphere and not radiated out into space.
  • The problem is not that too little sun warmth reaches the earth, but that too much is being kept/trapped in the atmosphere (the greenhouse effect), so the earth is warming faster than at any previous time in history.
  • Vocabulary to note (parts of speech): radiated, acted, reflective, terrible, trapped.
  • Listen, fill gaps, and discuss a local problem caused by global warming and its solutions.
