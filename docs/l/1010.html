Word formation — prefixes (negative, opinion, repetition)

The unit lists common prefixes that change meaning. Know these and be able to derive new words:

  • re- (again): rewrite, rebuild, recycle
  • in- / im- (not): invisible, impossible
  • dis- (not/opposite): disagree, disappear
  • un- (not): unhappy, unable
  • mis- (wrongly): misunderstand, mislead
  • co- (together): cooperate, co-worker
  • none (not): nonexistent
  • pro- (for/forward): proactive, promote
  • super- (above): supernatural, supermarket
  • hyper- (excessive): hypertension, hyperactive
  • circum- (around): circumstance, circumference
  • auto- (self): autobiography, automatic
  • anti- (against): anti-social, antibiotic
  • im- (into): intake, import
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: The textbook's example derivations are take → intake and tension → hypertension. Recognise the prefix and you can guess meaning: in-/im-/un-/dis-/mis- usually negate; re- repeats; co- together; super-/hyper- above/excess.
