1.4 The Mission of Physics and Career Awareness

◌ Not started
  • The mission of physics = to advance science, engineering, and innovation throughout the world for the benefit of all and in serving society.
  • Typical physics careers (job opportunities) include: space & astronomy, healthcare, engineering, energy, technology, geophysics and meteorology, and research scientist roles.

✍️ Must-know (career fields): astronomy/space, healthcare, engineering, energy, technology, meteorology/geophysics, research scientist.
