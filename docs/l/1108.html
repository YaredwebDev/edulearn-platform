6.4 Phrasal Verbs: clear, come, clog, close

Phrasal verbMeaningExample
clear upto become clear; to solve/explainThe sky cleared up after the rain.
come downto decrease; to descendPrices came down last month.
come aboutto happen; to occurHow did the change come about?
clog upto block; to become blockedRubbish can clog up the drains.
come acrossto find by chance; to give an impressionI came across an old photo.
come apartto break into piecesThe old book came apart.
close downto stop operatingThe factory closed down.
come backto returnHe came back from the market.
close offto block the entrance toPolice closed off the road.
Note: ✋ Trap: come down = decrease/descend; come about = happen; come across = find by chance (or "give the impression of being"). close down = cease operating.
