1.3 Related Fields to Physics

Physics is the foundation of many important scientific disciplines:

  • Chemistry: deals with interactions of atoms/molecules, but is rooted in atomic and molecular physics.
  • Engineering: most branches apply physics (architecture uses physics for structural stability, acoustics, heating, lighting, cooling).
  • Geology: relies on physics for radioactive dating, earthquake analysis, and heat transfer across Earth's surface.
  • Biophysics: applies the principles and methods of physics to study biological phenomena.
  • Geophysics: applies physics to the study of the Earth.
  • Medical Physics: diagnostics and medical therapy (X-rays, MRI, ultrasonic blood flow measurements).
✍️ Must-know: Physics is the foundation of Chemistry, Engineering, Geology, Biophysics, Geophysics, and Medical Physics.
