3A Listening Skills

  • Listen to a lecture on causes/effects of road accidents worldwide.
  • Key ideas (true/false answers): road accidents are preventable and need immediate, concerted action; it is not true they are steadily declining; the impact is significant (not insignificant) in low-income countries; there are differences between high- & low-income countries in prevention.
  • Organise into a causes → effects → solutions table (e.g. cause: poor road quality, speed, non-use of seat belts/helmets).
