3.4 Introduction

◌ Not started

An organic molecule is a compound that contains carbon and is found in living things. Carbon forms large, complex molecules via covalent bonding.

Four principal groups: Carbohydrates, Lipids, Proteins, Nucleic acids.

Monomers and polymers (Table 3.3):

Macromolecule Elements Monomer
Proteins C, H, O, N Amino acids
Lipids C, H, O Glycerol and fatty acids
Carbohydrates C, H, O Monosaccharides (glucose, galactose, fructose)
Nucleic acids C, H, O, N, P Nucleotides

Monomers = small single molecular units; polymers = long chains of many monomers joined by strong covalent bonds (polymerization).
