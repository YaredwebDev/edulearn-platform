10.2 Vocabulary of the Unit (matric-ready)

Vocabulary taken directly from the reading passage:

WordMeaningExample (context)
counter (¶1)the opposite point; a counter-argumentFor each positive aspect there is a negative counter-point.
vigorous (¶2)energetic; strongGone are the days of vigorous handwriting practice.
legibleclear enough to be readAssignments are now neat and legible.
scope (¶2)room/opportunity; rangeThere is little scope for spelling errors.
pervasive (¶4)spreading widely through everythingComputers are becoming pervasive in our lives.
caste (¶5)a social class/group based on birthIt builds a global family instead of groups based on community and caste.
garner (¶6)to gather/collect; to gainPeople use the internet to garner support.
dexterity (¶7)skill of the hands; nimblenessVideo games can improve reaction time and dexterity.
deteriorating (¶8)becoming worseCommunicative skills are deteriorating.
boon (¶8)a benefit; a blessing (opposite: bane)Used wisely, the computer is a boon, not a bane.
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: boon (blessing) vs bane (curse/source of harm) is a key contrast in the passage. deteriorating = getting worse. pervasive = widespread throughout.
