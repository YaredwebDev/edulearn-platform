6.3 Phrasal Verbs: "go"

◌ Not started
Phrasal verb Meaning
go around go to many places; circulate
go away leave; disappear
go ahead proceed; begin
go through examine/search; experience
go into enter; discuss in detail
go back return
go back on break a promise
go beyond exceed the limit
go by pass (time); follow
go down decrease; fall
go for choose; attack
go in for be interested in / take part in
go on continue

✍️ Must-know: In the argumentative-communication text: "go around the bush" (avoid the point), "go through others' ideas," "go beyond the limit," "go by the rules," "go on with the discussion," "go back on your promise" (break a promise). Learn these in context.
