3.1 Reading: Traffic Accidents

◌ Not started

(the core "story"/text of the unit)

The passage explains the causes of traffic accidents and how to prevent them.

Causes of traffic accidents: - Breaking traffic rules and regulations. - Jumping/ignoring red lights. - Drunk driving (driving under the influence of alcohol). - Speeding / reckless driving.

Prevention & safety measures: - Wearing seat belts and helmets. - Respecting speed limits. - Following traffic rules and signals.

✍️ Must-know: Questions test (a) causes vs prevention, (b) safety equipment (seat belts, helmets, speed limits), and (c) vocabulary in context.
