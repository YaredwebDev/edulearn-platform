⚡ Quick Revision Box (Unit 6)

  1. v = λ·f and f = 1/T (T = 1/f).
  2. Pendulum: T = 2π√(L/g) — independent of mass.
  3. Spring: F = −kx (Hooke's law); T = 2π√(m/k).
  4. Mechanical waves need a medium (sound, water); EM waves don't (light).
  5. Transverse: propagation ⊥ vibration. Longitudinal: propagation ∥ vibration (sound).
  6. Sound speed in air at 0 °C = 331 m/s; at 20 °C ≈ 343 m/s.
  7. Sound: fastest in solids, slowest in gases.
  8. Echo = reflection of sound; used in SONAR.
  9. Standing wave: distance between adjacent nodes/antinodes = λ/2; node to antinode = λ/4.
  10. Pitch ← frequency; loudness ← amplitude (dB); noise harmful > 75 dB, painful > 120 dB.
  11. Compressions/rarefactions in longitudinal waves (like crest/trough).
