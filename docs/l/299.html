🧮 Key Expressions to Memorise

◌ Not started
  • rate = Δc/Δt
  • aA + bB → cC + dD: rate = −(1/a)Δ[A]/Δt = −(1/b)Δ[B]/Δt = +(1/c)Δ[C]/Δt = +(1/d)Δ[D]/Δt
  • Rate ∝ number of effective collisions per second
  • Rule of thumb: rate roughly doubles per ~10 °C rise

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) define rate and write correct rate expressions for any balanced reaction; (2) compute average and instantaneous rates from data/graphs (tangents) and identify initial rate; (3) state the three pre-conditions for reaction (collision, orientation, activation energy); (4) predict how nature of reactant, surface area, concentration/pressure, temperature and catalysts change the rate and explain why in collision-theory terms; (5) classify positive vs negative and homogeneous vs heterogeneous catalysts?
