12.2 The Scope of Biodiversity

◌ Not started

Biodiversity deals with the diversity of: - plant species — crop genetic resources & forest genetic resources; - animal species — farm-animal genetic resources & wildlife genetic resources; - microbial genetic resources. - Assessment can be at different scales (specific sites/regions, country or global) depending on the objective — e.g. a single forest/water body vs national biodiversity status (all habitats + all species).
