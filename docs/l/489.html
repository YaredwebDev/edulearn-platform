4.3 Phrasal Verbs: burn, call, calm, care, carry

◌ Not started
Phrasal verb Meaning Example
burn up to be destroyed by fire; to consume (energy) The dry grass burned up quickly.
call back to return a phone call I'll call back in an hour.
call for to require/need; to request This task calls for patience.
call up to telephone; to summon She called up her friend.
call off to cancel They called off the meeting.
calm down to become/make less excited or angry He took deep breaths to calm down.
care for to look after; to like The rangers care for the animals.
carry out to perform/complete (a task) Scientists carried out a survey.

✋ Trap: call off = cancel; call for = require; call up = telephone/recruit; carry out = perform. Pick the meaning from the object/context.
