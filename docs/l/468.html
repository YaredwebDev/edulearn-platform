10B.1 Expressing dissatisfaction

  • Expressions: It is dissatisfying/disappointing; It's very disappointing; It's terrible; I'm disappointed with…; What a terrible/awful…; How awful! It's terrible/annoying; You should have done/known better; I want to complain about…; I have a complaint to make; (I'm afraid) it just isn't good enough.
  • Construct sentences & dialogues showing dissatisfaction with AI replacing human labour (e.g. fear of losing jobs, companies apologising but not acting).

10B.2 Debate

  • Debate proposition: "Artificial intelligence is very important to Ethiopia." List arguments for/against using agree, disagree, objection, in my opinion, etc.

10B.3 Making a public speech

  • AI has posed a threat to human labour and caused public complaint; propose a solution and give a public speech about the threat AI poses.
