5.2 Types of muscles

◌ Not started

The muscular system has ~700 muscles (about half of body weight). Three types: 1. Cardiac muscle — the heart muscle; forms the thick middle layer (myocardium) between the outer pericardium and inner endocardium; supplied via the coronary circulation. It is involuntary, striated; individual cells joined by intercalated discs. 2. Skeletal muscle — attached to bones, arranged in opposing pairs around joints; ~30–40% of total body mass; voluntary (striated). Skeletal muscle fibers innervated by a single motor axon = a motor unit. 3. Smooth muscle — involuntary, non-striated, unconsciously controlled; found in the walls of hollow organs (stomach, intestine, bladder, uterus), blood & lymph vessels, respiratory/urinary/reproductive tracts, eyes (ciliary muscle). The erector pili of the skin make hair stand erect (cold/heat/fear).

✍️ Must-know: cardiac = involuntary + striated + only in heart (intercalated discs); skeletal = voluntary + striated; smooth = involuntary + non-striated (hollow organs). The muscular system comprises cardiac, skeletal and smooth muscles (True). White blood cells protect against infection.
