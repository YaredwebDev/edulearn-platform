1.2 Why Do We Study Biology?

  • Humans have inborn curiosity about the natural world that leads them to study living organisms and their habitats.
  • Biology helps us understand how living things work, function, and interact with the environment.
  • Examples of biological application:
  • Discovery of drugs to treat human diseases.
  • Understanding how our bodies, ecosystems, genetics, and cell biology work — transforming medicine and agriculture.
  • Use of forensic biology to investigate crime.
  • Understanding global warming through ecology.
  • Fermentation used to produce beer and wine.
  • Treating patients with AIDS, tuberculosis, and some types of cancer.
Note: 📌 Applications of Biology in daily life (note box):
Note: - Wine-making, brewing, baking of bread, and cheese production depend on fermentation by yeasts, fungi, and bacteria.
Note: - Antibiotics such as penicillin are produced by mould fungi or bacteria.
Note: - Industrial chemicals like citric acid or lactic acid need bacteria or fungi.
Note: - Sewage disposal depends on bacteria in filter beds.
Note: - DNA structure discovery and forensic analysis of DNA samples from crime scenes.
