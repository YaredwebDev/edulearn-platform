13.3 Strategies of Mitigating Climate Change

◌ Not started

Mitigation = activities to reduce or remove greenhouse-gas emissions. Works two ways: ① reduce greenhouse-gas emissions; ② enhance removal of gases by carbon sinks.

13.3.1 Reducing greenhouse-gas emissions from sources

  • Energy: fossil-fuel burning was the largest source of human-induced CO₂. Shift to renewable energy (solar, wind, hydropower) to cut emissions.
  • Deforestation: clearing forests releases stored carbon. Reducing deforestation cuts forest carbon emissions.

13.3.2 Enhancing carbon sinks

  • Afforestation = planting new forests, which store carbon (biomass, forest floor, soils) and remove CO₂ → enhances sink capacity, mitigates climate change.

13.3.3 Improved soil management

  • Soils store a large amount of carbon — ~2× more carbon is stored in soil than in the whole atmosphere. Soil conservation & management enhance soil carbon storage. Practices: crop rotation, mixed cropping, reduced soil tillage, mulching, terraces & other physical soil-conservation structures.
