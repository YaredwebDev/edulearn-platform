4.2 Methods of Charging a Body

◌ Not started

Charging = the process of electrifying bodies by removing or adding charges. Three methods:

1. Charging by rubbing (friction): two different neutral materials rubbed together → charges transfer. Leaves the two bodies with opposite signs of charge. (e.g., comb + hair.)

2. Charging by conduction (contact): a charged object touches a neutral object → electrons move. Leaves both bodies with the same sign of charge but the neutral one weaker. (e.g., positively charged aluminium plate touches neutral sphere → sphere becomes positive.)

3. Charging by induction: a charged object is held near (but does not touch) a grounded neutral conductor. Leaves the bodies with opposite sign of charge. (e.g., negatively charged ebonite rod near a grounded metal sphere → sphere becomes positive.)

✍️ Must-know: Rubbing → opposite signs; conduction → same sign; induction → opposite sign.
