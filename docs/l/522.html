10.3 Phrasal Verbs: cut, do, dress

◌ Not started

Cut + particle (from the unit's exercise — choose the right particle):

Phrasal verb Meaning Example
cut down (on) to reduce/consume less Cut down the amount of food you eat.
cut up to chop into pieces She cut up the meat for her brother.
cut back (on) to reduce The doctor told him to cut back on salt.
cut off to interrupt/disconnect My phone call was cut off.
cut out to stop doing (a habit) He had to cut out smoking.

Do + particle: | Phrasal verb | Meaning | Example | |---|---|---| | do without | to manage without | There is no sugar, so I'll have to do without it. | | do over | to do again | I'll do it over because my first attempt failed. | | do away with | to abolish/remove | Many countries did away with the death penalty. | | do up | to fasten; to repair/renovate | He did up his coat. | | do with | to need/want; to deal with | People were employed to deal with the backlog. |

Dress: | Phrasal verb | Meaning | Example | |---|---|---| | dress up | to put on formal/elaborate clothes | It's not customary for men to dress up as women. | | dress down | to dress informally; to scold | She dressed down for the picnic. |

✋ Trap: cut down / cut back on = reduce; cut off = disconnect/interrupt; cut out = stop a habit; do away with = abolish; do without = manage without; do over = repeat. Learn the particle changes meaning.
