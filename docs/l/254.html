5.3 Breathing System

◌ Not started

5.3.1 Anatomy

Air passage: Nose (nasal cavity) → Pharynx → Larynx → Trachea → Bronchi → Bronchioles → Alveoli.

  • Nose: warms, moistens, filters air (cilia and mucus).
  • Pharynx: common passage for air and food.
  • Larynx (voice box): contains vocal cords (sound production); the glottis is the opening to it; the epiglottis covers it during swallowing.
  • Trachea (windpipe): tube with C-shaped cartilage rings; lined with ciliated mucus membrane.
  • Bronchi: two branches into the lungs; divide into bronchioles.
  • Alveoli: tiny air sacs where gas exchange occurs; surrounded by capillaries; enormous surface area.
  • Lungs: right (3 lobes) and left (2 lobes); enclosed by the pleural membranes.
  • Diaphragm: sheet of muscle below the lungs.

5.3.2 Mechanism of Breathing

Inspiration (inhalation): - Diaphragm contracts and flattens; rib cage moves up and out (external intercostal muscles contract). - Volume of the thorax increases, pressure decreases → air rushes in.

Expiration (exhalation): - Diaphragm relaxes and moves up; rib cage moves down and in. - Volume decreases, pressure increases → air forced out.

Lung volumes: Tidal volume (~500 mL), vital capacity (~4.5–5 L in adults), residual volume (air left after maximal expiration).

5.3.3 Gaseous Exchange and Composition of Air

Gas Inspired air Expired air
O₂ 21% 16%
CO₂ 0.04% 4%
N₂ 78% 78%
  • Gas exchange in the alveoli occurs by diffusion (O₂ into blood, CO₂ out).
  • O₂ is carried by haemoglobin as oxyhaemoglobin.

5.3.4 Respiratory Disorders

  • Bronchitis: inflammation of the bronchi (often from smoking/infection).
  • Emphysema: destruction of alveolar walls → reduced gas exchange (COPD).
  • Asthma: narrowing of airways due to inflammation/bronchospasm.
  • COPD (Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease): umbrella term (chronic bronchitis + emphysema).
  • Pneumonia: infection/inflammation of alveoli.
  • Tuberculosis (TB): bacterial infection of the lungs (Mycobacterium tuberculosis).
  • Lung cancer: often linked to smoking.

✍️ Must-know: Inspiration = diaphragm contracts/flattens, volume up, pressure down. Expired air: O₂ 16%, CO₂ 4%.
