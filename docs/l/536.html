2.6 Applications

◌ Not started

Maximum area problems (quadratic): Example: A 56 m wire is bent into a rectangle. Perimeter 2x+2y=56 → y=28−x. Area A=x(28−x)=−x²+28x = 196−(x−14)². Maximum area 196 m² at x=14, y=14 (a square of side 14 m).

Example (min product): Two numbers differ by 16, minimize the product. y=x−16, A=x(x−16)=(x−8)²−64. Minimum product −64 at x=8, y=−8.

Open box (volume): Removing x×x squares from an 80×50 cm card gives V=4x³−260x²+4000x. (x=20 → compute; x cannot be 30 because two corners' squares would overlap: 2x≤50.)

✍️ Must-know: In optimization word problems: (1) define variables, (2) write the quantity to optimize as a function, (3) put into vertex form by completing the square, (4) read the max/min and the x that gives it.
