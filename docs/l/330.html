  1. Z = protons; A = protons + neutrons; neutrons = A − Z.
  2. Electron ~1/2000 proton mass; proton 1.0073 amu, neutron 1.0087 amu.
  3. Isotopes = same protons, different neutrons. Isobars = same mass number.
  4. Electron discovered by Thomson (1897); nucleus by Rutherford; neutron by Chadwick (1932); proton by Goldstein/Rutherford.
  5. Thomson: plum pudding; Rutherford: planetary/nuclear; Bohr: stationary orbits.
  6. Max electrons per shell = 2n² (K=2, L=8, M=18, N=32).
  7. Electronic config of Ca (Z=20) = 2, 8, 8, 2; Ar (18) = 2, 8, 8.
  8. Average atomic mass = (Σ % × mass)/100.
  9. Valence electrons = outermost shell electrons; determine chemical properties.
  10. 1 amu = 1/12 mass of carbon-12 = 1.66×10⁻²⁴ g (the Dalton).
