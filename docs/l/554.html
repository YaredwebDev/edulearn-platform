  • Circle: A=πr², P=2πr; infinitely many lines of symmetry (any diameter).
  • Center–midpoint of chord ⊥ chord; perpendicular from center bisects chord.
  • Equal chords equidistant from center; equal chords subtend equal central angles.
  • Inscribed angle = ½ central angle (same arc); equal on same arc; 90° in semicircle.
  • Cyclic quadrilateral: opposite angles sum to 180°.
  • Arc s=2πr·θ/360; chord l=2r·sin(θ/2).
  • Sector area=πr²·θ/360; segment area=sector−triangle.
  • Chords inside: angle=½(sum arcs). Secants/tangents outside: angle=½(difference arcs).
  • Tangent ⊥ radius; equal tangents from external point.
