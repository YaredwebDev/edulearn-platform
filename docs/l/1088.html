2.3 Phrasal Verbs: blow, boil, break

Phrasal verbMeaningExample
blow awayto impress greatly; to carry off by windThe results blew us away.
blow offto (allow to) be carried away by wind; to ignoreHe blew off the meeting.
blow outto extinguish (a flame); to burstShe blew out the candles.
blow upto explode; to inflate; to become very angryA tyre blew up on the road.
boil down toto be ultimately a matter ofIt all boils down to hard work.
break into enter by force; to interruptThieves broke in at night.
break out(war/fire/disease) to start suddenlyA fire broke out in the warehouse.
break throughto force a way through; to make a discoveryScientists broke through with a new vaccine.
break downto stop working; to collapse; to become upsetThe car broke down on the way.
break offto separate; to end abruptlyShe broke off the conversation.
Note: ✋ Trap: break down has several meanings (machine stops / someone becomes emotionally upset / negotiations fail / to analyse into parts). Let the context decide.
