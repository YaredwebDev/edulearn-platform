5.3 Word Formation: Blending & Clipping

Blending = two words combined, keeping part of each: breakfast + lunch = brunch; smoke + fog = smog; motor + hotel = motel.

Clipping = a short form taken from a longer word without changing its meaning:

  • advertisement → ad
  • alligator → gator
  • examination → exam
  • gasoline → gas
  • gymnasium → gym
  • influenza → flu
  • laboratory → lab
  • mathematics → maths
  • telephone → phone
  • refrigerator → fridge
  • university → uni
  • professor → prof
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Blending creates a new word from parts of two words; clipping shortens a single word. In a blend the suffix is often detachable; a clip keeps the original meaning. Examples: brunch (blend), ad/exam/maths/flu/phone (clips).
