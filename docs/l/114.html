2.3 Asexual reproduction

◌ Not started

Reproduction = the process by which living organisms duplicate themselves, ensuring continuity of their species.

  • Involves a single individual; no fusion of gametes from two parents.
  • More common among invertebrates than vertebrates.
  • Budding and fragmentation — most common, especially in aquatic animals.
  • Parthenogenesis — unfertilised eggs develop into new offspring (some insects and vertebrates; e.g. honeybees/aphids).

✍️ Must-know: development of a female gamete (egg) into a new organism WITHOUT fertilization = parthenogenesis (review MCQ). Common features of budding/fragmentation/parthenogenesis: single parent, no gamete fusion, genetically identical offspring (mitosis-based).
