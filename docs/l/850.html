2.5 Seeds

A seed (fertilized ovule) has three parts: seed embryo, cotyledon/endosperm (reserve food), and seed coat. In angiosperms the seed is also covered by the fruit.

Seed embryo parts: radicle (future root), epicotyl, hypocotyl, and plumule (future shoot).

Difference between dicot and monocot seeds (Table 2.1)

Dicot seed (e.g., bean)Monocot seed (e.g., corn/maize)
Two cotyledonsOne cotyledon
Cotyledons are fleshy, store foodCotyledon thin, lacks food
Endosperm absentEndosperm large and well developed
Primary root from radicle bears many lateral rootsPrimary root replaced by adventitious fibrous roots
