13.5 Indigenous Knowledge in Climate Change Mitigation Strategies

◌ Not started
  • Gedeo environmental protection (southern Ethiopia): the Gedeo have rich indigenous knowledge encouraging environmental protection. They respect nature — mountains, trees, hillsides, riversides are seen as intermediates connecting God (creator) and man (creation). Protecting natural resources is a way of expressing desire & prayer to God; forests/wildlife/water should be respected like humans; some forest patches are sacred & protected. This tradition maintained Gedeo agroforestry systems for hundreds of years, enhancing climate-mitigation potential.
  • Konso indigenous soil conservation (south-western Ethiopia): traditional landscape of indigenous stone terraces used for soil conservation for hundreds of years. Terraces protect soil from erosion, collect maximum water & discharge the excess, and create suitable spaces for agriculture. Improved (protected) soils store carbon → helps mitigate climate change.
