2.14 🧮 Key facts/definitions to memorise

◌ Not started
  • Metamorphosis: complete 4 stages (egg–larva–pupa–adult); incomplete 3 stages (egg–nymph–adult); regulated by ecdysone
  • Bird oviduct: infundibulum (fertilization/engulfs yolk) → magnum (albumen) → isthmus (shell membranes) → uterus (shell) → vagina/cloaca (lay egg)
  • Rat gestation: brown 22–24 d, black ~22 d, giant mole 37–49 d; 7–12 pups/litter; male rats give no parental care
  • Insects economic: ~35% crops via pollination; 1,462+ edible insect species
  • Industrial products: honey/wax (honeybee), silk (silkworm Bombyx mori), shellac (lac insect Laccifer lacca), cochineal (Dactylopius coccus), tannic acid (Cynipidae galls)
  • Medicine: honey (wounds), bee/ant venom (joint pain), cantharidin (blister beetle; viral/cancer), grasshoppers (pain/migraine)
  • Disease vectors: Anopheles→malaria; Culex→filariasis; tsetse fly→sleeping sickness; housefly→food/water-borne
  • Learned behavior scientists: classical=Pavlov, operant=Skinner
  • Homeostasis 4 components: stimulus–receptor–control center(hypothalamus)–effector
  • Glucose: insulin (glucose→glycogen, lowers), glucagon (glycogen→glucose, raises)
  • Thermoregulation: cold→vasoconstriction+shivering+insulation; hot→vasodilation+sweating; heat exchange = radiation/evaporation/convection/conduction

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) list the animal characteristics & distinguish invertebrate/vertebrate (and chordate vs vertebrate); (2) compare asexual vs sexual reproduction and external vs internal fertilization; (3) order embryonic stages (cleavage→blastula→gastrula→organogenesis) and say which forms germ layers; (4) explain complete vs incomplete metamorphosis and reproduction in frogs/crocodiles/birds/rats including the oviduct parts and rat gestation; (5) give beneficial AND harmful economic examples of insects; (6) define and give examples of innate (instinct/reflex/orientation: taxis/kinesis) vs learned (habituation, classical/operant conditioning, sensitization, insight) behaviour and the patterns (cycles, reproductive, social, competition, territoriality, communication); (7) explain thermoregulation (poikilotherm vs homeotherm; vasodilation/vasoconstriction/shivering/sweating; SA:V ratio), osmoregulation (kidney, ADH) and blood-sugar regulation (insulin/glucagon); and (8) state how negative feedback maintains homeostasis? Re-answer the review MCQs too (autotrophs not an animal trait → bilateral symmetry; germ layers from gastrula; parthenogenesis; ecdysone controls metamorphosis; infundibulum = bird fertilization site; pupa = resting stage; circadian rhythm in darkness; correct negative-feedback order stimulus→receptor→control center→effector; insulin converts glucose→glycogen when high).
