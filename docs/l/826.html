5.7 Combining reactions — multiply the constants

◌ Not started

If a reaction is the sum of two or more steps, the overall equilibrium constant is the product of the individual constants: K_overall = K₁ × K₂ × K₃…. Reversing a reaction gives 1/K; multiplying an equation by ½ raises K to the ½ power.

Worked Example 5.5: Find KC for ½N₂ + ½O₂ + ½Br₂ ⇌ NOBr from (i) 2NO ⇌ N₂ + O₂, K₁ = 2.4×10³⁰ and (ii) NO + ½Br₂ ⇌ NOBr, K₂ = 1.4. Reverse & halve (i): ½N₂ + ½O₂ ⇌ NO, K₁' = (1/2.4×10³⁰)^½ = 6.5×10⁻¹⁶. Overall KC = K₂ × K₁' = 1.4×6.5×10⁻¹⁶ = 9.1×10⁻¹⁶.
