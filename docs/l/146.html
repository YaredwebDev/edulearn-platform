4.13 Bioinformatics (Introduction)

◌ Not started
  • Bioinformatics = a modern hybrid field linking biology, computer science and information technology to support the storage, organisation, generation, retrieval, interpretation and analysis of biological data using software tools.
  • Uses: improve disease diagnosis/detection, promote vaccine development by screening pathogen genomes, and increase understanding of evolution via sequence-mutation analysis.
  • Three main areas: 1. Genomics — DNA sequence data. 2. Proteomics — function, shape, interactions, abundance of proteins. 3. Systems biology — how protein & DNA interactions affect whole-cell/tissue/organ function (the most recent & complex branch).
