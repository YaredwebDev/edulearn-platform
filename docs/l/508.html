UNIT 7 · LESSON 5 OF 5 · GRADE 9 ENGLISH

📝 Writing focus: Descriptive / Expository Essay on Basic Needs

◌ Not started

Write a descriptive essay about basic needs (food, water, shelter, clothing, healthcare, education). Structure: 1. Introduction — define/state the topic and your main idea. 2. Body — describe each basic need and its importance. 3. Conclusion — summarise why meeting basic needs matters.

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) retell the community-service story (where, who, how the volunteers felt), (2) define all 5+ vocabulary words, (3) use 6+ phrasal verbs (drop/dry/eat), (4) choose who/whom/whose/which/that/where/when correctly, (5) tell defining vs non-defining clauses apart by commas, (6) write a descriptive/expository essay on basic needs?
