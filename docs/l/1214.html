7.7 Applications of Similarity

Similar triangles measure heights and distances of tall objects without climbing.

Example 1: Almaz is 1.6 m tall, shadow 2 m; brother's shadow 1.5 m. By proportionality: x/1.6 = 1.5/2 → x = 1.2 m.

Example 2: Building 35 m above water; observer 10 m from edge, eye level 1.8 m above top; align with boat. 35/1.8 = x/10 → x ≈ 194.4 m.

Example 3 (buildings): Given DC = 1, BC = 3, CF = 4, AB = 2; extend FA and CB to meet at G; use △DGE ~ △CGF and △AGB ~ △FGC → find the height x = 14/3.

Example 4 (river width): Person A moves 15 m, B moves 60 m; A moves 5 m from river. Using similar triangles: 15/60 = 5/FC → FC = 20 m (river width).

✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: For shadow/height problems, set up ratios of corresponding sides of similar triangles.
