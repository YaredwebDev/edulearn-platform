5.11 Causes of infertility in humans

◌ Not started

Infertility = inability to reproduce naturally. Female infertility = unable to conceive after unprotected sex. Male infertility = low sperm production, abnormal sperm function, or blockages preventing delivery of healthy sperm.

Female causes: ovulatory problems (sparse/absent periods), thyroid problems (over/under-active prevent ovulation), pelvic surgery complications (fallopian-tube damage/scarring, cut ovaries), cervical-mucus problems (hard mucus blocks sperm), fibroids (non-cancerous growths), endometriosis (growths blocking ovaries), pelvic inflammatory disease, sterilization, and medicine/drug side effects (e.g., marijuana, cocaine, cannabis, tobacco; blood-pressure meds, anti-depressants, anti-psychotics; alcohol misuse).

Male causes: low/abnormal/dysfunctional sperm, blockages, chronic illness, injuries, inherited disorders, hormonal imbalance, varicocele (dilated veins around the testicle), hypogonadism, urogenital infection, undescended testicles. - Symptoms of male infertility: immotile sperm, testicular discomfort/swelling/lump, groin/testicle surgery, low sperm count (fewer than 15 million/mL or <39 million per ejaculate). - Risk factors: smoking, alcohol, drugs, overweight, toxin exposure, past/present STDs, overheating/trauma to the testicles.
