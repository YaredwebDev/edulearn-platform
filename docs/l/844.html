📚 Unit 1 Summary (Key Facts)

  • The three major branches of biology are Zoology, Botany, and Microbiology.
  • Sub-fields include anatomy, physiology, bacteriology, mycology, genetics, cytology, histology, embryology, taxonomy, and evolution.
  • Pure biology studies how life functions; applied biology uses that knowledge to manage life.
  • Ground-breaking discoveries: the microscope (Leeuwenhoek), laws of inheritance (Mendel), evolution (Darwin), DNA structure (Watson & Crick).
  • Biological discoveries that changed civilization: microscopes, medications, laws of heredity, germ theory, blood circulation.
  • Ethiopian contributions: Aklilu Lemma (Endod against bilharzia) and Gebissa Ejeta (sorghum varieties).
