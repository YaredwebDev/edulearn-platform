UNIT 3 · LESSON 7 OF 7 · GRADE 11 CHEMISTRY

🧮 Key Equations to Memorise

◌ Not started
  • Pressure = Force/Area; 1 atm = 760 mmHg = 76 cmHg = 760 torr = 101.325 kPa = 101,325 Pa
  • K = °C + 273; °C = (°F − 32)×5/9
  • 1 L = 1000 mL = 1000 cm³ = 1 dm³; 1 mol gas = 22.4 L at STP
  • Boyle: P₁V₁ = P₂V₂ (constant T)
  • Charles: V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂ (constant P, T in K)
  • Gay-Lussac: P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂ (constant V)
  • Combined: P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂
  • Avogadro: V₁/n₁ = V₂/n₂
  • Ideal gas: PV = nRT, R = 0.082 atm·L·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹; n = m/M
  • Graham: r₁/r₂ = √(M₂/M₁); time ∝ √M
  • KE = ½mv²
  • ΔH_vap = −ΔH_cond; ΔH_cryst = −ΔH_fus; ΔH_sub = ΔH_fus + ΔH_vap = −ΔH_dep

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) compare solids/liquids/gases/plasma and explain by kinetic theory; (2) convert between P, V, T units and use STP/22.4 L; (3) state and apply each gas law (Boyle, Charles, Gay-Lussac, combined, Avogadro, ideal gas, Graham's) to numerical problems; (4) explain evaporation vs boiling, vapour pressure, volatile vs non-volatile, and why boiling point falls with altitude; (5) use ΔH_vap, ΔH_cond, ΔH_fus, ΔH_cryst, ΔH_sub; (6) interpret a heating/cooling curve and complete a phase-change energy table?
