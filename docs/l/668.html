1.2 Branches of Physics

◌ Not started
Branch Description
Mechanics Deals with the motion of objects with or without reference to force. Subdivided into quantum mechanics (behavior of smallest particles like neutrons, protons, electrons) and classical mechanics (laws of motion of forces and physical objects).
Acoustics Study of sound and its transmission, production, and effects.
Optics Behavior, propagation, and properties of light.
Thermodynamics Studies thermal energy and the transfer of heat.
Electromagnetism Study of electromagnetic force (electric fields, light, magnetic fields). Two aspects: "electricity" and "magnetism".
Nuclear physics Structure, properties, and reactions of the nuclei of atoms.
Astrophysics Uses the methods and principles of physics to study astronomical objects and phenomena.

✍️ Must-know: The branches of physics include Mechanics, Acoustics, Optics, Thermodynamics, Electromagnetism, Nuclear Physics, Astrophysics.
