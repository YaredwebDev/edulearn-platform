9.1 Introduction to Dairy Cattle Production & Management

◌ Not started
  • Dairy-type cattle are raised for milk production — excellent at converting feed into high-quality milk.
  • Dual-purpose cattle = raised for both meat and milk. Multi-purpose cattle = dual-purpose plus working traits (strong bones, sound constitution, quiet temperament).
  • Dairying / dairy cattle production = a branch of agriculture for raising dairy animals to produce milk & milk products. Milk is the primary product.
  • Before producing milk a cow must first have a calf. Terms & timings:
  • Heifer = female calf that has reached sexual maturity (modern breeds at 7–8 months) before first calf / with only one calf.
  • Age at first service = usually 15–18 months when heifers are mated.
  • Gestation period ≈ 265–300 days. First calving ≈ 2–2.5 years old. Bred again 4–8 weeks after calving.
  • Good dairy practice = safe, high-quality milk from healthy animals via breeding, feeding, housing, health care and record keeping.
