5.5 Magnetic Force on a Moving Charge

◌ Not started

A moving charged particle in a magnetic field feels a magnetic force; a stationary charge is unaffected.

Magnitude: F = qvB sin θ - q = charge, v = velocity, B = magnetic field, θ = angle between v and B. - Force is zero when v and B are parallel (θ = 0° or 180°). - Force is maximum when v and B are perpendicular (θ = 90°). - Direction of F is perpendicular to the plane formed by v and B (right-hand rule).

Right-hand rule (positive charge): thumb in direction of v, fingers in direction of B; the perpendicular to the palm gives the direction of F.

Example 5.2: q = 50 C, v = 3 m/s, B = 1 T, θ = 0° → F = qvB sin 0° = 0.

✍️ Must-know: F = qvB sin θ. Max when v⊥B; zero when v ∥ B.
