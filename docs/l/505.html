7.2 Vocabulary of the Unit (matric-ready)

◌ Not started

Key vocabulary from the "Community Service" passage:

Word Meaning Example
dedicated committed; giving all one's energy The volunteers were dedicated to their work.
devoted very loyal and caring She is devoted to helping others.
complete whole; total; to finish The task took a complete day.
fortunate lucky We felt fortunate to help.
bettered improved The experience bettered our lives.

✍️ Must-know: dedicated and devoted are near-synonyms (loyal/committed). bettered is the past form of the verb better = "to improve."
