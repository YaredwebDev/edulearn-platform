1B.1 Panel discussion / debating on environmental hazards

  • Stakeholders: the public, media, environmentalists, government. Role-play giving/asking opinions about preserving a healthy environment.
  • Be able to pick out which expressions ask for opinion, give advice, second (agree with) someone's idea, and oppose/reject an opinion.

1B.2 Asking for and giving opinions

  • Useful in everyday and formal speech/writing.
  • Expressions for giving opinions: I think / I don't think · I believe · I'm fairly/quite/absolutely certain that · I feel certain that · I strongly believe that · I'm convinced that · I don't doubt that · It seems to me that · As I see it · My impression is that · It strikes me that · I would have thought that.
  • Expressions for asking opinions: What do you think about …? What is your view about …? Do you agree with the idea that …?
  • Opinions can be weak / mild / strong — e.g. I think it's a good movie (weak) vs I'm convinced it's the best decision (strong).

1B.4 Pronunciation: Pure vowels (monophthongs)

Monophthongs are produced by holding the tongue in a certain position & height. Common pure-vowel sounds (with example words): - /iː/ need, keep, deep, sheep, eat, see, meet, read, beat, peak - /ɪ/ think, link, big, six, live, since, sit, pick - /ʊ/ put, would, look, good, took - /uː/ to, you, new, who - /e/ get, when, well, very, bed, pen, ten - /ə/ the, about, could, us - /ɜː/ her, work, learn, word, turn, firm, bird, shirt - /ɔː/ more, call, all, hall, fall, wall, warm - /æ/ have, can, sad, bad, land, sand, mat - /ʌ/ but, cut, one, much

✋ Pronunciation trap: pairs like /iː/ vs /ɪ/ (ship vs sheep), /uː/ vs /ʊ/ (fool vs full) differ in the length/energy of articulation and change meaning. Practice the pairs: sin/seen, ship/sheep, lives/leaves, rich/reach, fill/feel, sick/seek, full/fool, sit/seat, hill/heel, pull/pool.
