6.6 Natural Resources

◌ Not started

Natural resources = any natural substances/materials available naturally in the environment used by human beings (oil, coal, natural gas, metals, stone, air, sunlight, soil, water, animals, birds, fish, plants). Used to make food, fuel, clothes, and many products. Classified as: - Renewable — can be replaced after utilization / not exhausted even with continuous use: sunlight, air, trees, water, wind, tidal/solar/wind energy, biomass energy, hydropower. Note: renewable resources do NOT have a rapid recovery rate and can be depleted if overused or poorly managed. - Non-renewable — do not naturally replenish at the speed they're used; may take millions of years to form; rate of consumption exceeds rate of recovery. Examples: minerals, fossil fuels, coal, natural gas, uranium.

✍️ Must-know: renewable = solar, water, wind, biomass, hydropower; non-renewable = coal, natural gas, minerals, uranium.
