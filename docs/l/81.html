13.2 Climate Change and Its Effects

◌ Not started
  • Effects: changes in rainfall amount & patterns; increased global temperature; melting ice caps & glaciers; rising sea level; droughts increasing evaporation.
  • Effects on agriculture:
  • In rain-fed agriculture, decreased rainfall reduces water availability; higher temperature increases water loss from soils & surface water → water shortage for crops, animals, humans.
  • In some areas rainfall increases → more soil erosion; nutrients washed away → reduced yield & animal productivity; damaging croplands & property.
  • Global warming increases incidence of some crop & animal diseases.
  • Climate change affects animals via reduced forage yields, heat stress, water shortage, and increased disease/parasite incidence.
  • Ethiopia is highly vulnerable: small-scale farming, very low use of improved seeds/breeds, fertiliser, pesticides, irrigation, machinery; >90% of crop production is rain-fed with very low irrigation. Expected reduced rainfall & higher temperature will further reduce yields → a strategy is needed.
  • Effects on forestry: global warming & reduced water → reduced vegetation growth, lower productivity, loss of tree species; reduced capacity to absorb carbon via photosynthesis; deforestation/erosion/wildlife loss enhance desertification; forests lose ability to regulate climate through carbon storage → loss of plant & animal biodiversity.
  • Effects on sea level: melting polar ice caps → sea level rises → coastal communities vulnerable to flooding; floods damage communities & ecosystems.
  • Ocean acidification: oceans absorb ~30% of CO₂ released to atmosphere; increasing CO₂ → oceans absorb more → dissolved CO₂ makes carbonic acid → oceans more acidic → harms marine organisms.
