1.2 Physics Communities and Their Roles

◌ Not started

A physics community = an organised group of persons associated together for scientific purposes. Such organisations are established at the national, continental, and worldwide level according to their aims. Their benefits include access to equipment, computing facilities, scholarships, the exchange of scientific information, and popularising physics.

The Ethiopian Physical Society (EPS) — its objectives (as listed in the textbook): 1. Promote physics education and research in the country. 2. Organise & coordinate conferences on physics education and exchange scientific information. 3. Popularise physics to make students develop interest in physics. 4. Promote the active participation of Ethiopian physicists and the general public in designing and implementing the physics curriculum. 5. Create a means for disseminating scientific information, etc.

✍️ Must-know: physics communities exist at national, continental and worldwide levels; the EPS promotes physics education/research and popularises physics in Ethiopia. Communities give members equipment, computing facilities and scholarships.
