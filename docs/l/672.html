  1. Physics = Greek phusis = nature; describes the basic mechanisms of the universe.
  2. Branches: Mechanics, Acoustics, Optics, Thermodynamics, Electromagnetism, Nuclear Physics, Astrophysics.
  3. Mechanics = motion; Acoustics = sound; Optics = light; Thermodynamics = heat.
  4. Faraday invented the first electric generator.
  5. Joule → law of conservation of energy.
  6. Curie → radioactivity; discovered polonium & radium.
  7. Einstein → theory of relativity.
  8. Newton → three laws of motion + universal gravitation.
  9. Physics = foundation of chemistry, engineering, geology, biophysics, geophysics, medical physics.
