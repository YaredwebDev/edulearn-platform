1.3 Phrasal Verbs: "fall"

◌ Not started

Phrasal verbs = verb + particle (preposition/adverb) whose meaning often differs from the verb alone.

Phrasal verb Meaning Example
fall apart break into pieces; fail His plan fell apart at the last minute.
fall back move back / retreat The troops had to fall back.
fall back on rely on (as a last resource) I can fall back on my savings.
fall behind fail to keep up He fell behind in his studies.
fall down drop to the ground The old building fell down.
fall for be deceived by; fall in love with She fell for his trick.
fall off decrease; drop off Sales fell off in winter.
fall out quarrel; lose hair/teeth They fell out over money.
fall through fail to happen The deal fell through.

✍️ Must-know: Distinguish the two senses: fall out = (1) quarrel, (2) hair/teeth come out. fall through = a plan/agreement fails. fall back on needs an object (the resource).
