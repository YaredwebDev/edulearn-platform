7.1 Significance of Animal Housing

◌ Not started

Proper housing is essential for profitable animal production — good feeding & breeding won't give maximum production if animals aren't kept well. Housing provides:

  1. Environmental controls: housing lets the farmer control the inside environment (temperature, humidity, air movement, sun radiation, rain). Animals respond to environmental stress by lowering productivity/reproduction. E.g. at high temperature feed intake drops to regulate body temperature → less milk/meat/egg/wool; reproduction also suffers. Good housing protects animals to achieve desired production all year.
  2. Control of diseases & external parasites: a well-ventilated, dry house prevents pathogen buildup; facilitates strict sanitation; easy to clean/disinfect → minimises mortality and eliminates external parasites like ticks, mites, lice.
  3. Efficient use of feed & labour: careful building/equipment arrangement improves feeding efficiency, reduces feed wastage & labour cost; efficient communication between feed stores and feeding troughs; eases watering, breeding, egg collection, milking, cleaning; protects labourers from environmental stress (economic benefit).
  4. Safeguard against predators: all farm animals are prey animals, so housing protects them.

✍️ Must-know (purposes): environmental control; disease & external-parasite control; efficient feed/labour use; predator protection. (The review's answer: "all of the above".)
