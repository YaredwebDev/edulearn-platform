6.3 Word Formation — Suffixes

◌ Not started

Suffixes are added to the end of a word to change its part of speech or meaning.

Suffix Use Examples
-ate makes verbs (also adjectives/nouns) activate, separate, participate
-ise / -ize makes verbs ("to make/become") modernise, recognise, realise
-ment makes nouns (from verbs) development, improvement, movement
-tion makes nouns (from verbs) education, protection, reduction

✍️ Must-know: Adding -tion/-ment turns a verb into a noun (educate → education; develop → development). Adding -ise/-ize/-ate turns a noun/adjective into a verb (modern → modernise; active → activate).
