6.17 Resource depletion

◌ Not started
  • Resource depletion = when consumption of natural resources becomes faster than they can recover → they become scarce.
  • Major causes: (1) overpopulation; (2) poor farming practices (poor irrigation/soil management, heavy machinery, excessive pesticides/fungicides/herbicides killing soil microorganisms); (3) overconsumption (industrial-scale mineral & oil exploration); (4) industrial & technological development (toxins/chemical by-products + rising demand).
