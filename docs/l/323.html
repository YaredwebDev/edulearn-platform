  1. 7 SI base units: m, kg, s, A, K, mol, cd.
  2. K = °C + 273; °C = 5/9(°F − 32).
  3. d = m/V; water = 1.000 g/cm³ at 4 °C.
  4. 1 L = 1 dm³; 1 mL = 1 cm³.
  5. Sig figs: add/subtract → fewest decimal places; multiply/divide → fewest sig figs.
  6. Scientific notation: N × 10ⁿ, 1 ≤ N < 10.
  7. Precision = closeness of repeated results; accuracy = closeness to true value.
  8. Always add acid to water, never water to acid.
  9. Uncertainty methods: ±half-range, ±furthest-from-mean, ±instrument resolution.
  10. Scientific method: observation → hypothesis → experiment → conclusion.
