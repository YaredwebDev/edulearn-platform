5C Reading Skills: Opportunities for Innovation in Irrigation

◌ Not started
  • Irrigation's value: boosts agricultural productivity AND improves resilience & food security for farming families (esp. under climate change); empowers women & opens employment; small-scale African farmers show a surge of interest; a growing population needs reliable food → irrigation should be a top policy priority and an engine of agricultural transformation.
  • Traditional vs modern: many irrigated areas rely on basic/improvised techniques (flood recession, spate irrigation, wetlands) that are less productive/reliable than modern pressurized technologies (sprinklers & micro-irrigation), which reduce water use and improve horticultural yields/quality. ~18% of areas equipped with modern irrigation in Africa use pressurized equipment (vs 2% in Asia, 12% elsewhere).
  • Selection factors (site-specific): crop prices & market factors, energy costs, labour supply; technology costs, crop & farming characteristics, local climate, and regulatory provisions (groundwater pumping restrictions, drainage/discharge limits, water-transfer provisions).
  • Rainwater harvesting (collecting/storing/using rainwater for landscape irrigation & water-spreading dams) is widespread, at farmer or community level. Unlike big dams, small-scale harvesting loses less to evaporation (collected locally from roofs/ground catchment) and doesn't deplete aquifers or others' supply. Storage type depends on material, size, rainfall, demand, location, cost.
  • Water-spreading dams harvest floodwater into valleys so water infiltrates to the aquifer; can use local materials but need careful planning. In Niger, >4,700 farms benefited, raising arable valley land from 0.6 ha to 2.2 ha average, with +85% millet yields and +25–30% sorghum yields.

✍️ Reading skill: identify central message (importance of modernising irrigation), examples of modern vs traditional systems, advantages of modern technologies, and pronoun references (this/doing this/all of which/it/they); complete main-idea/supporting-detail tables; skim & scan.
