📚 Unit 4 Summary (Key Facts)

  • Cell cycle = interphase (G₁, S, G₂) + cell division (mitosis/meiosis + cytokinesis).
  • Interphase: growth and DNA synthesis (S phase).
  • Mitosis: somatic cells, 1 division, 2 identical diploid daughter cells; stages PMAT.
  • Meiosis: reproductive cells, 2 divisions, 4 haploid daughter cells; homologous chromosomes separate in Meiosis I, sister chromatids separate in Meiosis II.
  • Cell division is growth/replacement in multicellular organisms and reproduction in unicellular organisms.
  • Cancer results from failure to control the cell cycle.
