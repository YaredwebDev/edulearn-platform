⚡ Quick Revision Box (Unit 4)

  1. F = ma; 1 N = 1 kg·m/s².
  2. W = mg (g = 9.8 m/s² on Earth; 1/6 on Moon).
  3. 1st law = inertia; mass is a measure of inertia.
  4. 3rd law: F₁₂ = −F₂₁ (equal & opposite, act on different objects).
  5. Static friction (before sliding) > kinetic friction (during sliding).
  6. Work W = F∥·S (force in direction of displacement); Joule.
  7. Work = 0 if no displacement, no force, or force ⊥ displacement.
  8. E_k = ½mv² (motion); E_p = mgh (height).
  9. Mechanical energy = E_k + E_p.
  10. Power P = W/t = E/t; 1 W = 1 J/s.
  11. 1 J = 1 N·m = 1 kg·m²/s².
