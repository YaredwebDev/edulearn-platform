6.3 Frustum of Pyramids and Cones

Frustum of a pyramid (Def 6.6): the part of a pyramid between the base and a plane parallel to the base (the original base and the cross-section are the two bases; other faces are lateral faces, which are trapeziums).

Frustum of a cone (Def 6.7): the part of a cone between the base and a parallel plane.

Frustum of a cone

Frustum of a regular pyramid (lower perimeter p, upper p′, slant height l):

  • LSA = ½·l·(p + p′)

Example: square frustum, lower side 5, upper side 3, slant height 4 → p=20, p′=12 → LSA=½·4·(20+12)=64; TSA=64+5²+3²=98 cm².

Frustum of a right circular cone (radii r (bigger) and r′ (smaller), slant height l):

  • LSA = πl(r + r′)
  • TSA = πl(r+r′) + π(r²+r′²)

Volume of a frustum (cone or pyramid):

V = ⅓h(A + A′ + √(AA′)) (A, A′ base areas, h altitude)

For a cone: V = ⅓πh(r² + r′² + rr′).

Example: cone frustum height 24, base radius 22, slant height 26. l²=h²+(r−r′)² → 26²=24²+(22−r′)² → (22−r′)²=100 → r′=12. LSA=26π(22+12)=884π; TSA=884π+π(22²+12²)=884π+628π=1512π.

Example (volume): top & bottom diameters 6 & 10 (r′=3, r=5), h=12 → V=⅓π·12·(9+25+15)=196π≈615.44 m³.

✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Frustum LSA (cone)=πl(r+r′); volume=⅓πh(r²+r′²+rr′) or ⅓h(A+A′+√(AA′)). Find missing radius via l²=h²+(r−r′)².
