3B.1 Conversation: traffic-police reporting

  • Practise reporting an accident: showing licence/registration, describing what happened (running a red light, wrong side, speeding, damage = "a write-off"), and knowing speed limits (e.g. school crossing = 30 km/h).
  • Asking for & giving opinions (revisit from Unit 1): give your opinion (I believe…, I think/I don't think…, I'm convinced that…, I strongly believe…, From my point of view…) and ask for it (What do you think of…? How do you feel about…? What is your opinion on…? What are your thoughts on…?).

3B.2 Participation in discussions

  • Discuss causes, effects & solutions of road accidents in your town; assign a leader, take notes, report (5–7 minutes).

3B.3 Giving a public speech (7–10 min)

  • Choose a topic (e.g. importance of pedestrian codes, wearing helmets/gloves, serious measures on traffic-code violators).
  • How to prepare: know the purpose; know your audience; research/gather data; organise coherently (clear introduction–development–conclusion); be conscious of language; use body language; adjust volume. Rehearse; start confidently and close with strong sincere words & appreciation.
