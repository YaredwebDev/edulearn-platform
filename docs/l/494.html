5.2 Vocabulary of the Unit (matric-ready)

◌ Not started

Key vocabulary from the "Health Benefits of Gardening" passage:

Word Meaning Example
relieve to reduce/remove (pain, stress) Gardening relieves daily stress.
unwind to relax After work she goes to the garden to unwind.
contribute to give/help towards Fresh vegetables contribute to good health.
consume to eat/drink; to use up We should consume more fruit.
enhancing improving/increasing Eating well is enhancing my energy.
instantaneous immediate; happening in an instant The relief was almost instantaneous.

Word formation — the prefix ex-: - ex- can mean "former" (ex-president, ex-teacher) or "out of" (extract, exit, export). - Examples: ex-husband, export, extract, exchange.

✍️ Must-know: "instantaneous" is the adjective for instant. Know that ex- = former / out.
