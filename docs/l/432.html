4B.1 Pronunciation: Triphthongs

  • Triphthongs = three vowel sounds coming together (a diphthong + a schwa), often "with three sounds/tones". They can be separated into different syllables.
  • Examples: lawyer /aʊə/-type, layer, admire, power, lower, fire, hire, tower, player, royal, hour, slower, mayor, soya, mower. (The added /ə/ is only spoken; words with triphthongs usually have a written consonant in the cluster, e.g. "w" in power.)

4B.2 Making a public speech (3–5 min, e.g. forest conservation)

  • Prepare: think about the audience (educated/uneducated/mixed), the way you present (notes, projector), and planning/ordering your presentation to attract the audience. Research, prepare notes, compare/develop, revise & recite, then present.

4B.3 Asking for & giving directions

  • Asking: How can I get to …? Where is …? How far is …? Is there a … around here? Could you tell me how to get to …? Pardon me, I'm lost — how do I get to …? Which is the best way to …?
  • Giving — "go straight": go straight along this road, down there, go down/walk down the street, continue straight ahead ~a mile, continue past the petrol station, keep going for another block, take this road, walk to the corner, follow this street ~300 m, follow the road until you get to the bank.
  • Giving — "turn": turn right/left past the bank, take the first right, it's round the corner from the post office, cross/pass the school.
  • Locations: it is straight ahead / near / next to / behind my office.
  • If you don't know: I'm sorry, I don't know; I'm a stranger here myself.

4B.4 Expressing complaint (and accepting it)

  • Complaining: Sorry to bother you, but…; I'm sorry to say this but…; I'm afraid I've got a complaint about…; I'm afraid there is a slight problem with…; Excuse me, but there is a problem about… (State the problem and ask how it can be fixed/amended.)

4B.5 Saying "no" tactfully

  • Polite refusal: I'm not keen on…; I don't particularly like…; I'd really rather not…; If it's OK with you, I'd prefer not to…
