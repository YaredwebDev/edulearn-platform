1.3 Phrasal Verbs: act, add, allow, back

Phrasal verbs = verb + particle (preposition/adverb) whose meaning often differs from the verb alone.

Phrasal verbMeaningExample
act outto perform/express (feelings, a story)The pupils acted out the scene.
act onto take action because of (advice/information)She acted on the teacher's advice.
act upto behave badly; to malfunctionThe engine acted up on the highway.
add onto attach / include as an extraThey added on a new room.
add up toto result in; to meanThe figures add up to a big total.
aim atto be directed at; to intend forThe campaign aims at young people.
allow forto take into account; to considerAllow for traffic when you plan the trip.
allow ofto permit (something)The rules do not allow of any delay.
back awayto move backward (in fear/surprise)He backed away from the angry dog.
back downto admit defeat; to withdraw a demandHe refused to back down in the argument.
back upto support; to move in reverse; to make a copyPlease back up my decision.
Note: ✋ Trap: back down = give in (lose a fight/argument); back up can mean support OR reverse a vehicle OR make a data copy — use context to pick the meaning.
