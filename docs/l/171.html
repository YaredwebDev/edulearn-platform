  1. Biology = Greek bios (life) + logos (study) = scientific study of living things.
  2. Hypothesis = a proposed, testable explanation.
  3. Scientific method order: Observation → Question → Hypothesis → Experiment → Conclusion → Communication.
  4. Magnification = how many times larger; Resolution = ability to distinguish two points.
  5. Light microscope uses a beam of light; electron microscope uses a beam of electrons (higher magnification).
  6. Total magnification = eyepiece × objective (e.g., 10× × 40× = 400×).
  7. Autoclave sterilizes by high temperature + pressurized steam (kills spores).
  8. Compound light microscope magnifies up to 2000×.
  9. Always focus on low power first; use immersion oil only on 100×.
  10. Safety: never eat/drink/taste chemicals in the lab; report all accidents; know where the fire extinguisher and first-aid kit are.
