4.3 Phrasal Verbs: burn, call, calm, care, carry

Phrasal verbMeaningExample
burn upto be destroyed by fire; to consume (energy)The dry grass burned up quickly.
call backto return a phone callI'll call back in an hour.
call forto require/need; to requestThis task calls for patience.
call upto telephone; to summonShe called up her friend.
call offto cancelThey called off the meeting.
calm downto become/make less excited or angryHe took deep breaths to calm down.
care forto look after; to likeThe rangers care for the animals.
carry outto perform/complete (a task)Scientists carried out a survey.
Note: ✋ Trap: call off = cancel; call for = require; call up = telephone/recruit; carry out = perform. Pick the meaning from the object/context.
