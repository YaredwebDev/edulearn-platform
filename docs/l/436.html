4F.1 Punctuation: ellipsis, parentheses, quotation marks

  • Ellipsis (…) — three dots. Used to show omission of words in a quote (shortening it without changing meaning), or a break/trailing-off of thought or pause (After school I went to her house … and then came home; She opened the door … and saw … a cake!).
  • Parentheses ( ) — enclose additional, separate or less important words/numbers: He has an exercise book and pens (2); They are at the lakeside (Abebe and Alemu).
  • Quotation marks (" ") — enclose a direct quotation (word-for-word): "I am busy," he said. Practise adding them to direct speech: He said, "The case is far from over, and we will win."

4F.2 Descriptive writing (guided paragraph + free/expository writing)

  • Descriptive writing describes people, places, objects or events using descriptive language (adjectives, adverbs) that creates an image in the reader's mind. Steps: brainstorm → outline → sort words → write sentences → use transition words. (Model: The lake side is the perfect place for me to study… — note the descriptive and connecting words.)
  • Expository writing states facts or information about a topic (write an expository paragraph on a natural resource in your area and how it is affected).

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) summarise the listening text on factors/vicious-circle degradation of Ethiopian resources; (2) pronounce triphthongs and give a short speech, ask for/give directions, complain, and refuse tactfully; (3) explain the FAO indigenous-forest-conservation findings (carbon-loss rates, six pillars) and answer comprehension/reference questions; (4) form opposites and use prefixes/suffixes (with the spelling rules) and "look/lock" phrasal verbs; (5) identify the eight parts of speech, form the future-perfect tense, and use adverbial clauses of purpose with so that/in order that/lest; and (6) punctuate with ellipsis/parentheses/quotation marks and write descriptive & expository paragraphs? Try the prefix/suffix exercises and the "Losing your memory" error-correction passage.
