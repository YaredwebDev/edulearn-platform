6.1 Reading: The Impact of Poverty

(the core "story"/text of the unit)

The passage analyses the three main consequences of poverty:

  • Entrenched (deep, lasting) inequality.

  • Poor physical and mental health.

  • Crime (poverty drives crime).

It also discusses modern slavery, whose forms include:

  • Chattel slavery (owning a person as property).

  • Child slavery.

  • Debt bondage (working to repay a debt).

  • Human trafficking.

✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Questions test (a) the three consequences of poverty, (b) the forms of modern slavery, and (c) vocabulary in context.
