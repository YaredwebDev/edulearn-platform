4.8 The Menstrual Cycle

◌ Not started
  • The approximately monthly changes in the uterus, coordinated with the ovarian cycle. The first day is marked by the onset of menstruation (flow of bits of uterine lining and blood out through the vagina).

Three phases: 1. Flow phase (menstruation): the first phase — shedding of the uterine lining. 2. Follicular phase: the second phase — FSH stimulates maturation of an ovarian follicle; follicle cells secrete estrogens that stimulate the endometrium to thicken; rising estrogens encourage the pituitary to release more LH. 3. Luteal phase: the third phase (after ovulation) — LH stimulates formation of the corpus luteum, which secretes some estrogens and a lot of progesterone; these thicken the uterine lining and grow blood vessels, preparing the uterus for pregnancy.

  • Menopause: when all follicles have been released or disintegrated; estrogen and progesterone production diminishes and menstrual cycles cease (known only in humans and two species of whales).

Menstrual hygiene & health benefits: - Wear lightweight, breathable clothing (cotton underwear). - Change menstrual products regularly. - Keep the genital area clean; wipe front to back; use only water to rinse the vulva. - Use unscented products. - Drink enough liquids. - Track/monitor your period. - Annual check-up (pap smear, pelvic exam, breast exam).

✍️ Must-know: Menstrual cycle phases: flow → follicular → luteal. FSH → follicle maturation; LH → ovulation + corpus luteum; progesterone → thickens uterine lining. Menopause = end of menstrual cycles.
