UNIT 4 · LESSON 15 OF 15 · GRADE 11 BIOLOGY

🧮 Key facts/figures to memorise

◌ Not started
  • DNA: A=T, C≡G (H-bonds); purines A,G; pyrimidines C,T (RNA uses U instead of T); sugar deoxyribose; double helix, antiparallel.
  • Human: 46 chromosomes = 23 pairs (22 autosomes + 1 sex pair); XX female, XY male.
  • DNA replication is semiconservative; new strand grows 5′→3′; helicase→polymerase→ligase; topoisomerase prevents supercoiling.
  • Genetic code: 4³ = 64 codons for 20 amino acids; start AUG; stops UAA, UAG, UGA.
  • Monohybrid F2 ratio: genotypic 1:2:1, phenotypic 3:1. Dihybrid F2: 9:3:3:1. AaBb selfed: P(AABB) = 1/16.
  • Crossing over happens in Prophase I; independent assortment happens at Metaphase I.
  • ABO has 3 alleles (6 genotypes); A & B co-dominant, O recessive. Rh⁺ dominant, Rh⁻ recessive.
  • Ethiopian livestock (CSA 2020): 65 M cattle, 40 M sheep, 51 M goats, 8 M camels, 49 M chickens.

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) compare DNA vs RNA; (2) describe DNA replication with enzymes; (3) order the cell-cycle phases and mitosis/meiosis stages and distinguish them; (4) explain transcription vs translation incl. codons/anticodons and central dogma; (5) do monohybrid/dihybrid crosses and give the ratios; (6) state both Mendel's laws and their physical bases; (7) explain when/how you'd use a test cross vs back cross; (8) describe sex determination and why males get more X-linked disorders; (9) distinguish co-dominance, incomplete dominance, multiple alleles and solve ABO/Rh inheritance; (10) define phenocopy; (11) read/interpret a pedigree; (12) classify genetic disorders and give examples; (13) define genetic testing, counseling, and the two types of gene therapy; and (14) explain breeding methods (inbreeding vs crossbreeding, plant-breeding methods) and Ethiopian indigenous breeding/bioinformatics? Review-answer also the practice items (crossing over in prophase I; BB×bb → 100% black; probability next 3 of 3 children all boys = 1/8; complement of 5′‑CGA TTG CTA‑3′ is 3′‑GCT AAC GAT‑5′; mRNA A-A-U ↔ tRNA anticodon U-U-A; AaBb selfed AABB = 1/16; X-linked traits commoner in males; Rh⁻ woman + Rh⁺ man marriage is risky; far-apart genes cross over more).
