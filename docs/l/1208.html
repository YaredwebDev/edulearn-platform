7.1 Revision on Congruency of Triangles

Definition: Two triangles are congruent if they have exactly the same three sides and the same three angles — identical in size and shape.

Notation: △ABC ≡ △DEF means AB ≡ DE, BC ≡ EF, AC ≡ DF and ∠A≡∠D, ∠B≡∠E, ∠C≡∠F.

Congruent triangles

Congruence postulates/criteria:

  1. SSS — three pairs of equal sides.
  2. SAS — two pairs of sides equal and the included angle equal.
  3. ASA — two angles and the included side equal.
  4. RHS — for right triangles: equal hypotenuse and one leg.

Example: If △ABC ≡ △DEF, ∠A = 45°, ∠C = 85°, then ∠B = 50°; so ∠D = 45°, ∠E = 50°, ∠F = 85°.

✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Congruent = same size and shape. Criteria: SSS, SAS, ASA, RHS. "Included" means between the two sides/angles.
