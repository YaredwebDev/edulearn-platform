8.1 Vector and Scalar Quantities

Definition: Scalars are quantities fully described by a magnitude (numerical value) alone. E.g., mass (10 kg), density (1 g/cm³), temperature (27 °C), distance, speed, area.

Definition: Vectors are quantities fully described by both a magnitude and a direction. E.g., force, velocity, acceleration, displacement, weight.

Examples:

  • Distance (scalar) vs displacement (vector — distance with direction).
  • Force of 10 N applied upward vs horizontally produce different effects → force is a vector.
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Scalars: magnitude only. Vectors: magnitude + direction. Displacement is a "directed distance."
