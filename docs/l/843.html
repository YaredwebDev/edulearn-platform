1.5 Ethiopian Biologists and Their Contributions

1. Prof. Yalemtsehay Mekonnen

  • Biologist and academic at Addis Ababa University; Ph.D. in human physiology (University of Heidelberg, Germany).
  • Researched the impact of chemical pesticide hazards on humans (Upper Awash farms, Rift Valley).
  • Researched medicinal plants, especially Moringa stenopetala (Shiferaw/Alekko Shekatta).
  • Published over 100 scientific papers.

2. Dr. Aklilu Lemma

  • Studied schistosomiasis (bilharzia), caused by parasitic flatworms (spend part of life in freshwater snails).
  • Discovered the soapberry "Endod" (Phytolacca dodecandra) kills the carrier snails.
  • Endod is cheap, locally known, and environment-friendly (breaks down naturally in ~2 days).
  • Trials: before treatment 50% of children 1–6 infected; after treatment only 7%.

3. Prof. Gebissa Ejeta

  • Specialist in plant breeding and genetics; Ph.D. from Purdue University (USA).
  • Developed Africa's first commercial hybrid sorghum (drought-resistant, higher yield).
  • Developed sorghum varieties resistant to the parasitic Striga weed; yields up to 10× more.
  • Awarded the World Food Prize (2009) and Ethiopia's National Hero award.
Note: 📌 Other Ethiopian biologists to research (Activity 1.11): Prof. Beyene Petros, Dr. Brihane Asfaw, Prof. Ensermu Kelbessa, Prof. Legesse Negash, Dr. Melaku Worede, Prof. Mogessie Ashenafi, Dr. Tewolde-Berhan Gebre Egziabher, Prof. Tilahun Yilma, Dr. Tsehaynesh Asfaw, Dr. Zeresenay Alemseged.
