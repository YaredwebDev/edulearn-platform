4.2 Newton's Laws of Motion

◌ Not started

Newton's First Law of Motion (Law of Inertia)

A body continues in its state of rest or of uniform motion in a straight line unless acted on by an unbalanced force.

  • Inertia: the property of matter by which it continues in its existing state of rest or uniform motion, unless changed by an external force. It is the tendency of an object to resist any attempt to change its velocity.
  • Mass is a measure of inertia. The greater the mass, the greater the inertia (more resistance to change of motion).
  • Inertial frame: a reference frame in which Newton's first law is valid. Any frame moving with constant velocity relative to an inertial frame is itself inertial.
  • Mass vs weight: Mass is an inherent property, constant everywhere. Weight = gravitational force (W = mg) and can change with location.

Newton's Second Law of Motion

The acceleration of a body is directly proportional to the net force acting on it and inversely proportional to the mass of the body.

  • F = m·a (4.1)
  • Unit of force: 1 N = 1 kg·m/s² (the force that gives a 1 kg mass an acceleration of 1 m/s²).
  • Weight: F_g = W = mg, where g = 9.8 m/s² (acceleration due to gravity on Earth's surface, directed toward Earth's center).
  • Note: g varies with location — on the Moon g is about 1/6 of Earth's; g changes as an object moves farther from Earth's surface.

Example 4.1: F = 10 N, m = 2 kg → a = F/m = 10/2 = 5 m/s² (in the direction of force). Example 4.2: F = 100 N, a = 2 m/s² → m = F/a = 50 kg. Example 4.3: m = 10 kg → W = mg = 10 × 9.8 = 98 N.

Newton's Third Law of Motion

Every action has an equal and opposite reaction. Forces always act in pairs.

  • F₁₂ = −F₂₁ (4.5): the force exerted by object 1 on object 2 equals in magnitude but is opposite in direction to the force exerted by object 2 on object 1.
  • The action and reaction forces act on different objects.
  • For a block on a table: block pushes down on table (F₁₂ = weight); table pushes up on block (F₂₁ = normal force).

✍️ Must-know: 1st law = inertia (mass measures inertia). 2nd law F = ma; 1 N = 1 kg·m/s². 3rd law: action = reaction, equal & opposite, act on different objects. Weight W = mg.
