UNIT 13 · LESSON 7 OF 7 · GRADE 11 AGRICULTURE

🧮 Key facts to memorise

◌ Not started
  • Climate change = long-term (decades) fundamental ecosystem change; climate variability = short-term, no fundamental change.
  • Main GHGs: CO₂, CH₄, N₂O. Largest human CO₂ source = fossil-fuel burning. Climate change mainly human-caused.
  • Oceans absorb ~30% of CO₂ → carbonic acid → ocean acidification.
  • Soil stores ~2× more carbon than the whole atmosphere.
  • Mitigation (reduce/remove emissions: renewable energy, reduce deforestation, afforestation, soil management) vs adaptation (farmer adjustments to actual/expected change).
  • Ethiopian adaptation: perennials (enset), income diversification, drought-tolerant varieties, mixed cropping, better breeds, improved manure/nutrition/cut-and-carry.
  • CSA three aims: sustainable productivity + reduce vulnerability/adapt + cut GHG emissions.
  • Ethiopian vulnerability: small-scale, low inputs, >90% rain-fed.
  • Indigenous: Gedeo agroforestry (sacred nature, hundreds of years); Konso stone terraces (collect/discharge water, protect soil).
  • Review answers: long-term climate change = Climate change (B); indicator of global climate change = warming temperatures & sea-level rise (C, A and B); farmer adjustments to climate change = Climate adaptation (A); a mitigation practice = All (D) (agroforestry, afforestation, enhancing soil carbon); adaptation mechanism in crop production = All (D) (change variety, planting time, mixed cropping).

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) distinguish climate variability from climate change; (2) explain consequences on crops/animals (and why Ethiopia is vulnerable); (3) give personal locality observations; (4) distinguish mitigation from adaptation with strategies/examples (renewable energy, afforestation, soil carbon vs perennials, diversification, CSA); and (5) give indigenous-knowledge examples (Gedeo, Konso) used for adaptation/mitigation?
