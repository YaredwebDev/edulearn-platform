6.3 Word Formation — Suffixes

Suffixes are added to the end of a word to change its part of speech or meaning.

SuffixUseExamples
-atemakes verbs (also adjectives/nouns)activate, separate, participate
-ise / -izemakes verbs ("to make/become")modernise, recognise, realise
-mentmakes nouns (from verbs)development, improvement, movement
-tionmakes nouns (from verbs)education, protection, reduction
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Adding -tion/-ment turns a verb into a noun (educate → education; develop → development). Adding -ise/-ize/-ate turns a noun/adjective into a verb (modern → modernise; active → activate).
