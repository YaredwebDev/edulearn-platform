6.6 Grammar: Obligation, Prohibition, Absence of Obligation

◌ Not started
Modal Meaning Example
must obligation (from speaker's own view/strong) We must work to end poverty.
have to obligation (external rule/general law) People have to pay taxes.
mustn't prohibition (not allowed) You mustn't waste food.
don't have to no obligation (not necessary) You don't have to donate; it's optional.
need to necessity We need to find solutions.

✋ Trap — the big difference: mustn't = it is forbidden ("You mustn't steal"). don't have to / need not = it is not necessary ("You don't have to attend"). They are NOT the same.
