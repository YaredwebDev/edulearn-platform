📚 Unit 1 Summary (Key Facts)

  • Chemistry = study of properties, composition, structure, transformations, and energy of substances.
  • Substance, matter, property, composition, structure, transformation — key definitions.
  • Five branches: physical, organic, inorganic, analytical, biochemistry.
  • Chemistry = "central science" (overlaps: biochemistry, geochemistry, chemical physics, medicinal chemistry).
  • Roles: agriculture, food production, medicine, building construction.
  • Six classes of drugs.
  • Ethiopian chemical industries and products.
