15.5 Agriculture–Nutrition Linkage and Nutrition-Sensitive Agriculture

◌ Not started
  • Agriculture–nutrition linkage = the set of relationships showing the mutual dependence of nutrition, health & agriculture. Agricultural changes affect individual health/nutritional status; conversely, nutrition/health changes affect agricultural production. Agriculture greatly impacts poor rural households' food & nutrition security. Food security is one of the three pillars of good nutrition (with good care & good health). Better nutrition → better work capacity & productivity.
  • Nutrition-sensitive agriculture = a food-based approach to agricultural development that prioritises nutritionally rich foods, dietary diversity, and food fortification to overcome malnutrition. Agriculture is nutrition-sensitive when it goes beyond food production to address the underlying causes of malnutrition & micronutrient deficiencies. It stresses multiple benefits of eating a variety of foods and the social significance of food/agriculture for livelihoods. Overall objective: make the global food system better equipped to produce good nutritional outcomes.
