UNIT 1 · LESSON 8 OF 8 · GRADE 11 AGRICULTURE

🧮 Key facts to memorise

◌ Not started
  • Agriculture = deliberate cultivation of crops + rearing of animals.
  • Shifting = clear, cultivate a few years, abandon; Settled = continuous same land with improvements; Subsistence = only for household use (no surplus for trade).
  • Plant-selection features: ease of harvest, availability, transport, predictability/seasonality, climatic tolerance, flavour, grain size.
  • Eras: Mechanical (1930–50), Chemical (1950–70), Biotechnology/IT (1970–present).
  • Ethiopia: ~51 M ha arable, ~20% cultivated; top five crops teff/wheat/maize/sorghum/barley ≈75% of area ≈64% of calories.
  • Low yields due to: soil degradation, low-tech traditional farming, variable rainfall, low irrigation, low inputs.
  • Global top food crops: wheat, rice, maize.
  • Monocot → food in endosperm; Dicot (legumes/oil seeds) → food in cotyledons.
  • C3 crops (wheat, barley, teff, soybean, groundnut, sweet potato) need more CO₂; C4 crops (sorghum, maize, sugar cane, weeds) accumulate CO₂, more water-efficient, high rates at low CO₂.
  • Cropping systems: mono, mixed (intercropping, relay), crop rotation.
  • Indigenous knowledge examples: Konso terracing/irrigation, Gumuz conservation, Bale trenches, Gondar dung, highland ash & smoke.

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) define agriculture, shifting/settled agriculture, and subsistence farming and contrast subsistence vs commercial farming; (2) list the selection features and foods of hunter-gatherers and explain why legumes needed detoxifying; (3) state the three agricultural eras and the reasons for low Ethiopian yields (and why cereal area/production rose but yield per ha did not); (4) name the four/five end-use crop classes and the C3 vs C4 distinction with correct examples; (5) describe mono, mixed/intercropping/relay and rotation systems with pros/cons; and (6) give specific examples of indigenous knowledge in crop production? Re-answer the review too (which crop is NOT widely grown in Ethiopia — rice; the global big three = maize, rice, wheat; teff is NOT a legume; intercropping = two crops same time; cotton is not a forage crop; barley is a C3 crop; forage crops = animal feed; which factor is not used in domestication selection = susceptibility to pests).
