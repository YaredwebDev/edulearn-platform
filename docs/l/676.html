2.4 Unit Conversion

◌ Not started
  • Unit conversion: converting between different units of measurement for the same physical quantity, typically through multiplicative conversion factors.

Length conversions:

Conversion Factor
1 km 1000 m
1 m 100 cm
1 m 1000 mm
1 cm 10 mm
1 m 0.001 km
1 cm 0.01 m
1 mm 0.001 m

Example 2.3: 200 m = 20000 cm = 0.2 km = 2×10⁵ mm.

Mass conversions:

Conversion Factor
1 kg 1000 g
1 g 0.001 kg
1 mg 0.001 g
100 kg 1 quintal
1000 kg 1 tonne

Example 2.4: 1.5 kg + 500 g + 250 g + 25 g + 0.8 g = 2275.8 g = 2.2758 kg.

Time conversions:

Conversion Factor
1 minute 60 s
1 hour 60 min (3600 s)
1 day 24 hrs
1 week 7 days
1 month 30 days
1 year 365.25 days

Example 2.5: 2 hr = 7200 s; 0.5 hr = 1800 s; (3/5) hr = 2160 s. 1 km/hr = (10/36) m/s ≈ 0.278 m/s (used to convert speeds).
