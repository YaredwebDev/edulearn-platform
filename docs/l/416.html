1D Vocabulary Skills

◌ Not started

1D.1 Increase your word power

  • Vocabulary = knowledge of words and word meanings — how a word fits into the world. It expands over a lifetime; learned incidentally (exposure) and intentionally (explicit word-learning strategies).
  • "Without vocabulary nothing can be communicated, but without grammar something could be communicated."

1D.2 Contextual use of words / inferring meaning

  • Infer meanings of unknown words from context that precedes/follows, word parts (morphology), surrounding cues; construct a coherent definition; check the definition fits each use.

1D.3 Word-attack skills (strategies)

  1. Infer meaning from the general context preceding the word.
  2. Infer from the general context that follows the word.
  3. Look at word parts (morphology).
  4. Estimate how definite a definition is needed.
  5. Look for specific cues in the surrounding context.
  6. Construct a coherent definition using internal & external cues and world knowledge.
  7. Check the definition fits every appearance of the word.

1D.4 Phrasal verbs

  • A phrasal verb = verb + a particle (adverb/preposition) forming one meaningful unit (e.g. turn down, run into, turn over, set up).
  • Examples practised: work out (solve/exercise), get up, turn off, put on, go out, take off, wake up, and call for, come after, step in, result in, turn into, meet with. Other phrasal verbs to know: keep off, keep on, keep to, keep up, kick back, kick out, knock off, knock over, knock out.
  • Vocabulary practised (topic words): acid rain, bacterial, air pollution, deforestation, longer droughts, hazardous, climate change, pesticides, extreme flooding, oil spills, infectious viruses, consumerism.

✍️ Exam tip: environmental-hazard vocabulary (pollution, deforestation, drought, flooding, acid rain, toxic, hazardous) is frequent in national writing/vocabulary items. Learn phrasal verbs by particle meaning groups.
