📚 Unit 4 Summary (Key Facts)

◌ Not started
  • Early classifications: Dobereiner's triads; Newlands' law of octaves; Mendeleev's periodic law (atomic weight); modern law (atomic number, Moseley).
  • Mendeleev: 63 elements, 7 periods, 8 groups; predicted undiscovered elements; demerits (Ar/K, Te/I, Co/Ni).
  • Modern table: 7 periods, 18 groups; s, p, d, f blocks; representative vs transition vs inner-transition elements.
  • Period number = number of shells; group number (A) = number of valence electrons.
  • Four major trends: atomic radius, ionization energy, electron affinity, electronegativity.
