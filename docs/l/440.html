5D.1 Contextual meaning of words

  • Infer meaning from surrounding clues. Practised: eccentric (strange/unconventional — clues: letting a shepherd sit at the dinner table, refusing yellow food, visiting the fridge), atrocities (terrible cruel acts — clues: holocaust, genocide, Rwanda), excruciating (extremely painful — clues: cried out, collapsed, agony). Write contexts for: vulnerable, stricken, sustainable, diligence.

5D.2 Phrasal verbs (context)

  • look up (search for info) · look up to (admire/respect) · look after (take care of) · look around (explore) · make for (head toward) · make off with (run away carrying sth) · make up for (compensate for) · mess up (spoil/untidy) · mix up (confuse two people/things) · move out / move in (change residence).
