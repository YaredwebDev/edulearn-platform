⚡ Quick Revision Box (Unit 4)

  1. Interphase phases: G₁, S, G₂; DNA replicates in the S phase.
  2. Mitosis → 2 identical diploid (2n) cells; Meiosis → 4 haploid (n) cells.
  3. Meiosis I separates homologous chromosomes; Meiosis II separates sister chromatids.
  4. Meiosis II resembles mitosis.
  5. Human body (somatic) cells = 46 chromosomes (23 pairs, diploid 2n); gametes = 23 (haploid n).
  6. Mitosis stages = Prophase, Metaphase, Anaphase, Telophase (PMAT).
  7. Cell doctrine = Rudolf Virchow (1858) — new cells from pre-existing cells.
  8. Cytokinesis: animal cells pinch; plant cells form a middle lamella wall.
  9. Cancer = unregulated cell division ("cell madness") due to failure of the cell-cycle control.
  10. Ethiopian geneticist = Dr. Melaku Worede (seed conservation; Right Livelihood Award 1989).
