📚 Unit 5 Summary (Key Facts)

◌ Not started
  • Magnets have N and S poles that always come in pairs; like repel, unlike attract.
  • Types: permanent, temporary, electromagnet.
  • Magnetic field lines: closed loops, N→S outside / S→N inside, never intersect.
  • Earth's field ~ a bar magnet tilted ~11°; origin = molten iron in outer core.
  • Current-carrying wire → circular magnetic field; B = μ₀I/2πd; right-hand rule.
  • Moving charge force: F = qvB sin θ; current-carrying wire: F = ILB sin θ.
  • Parallel wires: same-direction currents attract; opposite repel.
  • Applications: MRI, speakers, hard drives, motors/generators, compass.
