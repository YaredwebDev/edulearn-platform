9.6 Major Diseases of Dairy Cattle and Their Control

◌ Not started

Diseases heavily affecting dairy cattle: FMD, blackleg, anthrax, brucellosis, chronic respiratory disease (CRD), CBPP; internal parasites (liver fluke, schistosomiasis); external parasites (ticks, flee/flies, tsetse flies). Nutritional disorders: bloat, diarrhoea, acidosis, ketosis, milk fever. Reproductive diseases: dystocia, retention of placenta, vaginal prolapse. Prevent through good herd management, proper nutrition, housing and vaccination.

Disease Type Symptoms Transmission Prevention/Control
Chronic Respiratory Disease (CRD) Viral swollen face, nasal discharge, difficulty breathing contaminated feed, water, bedding vaccination, sanitation, good ventilation
Bovine tuberculosis (TB) Bacterial dry cough with blood-stained sputum, emaciation, death inhaling aerosol droplets; orally via contaminated feed vaccination
Blackleg Bacterial lameness, depression, loss of appetite, hot painful swelling on a limb that crackles when pressed ingestion of spores while grazing vaccination
Mastitis Bacterial uneasiness at milking, blood traces in milk, swollen/painful udder contaminated feed/water, udder/teat injury, dirty environment disinfect hands before milking, sanitation, avoid overcrowding, antibiotics
Trypanosomiasis Protozoan drowsiness, severe anaemia, weight loss, death tsetse fly (Glossina species) insecticides, good sanitation

Note: FMD, CBPP, Brucellosis, Anthrax, Taeniasis are covered in Unit 8.

✋ Trap: the review asks for a reproductive disease → Dystocia (not mastitis/anthrax/rickets).
