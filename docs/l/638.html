4.3 The First Condition of Equilibrium

◌ Not started

Equilibrium = a state of rest or of uniform motion along a fixed direction. The first condition of equilibrium: the net force on the body is zero, i.e. ΣF = 0 (equivalently ΣFx = 0 and ΣFy = 0). When in equilibrium the body is at rest or moves at constant velocity; acceleration is zero.

✍️ Must-know: equilibrium does not require the body to be at rest — a body moving at constant velocity is also in equilibrium (a = 0). If only one (nonzero) force acts on an object, it cannot be in equilibrium.

Free-body-diagram technique: draw all forces, resolve into components, and set ΣFx = 0 and ΣFy = 0.

Example 4.9: a 100 N traffic light hangs from a vertical cable tied to two cables making 37° and 53° with the horizontal — resolve and solve the two equilibrium equations to get the tensions in the three cables. Review: a mass 10 kg supported by two strings in equilibrium, find the horizontal tension.
