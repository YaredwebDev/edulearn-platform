5.6 Writing: Topic Sentence & Paragraph

◌ Not started
  • Topic sentence carries the main idea of the paragraph; can be at the beginning, middle, or end — but put it first for beginners (it guides supporting details).
  • A good topic sentence draws the reader's attention to read on.
  • Example: "Haile Gebresillassie is one of Ethiopia's most famous long distance runners." — the rest of the paragraph supports this.
  • Practise writing a topic sentence then adding supporting details (e.g., for Adwa, disability, transport, Catherine Ndreba paragraphs).
