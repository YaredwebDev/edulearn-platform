8.1 Reading: A Traditional Medicine, Moringa Oleifera (the core "story"/text)

◌ Not started

The passage has five paragraphs on the "Miracle Tree."

Paragraph 1 — What Moringa is: - Moringa Oleifera (Ethiopians call it "Shiferaw") is called the "Miracle Tree" and the "Tree of Life"; people benefit from it for prevention and healing; it is nutritious, natural, energising. - Immature pods are the most valued part — contain all essential amino acids plus vitamins/nutrients; eaten raw or like green peas/beans; mature pods fried (peanut-like flavour). - Pods yield 38–40% non-drying edible oil called Ben Oil — clear, sweet, unscented, never rancid; nutritional value resembles olive oil. - Root → pickle; leaves → greens, salads, curries, pickles, seasoning; bark → tanning + coarse fibre; flowers (must be cooked) → rich in potassium and calcium.

Paragraph 2 — A complete food: - Moringa is a complete food — contains all essential amino acids required for a healthy body (the 9 the human body cannot synthesise, so they must come from food). Quote from Ann Hirsch, PhD (California researcher).

Paragraph 3 — Fighting malnutrition: - Used in tropical developing countries to combat malnutrition, especially among infants and nursing mothers. - Three NGOs advocate it as "natural nutrition for the tropics": Trees for Life, Church World Service, Educational Concerns for Hunger Organization. - Leaves eaten fresh, cooked, or stored as dried powder for months without refrigeration without losing nutritional value.

Paragraph 4 — Recommended daily allowances (Optima of Africa, Tanzania): - 25 grams daily of Moringa leaf powder gives a child: Protein 42%, Calcium 125%, Magnesium 61%, Potassium 41%, Iron 71%, Vitamin A 272%, Vitamin C 22%. - Especially valuable when other food sources are scarce.

Paragraph 5 — Medicinal uses: - Studies in India, South Africa, Asian countries: Moringa contains anti-cancer compounds (phytochemicals) that help stop the growth of cancer cells; effective in treating ovarian cancer among other diseases (arthritis, anemia, heart complications, kidney problems, scurvy, asthma, digestive disorders — ulcer, gastritis, diarrhea, dysentery). - Folk medicine: diabetes relief, healthy skin, better nutrition, better sleep, decreased depression & anxiety; value in treating tumors.

✍️ Must-know: Moringa/Shiferaw = complete food (all 9 essential amino acids); parts used = pods (Ben Oil, 38–40%), leaves, root, bark, flowers; combats malnutrition; 25 g leaf powder daily = the RDA percentages (esp. Vitamin A 272%, Calcium 125%); anti-cancer phytochemicals; 3 NGOs (Trees for Life, Church World Service, Educational Concerns for Hunger).
