📌 QUICK REVISION / MUST-KNOW BOX (Unit 6)

  • Sum of interior angles = (n−2)×180°.
  • Sum of exterior angles = 360° (always).
  • Regular n-gon: interior = (n−2)×180°/n, exterior = 360°/n, central = 360°/n.
  • Interior + exterior = 180° at each vertex.
  • n lines of symmetry for a regular n-gon.
  • Inscribed polygon formulas: s = 2r sin(180°/n), a = r cos(180°/n), P = 2nr sin(180°/n), A = ½ aP.
  • Apothem = perpendicular from center to side midpoint.
