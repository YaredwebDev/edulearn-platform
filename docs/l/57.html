9.7 Profitability in Dairy Business

◌ Not started
  • Dairy has profitable margins in Ethiopia; demand rises with economic growth & urban middle class. Creates employment, especially for the young.
  • Main purpose = earn income from milk sales. Profit = income − expenses (feed, supplements, medication, labour, other).
  • Worked example: 10 cross-bred cows × 12 L/day = 120 L/day = ~3,600 L/month. At 50 birr/L → 6,000 birr/day = 180,000 birr/month. Expenses = 125,250 birr/month. Net profit = 54,750 birr/month.
  • Maximise margins via good management: forage production (year-round supply, cut feed costs — feed is ~60–70% of expenditure), feed & nutrition management, fertility management, hygienic milk production & marketing.
