4.8 Human Pedigree Analysis

◌ Not started
  • Since humans can't be crossed experimentally, researchers use pedigrees (family trees) to understand how traits/diseases are inherited.
  • A pedigree shows whether a trait is autosomal dominant, autosomal recessive, or X-linked.
  • Symbols: square = male, circle = female; affected individuals shaded/coloured; a horizontal "marriage line" connects parents; vertical lines connect children to couples; rows = generations (oldest at top).
  • What pedigree analysis can reveal: (1) dominant vs recessive; (2) autosomal vs sex-linked; (3) genotypes of family members; (4) probabilities of phenotypes in future generations.
