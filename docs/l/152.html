5.15 Family Planning

◌ Not started

Family planning = the ability to anticipate and attain a desired number of children, the spacing/timing of births, and the resources for couples to establish a family. Deals with marital, career, financial and child-number choices; ensures resources (time, social, financial, environmental) to raise children.

Risks of lack of family planning: higher maternal-health risk (care in pregnancy, childbirth, postpartum), increased risk in early & late motherhood (especially teenagers), whole-family threat when a mother is ill, and complications of pregnancy/childbirth (abortions, disability, deaths). In developing regions ~218 million women lack safe, effective family planning.

Family planning actions: prevent unwanted pregnancy; keep family safe from STIs + counseling + fertility management; adequate resources; wait at least 6 months after a miscarriage or abortion; adoption as an option; birth control & assisted reproductive technology; adjust the family-plan calendar to the natural menstrual cycle.

Family planning services: pregnancy testing & counseling; pregnancy-achieving services (preconception health); counseling on infertility; counseling on STDs; breast & pelvic examinations; breast & cervical cancer screening.
