2.1 Characteristics of Plants

◌ Not started

Plants share many characteristics with other living things but also have unique features:

Characteristic Detail
Living things They grow, reproduce, and respond to environmental changes
Multicellular Made of many eukaryotic cells with a well-defined nucleus and membrane-bound organelles
Rigid cell wall Made primarily of cellulose (in addition to the cell membrane)
Autotrophic (producers) Contain the green pigment chlorophyll to absorb sunlight and make their own food
Sessile Cannot move by themselves; anchored by roots (but leaves turn toward light, roots orient toward water)
Asexual & sexual reproduction Lower plants (mosses, liverworts) reproduce asexually by spores; higher seed-plants (gymnosperms, angiosperms) reproduce sexually by gametes
