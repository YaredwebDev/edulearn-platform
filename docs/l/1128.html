10.1 Reading: The Roles of Computers and the Internet in Our Lives

(the core "story"/text of the unit)

The passage gives advantages and disadvantages of computers and the internet.

Advantages:

  • A huge store of easily available information at a click of the mouse.

  • Students can submit neat, legible assignments and use software applications, coloured graphs and diagrams; spelling/grammar errors are largely avoided.

  • Knowledge spreads: anyone can publish on the internet; research students need not labour through manuscripts; universities, websites and queries can be consulted online — saving time, labour and money.

  • E-forums and conferences allow healthy discussion.

  • It builds a global family (international communities on social networks) instead of narrow community/caste groups.

  • People can be brought together quickly to protest, fight for rights, or garner support; doctors, lawyers and teachers can be consulted online.

Disadvantages:

  • A negative counter-point exists for each positive aspect.

  • People spend hours chatting or surfing and neglect family — less communicative, more dependent on the computer.

  • Children (esp. in developed countries) may spend ~20 hours a week playing computer games.

  • Online games often show violence and aggressiveness; children may accept violence as normal behaviour.

  • It can ring the death knell on good habits: reading is dying, communicative skills deteriorate, and health suffers from long hours at the screen.

  • Children can easily get in touch with negative groups.

The key message: "It depends on our wisely making use of a computer to turn it into a boon instead of a bane."

✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Questions test (a) advantages vs disadvantages, (b) the main idea of paragraph 1 (the computer is a controversial invention with merits and demerits), (c) what "these" refers to in ¶3, (d) how internet use decreases reading/communication, and (e) vocabulary in context. True/false questions require you to give evidence from the passage.
