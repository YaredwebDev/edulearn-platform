6.1 Reading: Migration in Ethiopia (the core "story"/text)

The passage has four paragraphs on the causes and challenges of Ethiopian migration.

Paragraph 1 — Root causes & the journey:

  • There is consensus on the need to address root causes of migration.

  • Factors: economic & political violence, ecological degradation, drought, human-right abuses.

  • The main driving forces = lack of decent livelihood and employment opportunities.

  • Travel by boat over the Red Sea, then by land through Yemen to Saudi Arabia and the Gulf states (favoured for employment).

  • Most emigrants travel irregularly, without legal status → treated inhumanly, sometimes killed.

  • The country loses respect and integrity too.

  • Ethiopia imposed a ban on migration to the Middle East in 2013, but it didn't help — it increased irregular crossings. In January 2016, ~10,000 migrants from east Africa reached Yemen, of whom 75% were Ethiopians.

Paragraph 2 — Youth employment challenge:

  • ~3 million young Ethiopians enter the labour force every year; full productive employment is a challenge in rural and urban areas.

  • Numbers moving to cities grow; they join informal economic activities — a stepping stone to migrating abroad.

  • The exact number of overseas migrants is unknown (no centralised registration); most go to Gulf countries, Europe, and South Africa via irregular channels.

Paragraph 3 — Educated unemployment & illegal activities:

  • Despite economic progress, job creation is insufficient; the number of unemployed educated youth has increased at an unprecedented rate.

  • Many graduates do work not compatible with their professions.

  • Due to desperation, some engage in illegal economic activities (smuggling, contraband) with limited social protections.

  • The situation is worse for women, concentrated in the informal sector.

Paragraph 4 — The solution:

  • 71% of the population is under 30 — a productive force.

  • Ethiopia is endowed with immense natural resources.

  • If youth are supported to create jobs and economic sectors expand, irregular migration will decrease substantially.

  • Countries with half of Ethiopia's resources have achieved huge economic progress — so "how, when, and by whom should this puzzle be solved?"

✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Root cause = lack of decent livelihood/employment (not just poverty); 2013 ban backfired; 2016: ~10,000/month reaching Yemen, 75% Ethiopian; 3 million new labour-force entrants/year; 71% under 30. Solution = support youth job creation + use abundant resources.
