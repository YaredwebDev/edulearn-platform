6.11 Climate change

◌ Not started
  • Climate = the usual weather of a place. Climate change = a change in the usual weather patterns (rain, temperature) over a month/season.
  • Causes: natural (internal fluctuations of energy/water/carbon; solar variations; volcanic eruptions) and human — especially combustion of fossil fuels, raising levels of greenhouse gases: carbon dioxide (CO₂), methane, nitrous oxide, fluorinated gases.
  • Effects: greenhouse effect → global warming (heat trapped like a greenhouse/blanket).
