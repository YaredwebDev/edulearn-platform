1.2 Branches of Physics

BranchDescription
MechanicsDeals with the motion of objects with or without reference to force. Subdivided into quantum mechanics (behavior of smallest particles like neutrons, protons, electrons) and classical mechanics (laws of motion of forces and physical objects).
AcousticsStudy of sound and its transmission, production, and effects.
OpticsBehavior, propagation, and properties of light.
ThermodynamicsStudies thermal energy and the transfer of heat.
ElectromagnetismStudy of electromagnetic force (electric fields, light, magnetic fields). Two aspects: "electricity" and "magnetism".
Nuclear physicsStructure, properties, and reactions of the nuclei of atoms.
AstrophysicsUses the methods and principles of physics to study astronomical objects and phenomena.
✍️ Must-know: The branches of physics include Mechanics, Acoustics, Optics, Thermodynamics, Electromagnetism, Nuclear Physics, Astrophysics.
