5.1 Reversible and irreversible reactions

◌ Not started

Do all reactions go to completion? How much reactant remains? Why does a reaction reach equilibrium? These are answered by chemical equilibrium.

  • Irreversible (one-way) reactions: reactants → products essentially completely; written with a single arrow (→). E.g. C(s) + O₂(g) → CO₂(g).
  • Reversible (two-way) reactions: proceed in both the forward and reverse directions; written with a double arrow (⇌). General form aA + bB ⇌ cC + dD. E.g. N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃; H₂ + I₂ ⇌ 2HI.
  • Forward reaction = reactants → products; reverse (backward) reaction = products → reactants.
