5.4 Classification of Simple Machines

◌ Not started

Simple machines are categorized into two groups, giving six types:

1. Inclined planes: - Ramp (inclined plane) - Wedge - Screw

2. Levers: - Lever - Wheel & axle - Pulley

Three categories by function: 1. Force multipliers: load > effort (load moves a smaller distance than effort). 2. Speed multipliers: distance moved by load > distance moved by effort in the same time. 3. Direction changers: load moved in a different direction to the effort.
