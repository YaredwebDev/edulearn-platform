UNIT 11 · LESSON 8 OF 8 · GRADE 11 AGRICULTURE

🧮 Key facts to memorise

◌ Not started
  • Carrying capacity = max population a resource can support (100 sheep/hectare example).
  • Sustainable use = meet current needs without harming future generations.
  • Soil management practices: mulching, mixed cropping, contour cropping/cultivation/strip cropping, organic matter & fertility improvement, liming (raise pH), acid-tolerant crops, saline-soil leaching.
  • Salt-tolerant: sorghum, barley, wheat; salt-sensitive: rice. Acid-tolerant: rice, cowpea, cassava, mango.
  • Water losses: evaporation, transpiration, runoff, deep percolation. Practices: rainwater harvesting, mulching (reduce evaporation), soil conservation (contour bunding, bench terraces e.g. Konso), drought-tolerant crops, efficient irrigation.
  • Forest management = afforestation, replanting, careful harvest, fire prevention; provides fuelwood, biodiversity, carbon storage, jobs.
  • Endangered endemics in Ethiopia: Walia Ibex & Ethiopian Wolf.
  • Wildlife management = protect habitats, buffer zones, control encroachment/invasive species/population.
  • Environmental management goal: prevent release of harmful chemicals; protect human health & all life.
  • Indigenous knowledge: N. Shewa (waterways, ditches, stone lines, edge trees, vegetative barriers, stone bunds+vegetation); Gumuz beliefs (sacred resources, Yamba/God gift, community ownership, customary laws by elders).
  • Review answers: a form of soil degradation = All (D); practice to reduce soil degradation = Mulching & Mixed cropping (C, A and B); agricultural water management = Conserving rain water & soil conservation (C, both A and B); effective forest management does NOT result in wildlife degradation (A); endangered wild animals in Ethiopia = Walia Ibex & Ethiopian Wolf (C, A and B).

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) explain carrying capacity & sustainable use with examples; (2) state the principal objective of soil management and list the practices (agronomic, organic matter, liming, saline management); (3) explain why agricultural water management is needed and describe rainwater conservation, evaporation reduction, soil conservation and drought-tolerant/irrigation methods; (4) relate forest management to wildlife management (incl. the Ethiopian examples & the grazing-in-national-parks problem); (5) explain the objectives & aims of environmental management; and (6) give examples of indigenous knowledge practices in soil/water and the Gumuz belief system?
