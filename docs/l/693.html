📚 Unit 4 Summary (Key Facts)

◌ Not started
  • Force = push/pull changing motion or shape (vector).
  • Contact vs non-contact forces.
  • Newton's three laws of motion; inertia; F = ma; action-reaction.
  • Mass (inherent, constant) vs weight (W = mg, changes with g).
  • Friction: static and kinetic; proportional to normal force.
  • Work = F∥ × S; scalar; Joule; work = energy transfer.
  • Kinetic energy ½mv²; gravitational potential energy mgh; mechanical energy = sum.
  • Power = rate of work/energy; watt.
