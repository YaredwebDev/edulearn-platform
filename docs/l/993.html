📚 Unit 3 Summary (Key Facts)

  • Atom = from Greek "a-tomos" (uncuttable). Greek four elements: earth, air, water, fire.
  • Democritus/Leucippus: atomism (solid sphere model).
  • Three laws of chemical combination: Conservation of Mass, Definite Proportions, Multiple Proportions.
  • Dalton's Atomic Theory (5 tenets); Modern Atomic Theory (postulates, isotopes, isobars).
  • Discoveries: electron (Thomson 1897), charge/mass (Millikan 1909), nucleus (Rutherford 1911/1920), proton (Goldstein 1886; Rutherford 1920), neutron (Chadwick 1932).
  • Models: solid sphere → plum pudding → planetary/nuclear → Bohr.
  • Z = protons; A = protons + neutrons; neutral atom p = e.
  • Isotopes vs isobars; average atomic mass calculation.
  • Electronic configuration (2n²); valence electrons.
