10.6 Causes & consequences of air pollution

◌ Not started
  • Atmosphere = thin air layer around Earth; ~78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen (99% together); remaining 1% = water vapour, CO₂, other gases.
  • Air pollution = abnormally high concentration of harmful/poisonous substances in the atmosphere. Air picks up dust, pollen, spores, particles; human activities release smoke, dust, gases.
  • Causes: solid particulate matter & harmful gases; vehicle exhaust, industrial chemicals, dust from agriculture & construction; soot & smoke from biomass burning; pollen & spores; ozone can be toxic to plants & animals.
  • Consequences: breathing polluted air lets toxic substances enter the body → respiratory problems, heart disease, lung cancer, lower life expectancy; also harms livestock & plants (reduced health & productivity).
