1.4 Some Common Chemical Industries in Ethiopia

  • Industry: an economic activity concerned with processing raw materials and manufacturing goods in factories.
  • Chemical industries: manufacture inorganic- and organic-industrial chemicals, explosives, fragrances, agrochemicals, polymers and rubber, ceramics, petrochemicals, oleochemicals, and flavors.

Three classes of chemical products: (1) basic chemicals (alkalis, acids, organic chemicals, salts); (2) chemical products for further manufacture (plastics, synthetic fibers, pigments); (3) finished chemical products for ultimate consumption (cosmetics, drugs, soaps) or use as materials/supplies (fertilizers, paints, explosives).
