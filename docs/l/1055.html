7.6 Speaking: opinions, agreement, disagreement, advice

Stating an opinion: In my opinion…, The way I see it…, If you want my honest opinion…, According to…, As far as I'm concerned…, If you ask me…

Asking for an opinion: What's your idea? What do you think? How do you feel about that? Wouldn't you say? Do you agree?

Agreeing: Exactly. That's so true. I agree with you 100%. I couldn't agree with you more. That's right. You're absolutely right.

Disagreeing (politely): I don't think so. Not really. I'm afraid I disagree. I beg to differ. Yes, but… Actually, no. (strong) I totally disagree.

Suggestions: Let's…; What about / How about + -ing; You could…

Advice: should / ought to / had better; Why don't you…?; What if…?
