3E.1 Adverbial clauses of reason/cause

  • Gives the cause or reason for the action of the main clause. Subordinating conjunctions: because, as, since, that.
  • As & since are used when the reason is already known to the listener (As it is raining again, we will have to cancel the match). Because gives info not already known.
  • Examples: Because he had not paid the bill, his electricity was cut off; Since he has apologized, we will take no further action; As he was not there, I left a message with his mother; I am glad that you have come.
  • Practise joining ideas (e.g. It is snowing → I will have to cancel the road trip: Because it is snowing, I will cancel the road trip).

3E.2 Adverbial clauses of result/effect

  • Says what happens (or may happen) as a result of the main clause. Conjunctions: so that, in order that, so…that, such…that.
  • so that / in order that = purpose (in order that is more formal): Speak clearly so that we can all hear you; He spoke loudly in order that everybody would hear him. Present or future tenses possible.
  • so…that — so directly before a that-clause: It was so hot that we didn't go out (often rephraseable with too…to: too hot to go out).
  • such…that — such + noun before a that-clause: She spoke in such a low voice that nobody could hear her; He is such a good man that all respect him.

Transition words for cause–effect

  • Signals: accordingly, as a result, because of, so, due to, for that reason, consequently, on account of, if…then, leads to, results in, hence, thus, therefore, since.
  • Also verbs/nouns that signal cause-effect (sample): verbs — cause, contribute, create, determine, induce, influence, lead to, result in, prevent; nouns — cause, effect, consequence, outcome, result, factor, source, impact.
