2B.1 Pronunciation: Diphthongs (glided sounds)

  • Monophthongs (Unit 1) hold the tongue in one position. Diphthongs are a sequence of two vowel sounds in one syllable where one vowel glides over the other (the movement = gliding), e.g. the "ou" in out, "oy" in boy.
  • The eight English diphthongs (with examples): 1. /aʊ/ town, sound, cow, house 2. /aɪ/ light, fight, die, time 3. /eɪ/ play, way, day, name 4. /eə/ pair, bare, fair, dare 5. /ɪə/ deer, near, dear, here 6. /əʊ/ slow, flow, home, blow 7. /ɔɪ/ toy, oil, boil, soil 8. /ʊə/ sure, tour, pure, lure.
  • Practise distinguishing diphthong vs monophthong word pairs (pool/pure, bite/bid, time/team, face/far).

2B.2 Interview (ways of asking questions)

  • Interviewer = person asking; interviewee = person answering.
  • Types of interview questions:
  • Open-ended — no specific answer: Tell me about yourself; Why did you make those choices?
  • Specific — concrete answers.
  • Motivational — about a candidate's motivation.
  • Unconventional — stressful/bizarre: If you were a tree/car, which/what would you be?
  • Language of interview: Can you please tell me about…? Why are you the best person for…? How has your experience prepared you…? When do you…? If you were…?

2B.3 Expressing preferences

  • Expressions: like / dislike · would rather · prefer to · rather than.
  • Patterns: prefer + to-infinitive + rather than (I prefer to collect old coins rather than have gold); would rather … than … (I'd rather visit ancient places than go to new buildings); would rather + perfect (I'd rather have stayed at home).
  • Report a partner: My friend likes/prefers…; my friend dislikes/doesn't…

✍️ Presentation skill note: an effective oral presentation needs attention to audience, planning, delivery; use notes, visual aids, and structure (a 3–5 minute talk on modern civilization/technology).
