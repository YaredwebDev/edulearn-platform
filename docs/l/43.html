UNIT 7 · LESSON 5 OF 5 · GRADE 11 AGRICULTURE

🧮 Key facts to memorise

◌ Not started
  • Significance: environmental control; disease/parasite control; efficient feed & labour; predator protection.
  • Housing type depends on: animal type, climate, local tradition, building materials, economics.
  • Conventional barns: fully roofed, complete walls + ventilation, intensive, heavy-rain/temperate; tie stalls being phased out (welfare).
  • Loose housing: free movement, self-feeding, less bedding/manure, tropical (Ethiopia), cheap & flexible.
  • Free range: large pasture area, attendant, natural behaviour, Ethiopia's local chickens/small ruminants.
  • Modern farms: cut-and-carry/prepared feed, high investment, best productivity; enriched cages & aviary systems for layers.
  • Five freedoms: thirst/hunger, discomfort, disease/pain/injury, distress/fear, natural behaviours.
  • Site: elevated & level, well-drained (porous, gentle slope), water/feed/vet nearby, accessible (roads, power), away from residences/hospitals/schools, avoid highways, enough space, size with expansion.
  • Floor: cement concrete, drained, non-slippery. Walls: protect from drafts, smooth inside. Roof: galvanised, sloped, overhang ≥60 cm. Ventilation: remove CO₂ & NH₃, ≥3 m height.
  • East–West = avoids sun (increased feed intake); North–South = dry floor.

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) explain the four purposes/significance of animal housing; (2) describe and compare conventional barns (incl. deep litter & tie stalls), loose housing, free range and modern farm systems (incl. enriched cages/aviary) and when each is used (esp. Ethiopia = loose housing); (3) list the five freedoms of animal welfare and explain what good welfare requires; (4) state the site-selection factors (topography/drainage, water/feed/vet, accessibility, distance from residences, health, size); and (5) give the construction guidelines for orientation (East–West vs North–South), floor, walls, roof and ventilation? Re-answer the review too (East–West does NOT keep the floor dry — that's North–South; purposes = all of the above; elevated-level site = topography; animals confined together tied at neck by stanchions = conventional barn; "housed with or apart from other animals" = freedom to exhibit natural behaviours).
