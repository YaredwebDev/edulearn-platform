5.3 Grammar: Conditionals (continued)

◌ Not started

5.3.1 The Second Conditional (unreal / unlikely present)

If + past (were), + would/could/might + V1. - "If I had a garden, I would grow vegetables." - "If I were you, I would relax more." Used for imaginary/hypothetical situations that are unlikely or not true now.

5.3.2 The Third Conditional (impossible past)

If + had + V3, + would/could/might + have + V3. - "If she had watered the plants, they would not have died." - "If he had rested, he would have recovered sooner." Used to talk about imaginary past — situations that did not happen.

✋ Trap — "I were": In second conditionals use were for all subjects: "If I were rich…" (not "if I was"). Third conditional expresses regret/speculation about the past — the condition is impossible to change.
