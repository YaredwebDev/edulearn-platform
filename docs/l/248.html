4.2.2 Meiosis

◌ Not started

Meiosis = a characteristic of sexually reproducing organisms; occurs in reproductive organs (ovaries, testes, anther and ovules of flowering plants).

  • Involves two fissions of the nucleus giving rise to four gametes (sex cells), each with half the number of chromosomes (n) — called reduction division.
  • A diploid (2n) mother cell → four haploid (n) daughter cells.

Why meiosis is important: if human gametes had 46 chromosomes, the zygote would have 92 (not normal). During gametogenesis in ovaries/testes, the 46 chromosomes are reduced to 23; sperm + egg (n + n) → zygote with the normal 46 (2n).

Meiosis I: - Prophase I: chromosomes appear shortened/thickened (two chromatids); homologous chromosomes pair up (synapsis); crossing over occurs. - Metaphase I: homologous pairs line up on the metaphase plate; centromeres do NOT separate. - Anaphase I: homologous chromosomes (each with paired chromatids) separate and move to opposite poles — reduction to haploid (n) occurs. - Telophase I: nuclear membrane reforms; cells begin to divide; may be a prolonged interphase but no further DNA duplication.

Meiosis II (similar to mitosis): - Prophase II: new spindles form; chromosomes move to the middle. - Metaphase II: chromosomes line up; spindle attached to sister chromatids from opposite poles. - Anaphase II: centromeres divide; sister chromatids separate and move to opposite ends. - Telophase II: nuclear membrane reappears; cytokinesis → four haploid daughter cells.

Feature Mitosis Meiosis
Purpose Growth, repair, asexual reproduction Gamete formation (sexual reproduction)
Where Somatic (body) cells Reproductive organs (ovaries, testes)
Number of divisions 1 2 (Meiosis I and II)
Daughter cells 2 4
Chromosome number Same as parent (2n → 2n) Halved (2n → n)
Daughter cells identical? Yes (identical clones) No (genetic variation)
Crossing over No Yes (Prophase I)

✍️ Must-know: Meiosis I separates homologous chromosomes (reduction to n); Meiosis II separates sister chromatids (like mitosis). Result: 4 haploid cells.
