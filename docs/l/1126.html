9.5 Speaking: Causes, Effects and Prevention of Discrimination

The unit gives a table on discrimination in society. Know these categories:

Causes of discrimination: age, disability, ethnicity, origin, political belief, race, religion, sex/gender, language, culture.

Effects of discrimination on society: unequal societies, social/environmental/economic/cultural problems, and unity problems.

Ways of preventing discrimination: respect cultural and racial differences in schools, workplaces and communal activities; be good in conduct and speech; refuse to initiate, participate in, or condone discrimination and harassment; avoid race-based or culturally offensive talks, humour or stories.

✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: In Ethiopia, policy holds there should be no educational discrimination among citizens (by ethnicity, nationality, age, gender, race, economic condition, disability or religion). This is a speaking/discussion topic.

📝 Writing focus: Descriptive Essay

  • Extended writing on the reading passage: define cultural diversity; describe the benefits of being aware of it; describe the challenges/disadvantages.
  • Descriptive essay using the present perfect: the textbook's model paragraph ("Merhawit has just walked outside with her grandmother...") shows how to use present perfect (has finished, has gathered, has just dropped, have not come, has moved in, has already sat down, has just finished) to describe an ongoing scene. Use the same pattern in your own paragraph.
  • Write about your community: describe where it is, how many people live there, livelihood, religion and ethnicity, natural/man-made heritages, and other features.
Note: ✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) explain equality vs equity vs justice and give the school-funding and runner examples, (2) define all 10 passage vocabulary words, (3) use the phrasal verbs count/crack/cross/cut, (4) form and choose between simple past and present perfect, (5) use since/for correctly, (6) list causes/effects/prevention of discrimination, (7) write a descriptive essay?
