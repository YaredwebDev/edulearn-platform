4.1 Charges in Nature

◌ Not started
  • Two types of electric charge: positive (protons) and negative (electrons).
  • Neutral objects have equal numbers of positive and negative charges.
  • If an object gains electrons → negatively charged; loses electrons → positively charged.

Unit of charge: the coulomb (C). 1 C = 6.25×10¹⁸ electrons. Charge of one electron = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C. Subunits: 1 µC = 10⁻⁶ C, 1 nC = 10⁻⁹ C.

Law of conservation of charge: charges are neither created nor destroyed, only transferred from one material to another. Total charge in an isolated system never changes.

Quantization of charge: charge occurs in integral multiples of the elementary charge e: q = ne (n = positive or negative integer).

✍️ Must-know: Like charges repel, unlike charges attract. Charge is conserved and quantized (q = ne).
