📚 Unit 1 Summary (Key Facts)

  • Science is systematized knowledge from observation, study, and experimentation.
  • Physics = branch of natural science describing the basic mechanisms of the universe.
  • Physicist = one who studies physics.
  • Branches: mechanics, acoustics, optics, thermodynamics, electromagnetism, nuclear physics, astrophysics.
  • Physics is the foundation of chemistry, engineering, geology, biophysics, geophysics, medical physics.
  • Classical physics → modern physics (20th century revolution).
  • Key contributors: Newton, Faraday, Joule, Curie, Einstein.
