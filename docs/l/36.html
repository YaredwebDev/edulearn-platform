6.4 Animal Identification and Record Keeping

◌ Not started
  • Performance records are one of the best management tools for a successful genetic-improvement program. They help identify weaknesses in herd/flock performance, inform decisions on flock management, selection of new breeding stock and culling low performers, and track treated animals (medical histories aid culling decisions).
  • Recording phenotypic data (an individual's observable traits such as height & colour) is the major driving force for genetic progress; progress depends on data accuracy.
  • Individual identification lets producers keep records of an animal's parentage, birth date and production.
  • Identification systems:
  • Permanent identification: branding, ear tags, tattooing, ear notching, microchips.
  • Temporary/simple methods: tail marking (pen & chalk), clipping hair, dying fur.
  • Animal identification & trait recording are fundamental to all breeding programs; there are two basic types of identification — permanent and temporary.

✋ Trap: permanent identification = branding, ear tags, tattooing, ear notching, microchips. Temporary = tail marking, hair clipping, fur dyeing.
