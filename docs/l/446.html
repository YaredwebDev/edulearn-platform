6D.1 Guessing meaning from context

  • Practise using surrounding words: grapple (struggle to deal with), snarl (block/tangle traffic), curtail (reduce/limit), potent (powerful/strong), unabated (not lessened), irreversible (cannot be undone).

6D.2 Phrasal verbs

  • In the box: pile up (accumulate), pay back (return money/repay), pay for (bear the cost), narrow down (reduce options), pay up (settle a debt).
  • Also find meanings & use: pick on (single out unfairly), pick out (select/choose), pick up (collect; learn), pay off (be worth it / repay fully).
