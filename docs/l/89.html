14.3 Uses of Some Farm Tools and Equipment

◌ Not started
  • Tractor — most popular & essential equipment; used for planting, tilling, spreading fertiliser.
  • Plough — one of the most ancient tools; cultivates soil & prepares it for sowing; modern ploughs pulled by tractors.
  • Subsoiler — tractor-mounted; breaks up & loosens soil during deep tillage; helps crop growth where soil compaction is a problem.
  • Cultivator — carries out secondary tillage; usually rotary; removes weeds and pulverizes/stirs soil before planting.
  • Cultipacker — crushes soil clods; eliminates cracks, presses small stones, removes air pockets to form a smooth, firm seedbed.
  • Harrow — smooths & breaks up the soil surface; breaks up lumps producing seedbed structure.
  • Seed drill — plants seeds at equal distance & proper depth.
  • Sprinkler — irrigates large crop areas & maintains humidity.
  • Combine harvester — versatile; harvests grain crops by combining four operations (reaping, threshing, gathering, winnowing) into a single process.
  • Straw collector — collects straw/hay into stacks. Baler — compresses hay/straw into bales for easy transport & storage (a bale is the simplest minimum marketing package).
  • Feed miller/grinder — power grinding mill reducing maize/wheat/barley to coarse meal for animal feeding. Feed mixer — thoroughly & hygienically mixes feed ingredients during ration formulation.
  • Milking machine — most important dairy piece; harvests milk automatically. Milk cooler — cools milk below 4 °C within 2 hours of milking to avoid rapid deterioration. Butter churner — converts cream into butter & buttermilk.
  • Incubator — device with fan & heater keeping fertile eggs warm for the 21-day incubation period.
  • Sprayer — sprays liquids (insecticides, herbicides, pesticides, water) on crops or on animals to control external parasites.
  • Pruning shears — sharp, heavy-duty scissors for cutting tree branches & plant stems.
