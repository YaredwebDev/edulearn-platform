⚡ Quick Revision Box (Unit 3)

  1. Universal solvent, high heat capacity → water (due to hydrogen bonds).
  2. Goiter = iodine deficiency; anemia = iron deficiency; Wilson disease = copper.
  3. Monosaccharides general formula (CH₂O)ₙ; C:H:O = 1:2:1.
  4. Maltose = glucose+glucose; Lactose = glucose+galactose; Sucrose = glucose+fructose.
  5. Starch & glycogen = glucose storage (plant / animal); cellulose = plant cell walls.
  6. Peptide bond = covalent, formed by condensation (removes water).
  7. Enzymes, haemoglobin, antibodies = globular proteins; keratin, collagen = fibrous.
  8. DNA: double helix, deoxyribose, A-T-G-C; RNA: single strand, ribose, A-U-G-C.
  9. Phospholipid = hydrophilic head + hydrophobic tails → bilayer.
  10. Benedict's test = reducing sugars (brick-red); Biuret test = proteins (mauve/purple); Iodine = starch (blue-black).
