3.8 Nucleic Acids

◌ Not started
  • Made of phosphoric acid, sugars, and organic bases; the cell's principal information-carrying molecules.
  • Two types: DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid) and RNA (ribonucleic acid).
  • Both are polynucleotides made of nucleotides.
  • Genes = stretches of DNA that code for RNA and amino acids (proteins).

Nucleotides = three components: 1. A nitrogen-containing base 2. A pentose (5-carbon) sugar 3. A phosphate group

Nitrogenous bases: In DNA — Adenine (A), Thymine (T), Guanine (G), Cytosine (C). In RNA — A, G, C, Uracil (U) instead of thymine.

Pentose sugars: Ribose (C₅H₁₀O₅) in RNA; Deoxyribose (C₅H₁₀O₄) in DNA (no oxygen on the 2nd carbon).
