5.3 Arc Lengths, Perimeters and Areas of Segments and Sectors

◌ Not started

Arc length (Def 5.2): s = 2πr·θ/360° (θ in degrees) [or s=πd·θ/360°]. Chord length: l = 2r·sin(θ/2), where θ is the central angle.

Example: Central angle 150° in circle radius 6 cm → arc length = 2π·6·(150/360) = 5π cm. Example: θ=120°, r=8 → chord = 2·8·sin60° = 16·(√3/2) = 8√3 cm.

Segment (Def): the region bounded by a chord and an arc. - Perimeter of a segment = length of chord + length of arc.

Sector (Def): the region bounded by two radii and an arc. - Area of a sector = πr²·θ/360°. - Perimeter of a sector = 2r + arc length = 2r + 2πr·θ/360°.

Example: sector radius 6 cm, angle 210° → Area=π·36·(210/360)=21π cm²; Perimeter=12+2π·6·(210/360)=12+7π cm.

Area of a segment = area of the sector − area of the triangle: A = πr²·θ/360° − ½r²·sinθ.

Example: r=4, θ=60° → segment area = π·16·(1/6) − ½·16·sin60° = (8/3)π − 4√3.

✍️ Must-know: arc = 2πr·θ/360; sector area = πr²·θ/360; segment area = sector − triangle = πr²·θ/360 − ½r²sinθ. Chord = 2r·sin(θ/2).
