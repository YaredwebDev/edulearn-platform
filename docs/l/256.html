5.5 Immune System

◌ Not started

5.5.1 Innate (Non-specific) Immunity

  • Present from birth, immediate, non-specific.
  • Defenses: skin (physical barrier), mucous membranes, cilia, stomach acid (HCl), lysozyme in tears/saliva, phagocytes (neutrophils, macrophages) engulfing pathogens, inflammatory response, fever.

5.5.2 Adaptive (Acquired/Specific) Immunity

  • Develops after exposure; specific and has memory (basis of vaccination).
  • Two components:
  • Humoral immunity: mediated by B-lymphocytes (B cells) → produce antibodies (Y-shaped immunoglobulins) that neutralize antigens.
  • Cell-mediated immunity: mediated by T-lymphocytes (T cells) — helper T cells, cytotoxic (killer) T cells.
  • Antigens: foreign molecules that trigger an immune response.
  • Antibodies: proteins produced by B cells that bind specific antigens.

5.5.3 Cells of the Immune System

  • Lymphocytes (T and B cells): recognize and respond to antigens; memory cells provide long-term immunity.
  • Macrophages: large phagocytes that engulf pathogens and present antigens.
  • Dendritic cells: antigen-presenting cells.
  • Natural killer (NK) cells: kill infected/cancer cells.
  • Neutrophils: most abundant white cells; phagocytic.
  • Mast cells: release histamine (allergy/inflammation).

5.5.4 Immunity Types

  • Active immunity: body produces its own antibodies (natural infection or vaccination).
  • Passive immunity: antibodies transferred (maternal to baby via placenta/breast milk; or antiserum).

Vaccination: introducing a weakened/inactive pathogen (antigen) so the body produces antibodies and memory cells → protection later.

5.5.5 Immunological Disorders

  • Allergy: overreaction to harmless antigens (allergens) → histamine release (hay fever, asthma, hives).
  • Autoimmune diseases: immune system attacks self tissues (Type 1 diabetes, rheumatoid arthritis, multiple sclerosis, lupus).
  • Immunodeficiency: weakened immune system (HIV/AIDS) → opportunistic infections.

✍️ Must-know: Innate = non-specific, immediate, from birth. Adaptive = specific, memory-based (vaccination). Antibodies = Y-shaped immunoglobulins from B cells.
