7.5 Safety Rules Against Hazards of Nuclear Radiation

◌ Not started
  • Handle radioactive materials only with tongs/forceps, at arm's length pointing away, keep away from eyes.
  • Three ways to protect from radiation (TIME, DISTANCE, SHIELDING): 1. Time: minimise the exposure time → reduce the dose. 2. Distance: increase your distance from the source → dose drops dramatically. 3. Shielding: insert lead, concrete or water barriers between you and the source (γ/X-rays stopped by lead/concrete/water; radioactive materials stored under water or in lead/concrete-lined rooms; dentists use a lead blanket).
  • Nuclear safety areas: safe extraction/transport/storage/processing/disposal of fissile material; safety of power generators; safe control/management of weapons and radioactive materials; safe handling & use in industrial/medical/research contexts; disposal of waste; limits on exposure.
  • In the event of a nuclear accident and for reactor safety, follow authorities' guidelines (the three main safety guidelines relate to control rods/moderator/coolant and containment, shielding, and limiting dose).
