4.1 Introduction

◌ Not started

Every chemical reaction proceeds at its own speed. Rusting starts quickly but fruit ripening takes days; weathering of stone takes decades and plastic breakdown in the environment >100 years; combustion of gasoline or an explosion of gunpowder take only seconds.

  • Chemical kinetics = the area of chemistry concerned with reaction rates (the word "kinetic" suggests movement/change).
  • Rate of reaction = the change in concentration of a reactant or product per unit time.
