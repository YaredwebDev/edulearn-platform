4D.1 Word formation — opposites/antonyms

  • Antonyms are words of opposite meaning; some have direct opposites, some don't. Practise forming opposites with prefixes (deforestation → afforestation/reforestation, protected → unprotected, irreversible → reversible, intangible → tangible, revitalize → diminish/weaken). Give direct opposites: friend/enemy, gloomy/bright, guilty/innocent, horizontal/vertical, important/unimportant, increase/decrease, inner/outer.

4D.2 Prefixes & suffixes

  • Prefixes = added to the beginning of a word to change its meaning. Table: de- (from/down/reverse: decode) · dis- (not/reverse: disagree) · ex- (out of/away/lacking/former: exhale) · il-, im-, in- (not: illegal, impossible, inaction) · mis- (bad/wrong: mislead) · non- (not: nonfiction) · pre- (before: prefix, prehistory) · pro- (for/forward: proactive) · re- (again/back: react) · un- (not/opposite: undo) · also anti- (against), tri-, sub-, over-, with-.
  • Suffixes = added to the end; can change the part of speech or meaning. Frequent ones: -ed (past tense: hopped, loved) · -ing (present participle) · -ly (characteristic of / adverb) · -s/-es (plural) · -able/-ible (able to) · -ful, and -ness, -less, -ous.
  • Spelling rules: adding -ness/-ly usually doesn't change spelling (dark→darkness) except words ending in y → change y to i (ready→readily, happy→happiness). When suffix starts with a vowel, drop the silent e (care+ing→caring, use+able→usable) unless word ends in ce/ge and suffix begins with a/o (replace→replaceable, courage→courageous). When suffix starts with a consonant, keep the silent e (care+ful→careful) with exceptions (true+ly→truly, argue→argument). Word ending in consonant + y: change y to i before any suffix not starting with i (sunny→sunnier, but hurry→hurrying). Double the final consonant when a vowel-starting suffix follows a one-syllable word or a last-accented word ending in single vowel + single consonant (tan→tanning, regret→regretting; cancel→canceled, prefer→preferred).

4D.3 Phrasal verbs

  • A phrasal verb = main verb + particle/preposition (e.g. look for = search).
  • With look: look out (careful watch) · look over (examine) · look at · look around · look down on (regard as inferior) · look up (search for info; raise eyes) · look into (investigate) · look forward to (anticipate with pleasure).
  • With lock: lock out (prevent entry) · lock up (secure/confine).
