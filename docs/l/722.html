1.3 Vector Addition and Subtraction

◌ Not started

Key ideas: - Scalars and vectors can never be added together. - Two vectors can only be added if they are of the same nature (e.g., two forces, or two velocities — but not a force and a velocity). - Resultant vector: the single vector whose effect is the same as the individual vectors acting together.

Addition: R⃗ = A⃗ + B⃗

Subtraction: vector subtraction is the addition of a negative vector: A⃗ − B⃗ = A⃗ + (−B⃗) - The negative of a vector has the same magnitude but opposite direction ("flip" the vector).

✍️ Must-know: Subtraction of a vector = adding its negative. Vector subtraction is order-independent.
