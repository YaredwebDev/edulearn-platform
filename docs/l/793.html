5.5 Joints

◌ Not started
  • Joints = where two or more bones meet (with binding tissues), allowing movement.
  • Types (Table): 1. Ball and socket — round head in a cup; movement in all directions (shoulder, hip). 2. Hinge — opens/closes in one plane like a door (elbow, knee). 3. Condyloid — no rotation but allows movement (finger, jaw). 4. Pivot (rotary/trochoid) — one bone swivels in a ring (ulna-radius, neck). 5. Gliding (plane) — limited movement, surfaces slip over each other (wrist). 6. Saddle — back-and-forth & side-to-side, no rotation (thumb base).

✍️ Must-know (review answers): hinge joint = knee; hip joint = ball and socket; bone-strengthening mineral = calcium; two lower-arm bones = radius and ulna. Saddle joints are at the base of the thumb.
