Key vocabulary from the "HIV/AIDS" passage:

WordMeaningExample
estimatea rough calculation/judgementThe WHO estimates millions are affected.
optimistichopeful; expecting the bestThe doctor was optimistic about treatment.
curbedlimited/restrained/controlledNew drugs curbed the death rate.
sparkto trigger/cause (something to start)Awareness sparks prevention.
sufficeto be enoughA short explanation will suffice.
avertto prevent; to avoidEducation can avert the spread of disease.
susceptibleeasily affected/harmed (by)Sick people are susceptible to infection.
