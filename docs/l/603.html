7.5 Ratio of Areas of Similar Plane Figures

◌ Not started

Definition: If two figures are similar, the ratio of their areas equals the square of the ratio of their corresponding side lengths (scale factor²).

Example: Similar rectangles 1×2 and 3×6: side ratio 3, area ratio = 3² = 9. Indeed areas 2 and 18 → ratio 9.

Example: If triangles are similar with corresponding sides 6 and 10, area ratio = (10/6)² = 25/9.

✍️ Must-know: Perimeter ratio = k; Area ratio = k².
