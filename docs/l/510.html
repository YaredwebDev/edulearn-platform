8.2 Vocabulary of the Unit (matric-ready)

◌ Not started

Key vocabulary from the "HIV/AIDS" passage:

Word Meaning Example
estimate a rough calculation/judgement The WHO estimates millions are affected.
optimistic hopeful; expecting the best The doctor was optimistic about treatment.
curbed limited/restrained/controlled New drugs curbed the death rate.
spark to trigger/cause (something to start) Awareness sparks prevention.
suffice to be enough A short explanation will suffice.
avert to prevent; to avoid Education can avert the spread of disease.
susceptible easily affected/harmed (by) Sick people are susceptible to infection.

✍️ Must-know: avert = prevent/avoid; curbed = restrained/controlled; susceptible = vulnerable; optimistic = hopeful (opposite: pessimistic).
