UNIT 6 · LESSON 8 OF 8 · GRADE 11 PHYSICS

🧮 Key facts to memorise

◌ Not started
  • Like charges repel, unlike attract; q = ne, e = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C.
  • Coulomb: F = kq1q2/r², k ≈ 8.99×10⁹ N·m²/C².
  • E = F/q = kq/r²; field lines from + to −, never cross, ⊥ to surface; uniform between plates.
  • Flux Φ = EA cosθ.
  • V = kq/r (point); ΔV = W/q; V = Ed in a uniform field; equipotentials ⊥ field, no work along one.
  • Current I = Q/t = nAv_de (A); Ohm's law V = IR.
  • Series: R_s = ΣR; parallel: 1/R_p = Σ1/R.
  • Power P = VI = I²R = V²/R; terminal V = ε − Ir.
  • Wheatstone bridge balance: R1/R2 = R3/R4.
  • Kirchhoff: junction ΣI_in = ΣI_out; loop ΣV = 0.
  • Capacitors: C = q/V; C = κε₀A/d; farad.
  • Parallel capacitors: C_eq = ΣC; series: 1/C_eq = Σ1/C (opposite of resistors).
  • Dielectric increases capacitance, decreases E and V (isolated).
  • Ammeter in series (low R); voltmeter in parallel (high R).
  • Bulbs in parallel brighter than in series.
  • Resistor colour code: bands→digits/multiplier/tolerance.

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) state properties of charge and compute number of electrons/protons for a given charge; (2) apply Coulomb's law and the superposition principle; (3) sketch/interpret field lines and compute E = kq/r² and flux Φ = EA cosθ; (4) compute potential V = kq/r, potential difference ΔV = W/q = Ed, and reason about equipotentials; (5) apply I = Q/t, current density, drift velocity, and Ohm's law V = IR; (6) combine resistors in series/parallel and compute equivalent resistance, currents and voltage drops; (7) explain ammeter vs voltmeter connections and galvanometer-to-ammeter/voltmeter conversions; (8) apply the Wheatstone-bridge balance and potentiometer ideas; (9) compare bulb brightness; (10) apply Kirchhoff's junction and loop rules to multi-loop circuits and handle internal resistance (V = ε − Ir); (11) compute capacitance, capacitor combinations (series/parallel) and effects of dielectrics; and (12) read a resistor colour code and relate circuits to daily life? Re-answer the end-of-unit questions (new force when separation doubles, units of electric field, effect of series/parallel connections on length/thickness/resistivity, largest/smallest resistance, ten 75 Ω resistors, bulb brightness, emf vs terminal voltage, ε and r from two measurements, currents in a circuit).
