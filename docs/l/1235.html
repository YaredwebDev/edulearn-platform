📚 Unit 2 Summary (Key Facts)

  • Scale = set of numbers used to measure/compare; four types: nominal, ordinal, interval, ratio (most physics scales are ratio scales).
  • Measurement = comparison of an unknown quantity with a known standard unit.
  • Prefixes simplify very big/small quantities (tera → pico).
  • Seven fundamental quantities; all others are derived.
  • Meter, second, kilogram, Kelvin, Ampere, mole, candela = fundamental units.
  • Physical quantities are scalars (magnitude only) or vectors (magnitude + direction).
  • SI units convert to non-SI units and vice versa.
  • Lab safety rules; three key elements of measurement.
