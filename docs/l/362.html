4.1 Reading: Tourism (the core "story"/text)

◌ Not started

The passage has four paragraphs and argues that tourism is vital for developing countries.

Paragraph 1 — Definition & importance: - Tourism = the sum total of relationships and interactions of tourists with business suppliers, guides, hosting communities and hospitality workers in attracting, transporting, hosting and managing tourists. - Tourists = people who travel to and stay in places outside their environment for more than twenty-four hours. - One of the largest, most rapidly growing industries; contributes to multidirectional social & environmental development. - Developing countries whose economy depends on exporting agricultural products (limited hard currency) give it attention; the World Tourism Organization (WTO) provides technical & professional support.

Paragraph 2 — Reasons tourism is vital (economic): 1. If incorporated into development strategies, tourism can be a cornerstone of new productive systems that reduce poverty and achieve economic growth. 2. Provides a wide range of employment from professionals to unskilled workers; stimulates productivity and creates jobs. 3. Unlike other sectors, tourism spreads employment geographically — not only to cities but to rural areas where attractions are located.

Paragraph 3 — More benefits: - Promotes export / earns hard currency (foreign exchange) — essential for development. Tourists bring their own money for consumables → generates income/revenue. - Community-based tourism: local (often rural, poor) people manufacture local products (souvenirs) and sell to tourists → additional income used for the community's benefit. - Cultural exchange: tourists study local languages/cultures and share their own; some marry local people (strong social ties/kinship).

Paragraph 4 — What hosting countries must provide: 1. Documents/information about sites. 2. Professionals with deep knowledge of the places and good proficiency in the tourists' language. 3. Quality accommodations (prepared food, neat rooms, security, courteous waiters/waitresses). 4. Modern roads comfortable to drive in all weather. 5. Security — no pick-pockets, brigands, bandits, or armed groups; peace is the responsibility of a non-fragile government.

✍️ Must-know: Tourism = interaction between tourists and the hosting community; definition of "tourist" (stays >24h); the 3 streams (economic, socio-cultural, environmental); benefits = employment, hard currency/export, community income, cultural exchange; hosting-country requirements = info, professionals, accommodation, roads, security.
