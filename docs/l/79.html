UNIT 12 · LESSON 7 OF 7 · GRADE 11 AGRICULTURE

🧮 Key facts to memorise

◌ Not started
  • Biodiversity components: species number, genetic diversity, population, habitat/landscape diversity.
  • ~29 endemic mammals & 18 endemic birds in Ethiopia; ~85% of population rural & resource-dependent.
  • Values: provisioning (food, fibre, medicine), regulatory (climate, pest control, pollination, purification, erosion control, waste decomposition), cultural (recreation, foreign currency, research).
  • Six threats: land-use change, unsustainable use, invasive species, climate change, pollution, population growth.
  • Invasive species in Ethiopia: Parthenium weed, Prosopis juliflora (Awash Valley), water hyacinth (Awash basin & Lake Tana).
  • Lake Haramaya disappeared from over-exploitation (restoring); Hagenia abyssinica threatened by timber overharvest.
  • Conservation = planned management; objectives: sustainable use, life-supporting systems, essential ecological processes.
  • Endangered animals: Walia Ibex, Mountain Nyala, Ethiopian Wolf.
  • Protected areas: national/regional parks, wildlife sanctuaries & reserves, botanical gardens & herbariums, controlled hunting centres.
  • Review answers: objectives of biodiversity conservation = sustainable use AND functioning ecological processes (C, both A and B); a regulatory service = All (D); major threats in Ethiopia = All (D); threatened by habitat destruction = Ethiopian Wolf & Walia Ibex (C, both A and B); biodiversity = range of different species of plants, animals, microorganisms (D, all).

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) define biodiversity and list its components; (2) name biodiversity resources in your locality and their uses; (3) describe the services/values of biodiversity with examples that affect humans; (4) describe the major threats (esp. for Ethiopia with examples like invasive species, overgrazing, deforestation, Lake Haramaya); and (5) justify conservation and describe Ethiopia's status, endemic counts, endangered animals, efforts & protected areas?
