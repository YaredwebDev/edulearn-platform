UNIT 5 · LESSON 7 OF 7 · GRADE 11 BIOLOGY

🧮 Key facts/numbers to memorise

◌ Not started
  • ~700 muscles (~half body weight); skeletal muscle = 30–40% of body mass.
  • 206 adult bones (born >300); axial = 80, appendicular = 126 (80 + 126 = 206).
  • Menstrual cycle ≈ 28 days (range 20–45): menstrual 1–4, follicular 5–13, ovulation 14, luteal 15–28.
  • Pregnancy ≈ 40 weeks (280 days); implantation ~6 days, completed 9–10 days.
  • Post-pill (ECP) works within 72 h; implant lasts ~3 years; vasectomy 0.15%, tubal ligation 0.04% failure.
  • FGM = 73% of harmful traditional practices in Ethiopia; ~218 million women lack family planning.
  • Alcohol euphoria limit 0.1%; ~6.2% (males) & 1.1% (females) of deaths globally.
  • HIV stages: acute 2–4 wk, chronic 1–7 yr, AIDS 10+ yr; AIDS CD4 <200, healthy 500–1600/mm³.

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) name the three muscle types and their features; (2) explain actin–myosin contraction and the 3 contraction types; (3) give axial vs appendicular bone counts and joint types; (4) list male & female reproductive parts and functions; (5) describe spermatogenesis & oogenesis; (6) walk through the menstrual-cycle phases and LH/FSH positive/negative feedback; (7) describe fertilization, implantation and pregnancy signs; (8) compare barrier vs non-barrier contraceptives incl. the IUD, pills, sterilization; (9) list causes of male & female infertility; (10) identify STIs by pathogen (bacteria/virus/parasite) and the HIV stages; (11) define harmful traditional practices incl. FGM; (12) explain family planning & its services; and (13) describe the effects of alcohol, khat, marijuana/cocaine/heroin and inhalants? Re-answer the review too (saddle joint at thumb base; radius & ulna in lower arm; knee is a hinge; ligament bone-to-bone; uterus NOT male; sperm duct NOT female; gestation 40 weeks; syphilis affects brain & heart; gulping alcohol → coma/death).
