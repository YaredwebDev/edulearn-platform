12.3 Values and Services of Biodiversity

◌ Not started

Biodiversity provides many values/services: - Provisioning (products): food production (cropping, livestock, food from wild plants/animals); firewood, fibre & construction materials; medicinal resources (plants & animals as medicine sources). - Regulatory services: climate regulation (soil & forests store large amounts of carbon → prevent release → regulate climate); disease & pest control (natural predators control pests); pollination (bees & insects assist seed dispersal & plant reproduction); purification of drinking water & air (organisms decompose/detoxify toxic substances); soil-erosion control by forests; waste decomposition (organisms decompose wastes, enhance nutrient cycling, prevent waste accumulation). - Cultural services: recreation, sports, hunting; source of foreign currency; education & research.
