4.2 Basic Trigonometric Functions

For a right triangle with angle α:

  • sinα = opposite/hypotenuse = a/h
  • cosα = adjacent/hypotenuse = b/h
  • tanα = opposite/adjacent = a/b

For an angle θ in standard position with point P(x,y) on its terminal side and r=√(x²+y²):

  • sinθ = y/r, cosθ = x/r, tanθ = y/x.
  • cscθ = r/y, secθ = r/x, cotθ = x/y (reciprocals).

Example: P(5,12): r=√(25+144)=13 → sinθ=12/13, cosθ=5/13, tanθ=12/5.

✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: sin=opp/hyp, cos=adj/hyp, tan=opp/adj. In coordinate form, sin=y/r, cos=x/r, tan=y/x. r=√(x²+y²).

The unit circle

The unit circle has radius 1 centered at the origin. For a point P(a,b) on it with central angle x: cos x = a, sin x = b, so (a,b)=(cos x, sin x).

  • Since a²+b²=1: cos²x + sin²x = 1.
Unit circle with cosθ and sinθ

Quadrantal angles (multiples of π/2):

cos0=1,sin0=0; cos(π/2)=0,sin(π/2)=1; cosπ=−1,sinπ=0; cos(3π/2)=0,sin(3π/2)=−1; cos2π=1,sin2π=0.

  • Periodicity: sin(x+2πn)=sin x, cos(x+2πn)=cos x (n integer).
  • sin x=0 when x=nπ; cos x=0 when x=(2n+1)π/2.
  • sin(−x)=−sin x; cos(−x)=cos x; tan(−x)=−tan x.

Signs in quadrants (Table 4.3):

IIIIIIIV
sin++−−
cos+−−+
tan+−+−

Example: sin x>0 and cos x<0 → second quadrant.

Reciprocal functions: cscθ=1/sinθ, secθ=1/cosθ, cotθ=1/tanθ.

  • sec30°=1/cos30°=2/√3. sec60°+csc30°=2+2=4.
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Memorize the quadrant signs and the standard values. sin²x+cos²x=1 always. sin/cos are periodic with 2π.

4.2.2 Trigonometric values of angles

Complementary angles: sum to 90°. Complement of x is (90°−x).

  • sin(π/2−α)=cosα; cos(π/2−α)=sinα; tan(π/2−α)=cotα; cot(π/2−α)=tanα.

Example: sin54°=cos36°; if sin3A=cos(A−26°), then 90°−3A=A−26° → 4A=116° → A=29°.

Reference angle (θ_R): the positive acute angle between the terminal side and the x-axis.

  • QI: θ_R=θ; QII: θ_R=180°−θ; QIII: θ_R=θ−180°; QIV: θ_R=360°−θ.
  • The value of a trig function of θ equals ±the value at θ_R (sign by quadrant).

Example: θ=155° (QII): θ_R=25°, sin155°=sin25°, cos155°=−cos25°, tan155°=−tan25°.

Reference angle diagram

Supplementary angles: sum to 180°. sinθ=sin(180°−θ), cosθ=−cos(180°−θ), tanθ=−tan(180°−θ).

Co-terminal angles: share the same terminal side; differ by multiples of 360° (or 2π).

  • Degrees: θ±360n; radians: θ±2nπ.
  • Co-terminal angles have the same trig values.

Example: co-terminal of 30°: 390° (n=1), −690° (n=−2). Co-terminal of π/4: π/4−2π=−7π/4.

Example: sin780°: 780°=2×360°+60° → sin780°=sin60°=√3/2.

✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Reference angle: reduce to the acute angle with the x-axis; apply the sign from the quadrant. Co-terminal: add/subtract 360° (or 2π) any number of times.

Graphs of sine, cosine and tangent

Periodic function: repeats its values at regular intervals. The period is the repeating interval.

Sine y=sinθ: period 360° (2π), domain ℝ, range [−1,1]. Max 1 at π/2, min −1 at 3π/2.

Graph of y=sinθ

Cosine y=cosθ: period 360° (2π), domain ℝ, range [−1,1]. Max 1 at 0 and 2π, min −1 at π.

Graph of y=cosθ

Tangent y=tanθ: period 180° (π), domain {θ≠nπ/2, n odd}, range ℝ. Undefined at odd multiples of π/2 (vertical asymptotes).

Graph of y=tanθ
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: sin & cos: period 2π, range [−1,1]. tan: period π, range ℝ, undefined at odd multiples of π/2. Amplitude (for sin/cos) = max deviation from axis.
