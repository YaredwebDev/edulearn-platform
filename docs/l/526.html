UNIT 10 · LESSON 7 OF 7 · GRADE 9 ENGLISH

📝 Writing focus: Expository Essay on the Uses of the Internet

◌ Not started

The unit gives a diagram of internet uses — video conference, online shopping, file sharing, online banking/communication — and a sample essay ("Importance of the Internet") that defines the internet and describes its role in communication.

Structure your essay: 1. Introduction — the internet is now essential in everyday life. 2. Body — describe each use (communication, information, video conference, online shopping, file sharing). 3. Conclusion — the internet is a great resource if used wisely.

Sentence-level opinion practice: For each statement, write a polite agreement or disagreement paragraph ("Internet use is important to our country. I think it is important..." / "Many people use social media dishonestly. In my opinion...").

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) list the advantages and disadvantages of computers/the internet, (2) define all 10 passage vocabulary words (including boon/bane), (3) use phrasal verbs with cut/do/dress, (4) form and choose among the four tenses, (5) read the social-media bar graph and state the highest/lowest values, (6) express opinions politely, agreeing/disagreeing, (7) write an expository essay on the uses of the internet?
