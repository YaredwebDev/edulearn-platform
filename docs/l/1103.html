5.3 Grammar: Conditionals (continued)

5.3.1 The Second Conditional (unreal / unlikely present)

If + past (were), + would/could/might + V1.

  • "If I had a garden, I would grow vegetables."
  • "If I were you, I would relax more."

Used for imaginary/hypothetical situations that are unlikely or not true now.

5.3.2 The Third Conditional (impossible past)

If + had + V3, + would/could/might + have + V3.

  • "If she had watered the plants, they would not have died."
  • "If he had rested, he would have recovered sooner."

Used to talk about imaginary past — situations that did not happen.

Note: ✋ Trap — "I were": In second conditionals use were for all subjects: "If I were rich…" (not "if I was"). Third conditional expresses regret/speculation about the past — the condition is impossible to change.

📝 Writing focus: Horticulture Paragraph

Write a paragraph describing the benefits of gardening/horticulture. Structure:

  1. Topic sentence (gardening improves health).
  2. Supporting sentences (relieves stress, gives exercise, provides food).
  3. Concluding sentence (therefore, gardening is valuable).
Note: ✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) list the health benefits of gardening, (2) define all 6+ vocabulary words and use the prefix ex-, (3) write second and third conditionals correctly and explain their difference, (4) write a well-structured horticulture paragraph?
