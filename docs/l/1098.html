4.4 Word Formation — Prefixes

Prefixes are added to the beginning of a word to change its meaning.

PrefixMeaningExamples
ante-beforeantenatal, anteroom
anti-againstanti-aging, antibiotic
en-make/cause to be; inenable, enlarge, enjoy
dis-not / oppositedisagree, disappear, dislike
un-notunhappy, unable, unclear
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: un- and dis- usually make opposites (unhappy, disagree). anti- means against (anti-social). en- means "to cause to become" (enable = make able). Recognise the prefix to guess the meaning of a new word.
