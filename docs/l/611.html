8.5 Applications of Vectors in Two Dimensions

◌ Not started

Example 1 (displacement): A boat moves 8 km South then 8 km West. Resultant displacement AC = √(8²+8²) = 8√2 km, in direction S 45° W.

Example 2 (rectangle midpoints): ABCD rectangle, E, F, G, H are midpoints. If AE→ = a and AH→ = b, then AF→ = 2a + b, BD→ = 2b − 2a, EC→ = a − 2b. Since BD→ = 2(b−a) = 2 EH→, BD→ is parallel to EH→.

Example 3 (forces): Two perpendicular forces 10 N and 10√3 N. Resultant |R| = √(10² + (10√3)²) = √(100+300) = √400 = 20 N. The third force that counteracts them is 20 N in the opposite direction.

✍️ Must-know: For perpendicular vectors, resultant |R| = √(|F₁|² + |F₂|²). Use triangle/parallelogram law for non-perpendicular ones.
