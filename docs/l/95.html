15.4 Food and Nutrition Security

◌ Not started
  • Food security (UN) = when all people, at all times, have physical & economic access to sufficient, safe, nutritious food that meets dietary needs & food preferences for an active, healthy life. A basic definition = access to sufficient quantity of food.
  • Nutrition security = having access to sufficient, safe & nutritious food, safe water & adequate sanitation, ability to access health-care services, and knowledge of sound household/community practices in childcare, food storage/preparation & hygiene. Nutrition security involves access to nutritious food.
  • Four pillars of food & nutrition security: availability, access, utilization, stability.
  • Availability = having sufficient quantities of appropriate food (physical existence); from own production or market.
  • Access = adequate income/resources to obtain food in sufficient quantity, quality & diversity (depends on household resources & prices).
  • Utilization/consumption = adequate dietary intake + ability to absorb & use nutrients in the body.
  • Stability = access to adequate food at all times (constant supply during the year & long-term).
  • Drivers of food insecurity (people lack access to basic good nutrition and often eat a nutrient-poor diet): lack of resources (financial/other); no access to affordable nutritious food; geographical isolation; lack of motivation or knowledge about nutritious diet; no clean drinking water; poor environmental hygiene; poor health infrastructure.
