4.3 Constraints in Animal Production and Their Mitigation Strategies

◌ Not started
  • Feed shortage: inadequate quantity & quality is the major constraint. Natural pasture & crop residues supply >90% of annual feed but have poor value (crude protein only 3–6%, low energy/minerals/digestibility); big seasonal variation (dry-season shortage). Agro-industrial by-products (wheat bran, oil-seed cakes, molasses) little used (unavailable, costly, low awareness). Mitigation: expand commercial feed production (improved grasses, legumes, formulated feeds); feed young succulent grass (at ~30% flowering), mix mature grass with legumes, chop mature grass + mix with molasses.
  • Genetic factors: traditional system uses local breeds (well-adapted, disease-resistant, produce on low-quality feed, quality yolk/milk-fat/meat taste) but considered low productivity. Mitigation: proper feeding, selecting good producers, crossing with exotic breeds.
  • Animal diseases & parasites: diseases reduce efficiency and hamper export; internal/external parasites add burden. Mitigation: proper feeding, vaccination, isolation of sick animals, treating sick animals, proper cleaning & housing management.
  • Traditional production system / poor knowledge: draft animals central in mixed farming; milk the focus in pastoral farming (meat seen as by-product); pastoralists treat livestock as capital accumulation, selling only when needed/feed&water shortage — absence of entrepreneurial awareness. Mitigation: recurrent capacity building, demonstrating modern farming, credit facilities, strong extension system.
  • Socio-economic constraints: lack of vital services/facilities — weak extension, shortage of technology, poor infrastructure, electric-power scarcity (needed to preserve meat, milk, drugs, vaccines), poor transport (hampers livestock/product flow), lack of credit/savings, little training, inadequate market information. Social constraints: taboos (e.g. not eating goat meat or fish in some areas; cross-breed products not preferred in certain communities).

✍️ Must-know (constraints list): feed shortage; genetic factors; animal diseases & parasites; farmer's poor knowledge / traditional system; socio-economic constraints (poor infrastructure, weak extension, power & transport scarcity, no credit); water shortage; socio-cultural taboos. Prevention of disease = feeding + vaccination + isolation + treatment + hygiene.
