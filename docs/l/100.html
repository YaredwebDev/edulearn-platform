16.1 Importance of Diversified Food Production

◌ Not started
  • Agriculture is indispensable for food & nutrition security.
  • Agricultural food diversification = the act of introducing or producing a variety of food from agricultural activities. Different food types supply different needed nutrients:
  • Children need variety to grow & develop; adults need variety for energy & productivity; pregnant/breastfeeding women need different foods for the baby & themselves.
  • Diversifying what a household grows improves dietary diversity & nutrition outcomes. A diverse diet is the best way to ensure people get all nutrients they need.
  • Integrated farming systems involve several subsystems (crops, animals, fish) whose synergistic interactions have a greater total effect than the sum of individual effects on nutrition.
  • It is recommended to consume at least four food groups in each meal. Improved nutrition/health → higher individual productivity → better household production & income.

✍️ Key term: integrated farming system = a sustainable system integrating livestock, crop production, fish, poultry, tree/plantation crops and other systems that benefit each other; based on the concept "there is no waste".
