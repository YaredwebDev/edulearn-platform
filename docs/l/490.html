4.4 Word Formation — Prefixes

◌ Not started

Prefixes are added to the beginning of a word to change its meaning.

Prefix Meaning Examples
ante- before antenatal, anteroom
anti- against anti-aging, antibiotic
en- make/cause to be; in enable, enlarge, enjoy
dis- not / opposite disagree, disappear, dislike
un- not unhappy, unable, unclear

✍️ Must-know: un- and dis- usually make opposites (unhappy, disagree). anti- means against (anti-social). en- means "to cause to become" (enable = make able). Recognise the prefix to guess the meaning of a new word.
