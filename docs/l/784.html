10.7 Environmental impact of mineral exploitation

◌ Not started
  • Minerals (gold, bronze, iron ore, aluminium) contribute to national economic growth & provide raw materials for industry.
  • Environmental problems: surface excavation releases dust (air pollution); leftover/waste materials pollute soils & water; separation uses water & chemicals; heavy metals and radionuclides (radiation-emitting elements like uranium) pollute water; air pollution causes human & animal health problems; water & soil pollution harm soil, aquatic organisms, and humans using polluted water.
