6.5 Data Table / Graph Reading

The unit presents a table of extreme-poverty rates (%) in East African countries for 2020 and 2021. The table is a data-reading exercise: compare countries and the two years. Below is the same data drawn as a bar graph for easy reading.

Extreme poverty rate in East African countries, 2020–2021

How to read such a graph/table:

  1. Read the title and the labels (what is measured, units = %).
  2. Compare bars or rows: which country is highest/lowest? Which year is worse?
  3. State a trend: e.g. "Extreme poverty fell slightly in most countries from 2020 to 2021."
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know (example readings): Poverty rates decreased from 2020 to 2021 in most listed countries (e.g. Ethiopia fell from ~17.3% to ~15.9%). Burundi had the highest extreme-poverty rate, while Comoros had the lowest of the group. Being able to read percentages and compare values is a testable skill.
