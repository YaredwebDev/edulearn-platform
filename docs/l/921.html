5.1 What is Food?

  • Food: any beneficial substance eaten, drunk, or otherwise taken into the body to sustain life, provide energy, promote growth, etc. Usually of plant, animal, or fungal origin and contains essential nutrients (carbohydrates, fats, proteins, vitamins, minerals).

Importance of food in living things:

  1. Growth: source of substances for making new cells, tissues, and organs.
  2. Energy: fuel for all biological activities (running, jumping, growing, reproducing).
  3. Replacement of damaged tissues: making new cells and repairing damaged ones.
  4. Protection from diseases: prevents deficiency diseases.
