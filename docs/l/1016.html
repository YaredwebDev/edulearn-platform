2.3 Phrasal Verbs (fill / fix / flip / float / follow / fool / freak / find)

Phrasal verbMeaningExample
fill incomplete (a form); give detailsPlease fill in this application form.
fill outcomplete (a form)Fill out the registration card.
fill upmake completely fullFill up the tank with petrol.
fix uprepair; arrange; decorateWe fixed up the old house.
flip outbecome very angry/excitedHe flipped out when he lost his phone.
float aroundbe circulating looselyA rumour is floating around the office.
follow uptake further action; pursueLet's follow up the complaint.
fool aroundwaste time; behave playfullyStop fooling around and work.
freak outpanic / become very upsetShe freaked out at the exam.
find outdiscover (a fact)I want to find out the truth.
Note: ✋ Trap: "fill in" and "fill out" both mean complete a form (mostly interchangeable). "fill up" = make full. "find out" = discover; "follow up" = continue after initial action.
