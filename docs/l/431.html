4A Listening Skills: Care for Natural Resources

◌ Not started
  • Factors influencing natural resources in Ethiopia: all of the above — overpopulation, urbanization/rapid urbanization, agricultural expansion, migration, resettlement, climate change, environmental pollution. (The huge population puts a great burden on resources.)
  • The various degradations (land, water, forest, rangeland, wildlife) are interconnected and "feed off each other", forming a vicious circle: severe soil loss, low vegetation cover, unsustainable farming, use of dung & crop residues for fuel, overgrazing, wildlife destruction/migration → further degradation → unsustainable environmental consequences such as water-quality deterioration, biodiversity decline, loss of ecosystem services.
  • Comprehension point: statements about Ethiopian governments being "extremely successful" in soil/water conservation & tree planting should be judged false (they have been unsuccessful / faced challenges per the text). Words to use: degradation, amplified, population, interconnected, burden, sustainability, deterioration, unsustainable.
