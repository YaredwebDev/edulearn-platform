4.1 The Concept of Force

◌ Not started
  • Force: a push or a pull exerted on a body that changes the state of motion of the body. Force can change the velocity of a body or cause deformation (change its shape or size). A vector quantity (represented by ⃗F or bold F). SI unit newton (N).
  • Any time the state of motion of an object changes, a force has been applied.
  • Examples: friction force, normal force, the force of gravity.

Contact vs Non-contact forces: - Contact forces: involve physical contact between objects (e.g., applied forces, normal forces, frictional forces, spring forces). - Non-contact forces: do not involve physical contact (e.g., magnetic force, gravitational force).

Measuring force: most conveniently done using the deformation of a spring — the spring elongates when force is applied and a pointer reads the value.

Weight (W): the magnitude of the gravitational force acting on a body, measured in newtons, directed towards the center of the Earth.

✍️ Must-know: Force = vector (push/pull that changes motion or shape). Contact forces touch; non-contact forces don't. Weight = gravitational force (N).
