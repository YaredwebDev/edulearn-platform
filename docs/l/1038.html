5.4 Phrasal Verbs: get, give, go

  • get together — meet socially; get up — rise from bed.
  • give away — distribute for free; reveal a secret.
  • give in — surrender; yield: "The school managements are giving in."
  • give out — distribute; run out.
  • give up — stop trying; quit a habit: "I gave up smoking."
  • go about — start/approach a task; go after — pursue; go ahead — proceed: "they support students to go ahead with the tradition"; go along with — agree with.
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Unit 5 list: get together, get up, give away, give in, give out, give up, go about, go after, go ahead, go along with. In the reading, "they are giving in" = yielding/relenting; "go ahead" = continue.
