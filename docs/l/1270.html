7.2 Extreme Temperature Safety

  • Normal body temperature: between 97 °F (36.56 °C) and 99 °F (37.2 °C); average 98.6 °F (37 °C). To maintain this without devices, surroundings need to be about 82 °F (28 °C).
  • Heat risks:
  • 90–105 °F (32–40 °C): heat cramps and exhaustion.
  • 105–130 °F (40–54 °C): heat exhaustion more likely — limit activity.
  • Over 130 °F (54 °C): heat stroke.
  • Safety precautions: stay well-hydrated; wear appropriate clothing; wear sunscreen; avoid extremely hot places.
