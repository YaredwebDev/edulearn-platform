16.4 Indigenous Knowledge in Nutrition-Sensitive Agriculture

◌ Not started
  • Ethiopian farmers run mixed crop/livestock farming systems, producing plant- & animal-based foods for household use & market. Organic vegetable & fruit production is becoming common.
  • Area-specific traditional foods:
  • Chuko — common in most of Oromia; made from barley flour mixed with concentrated butter; a very rich source of essential nutrients.
  • Enset (in many forms, e.g. Kocho) — mainly in southern Ethiopia. Such nutrient-rich traditional foods are luxurious, often used to complement staples or during ceremonies.
  • Wild plants can contribute to diversified food & nutrition security. Examples rich in valuable minerals: bamboo shoot (Oxytenanthera abyssinica L.), stinging nettle (Urtica simensis L.), wild mushroom species — found in north-western Ethiopia, central highlands & south-western Ethiopia, consumed by local communities. Domesticating these edible wild plants in appropriate agro-ecologies and promoting their consumption can improve food diversification & nutritional security.
