9.2 Vocabulary of the Unit (matric-ready)

◌ Not started

Vocabulary taken directly from the reading passage (with the paragraph references from the book):

Word Meaning Example (context)
scheme (¶1) a system or plan (here, a system of law) Justice is a scheme of law in which each person gets their due.
domain (¶2) an area/field of concern Equal funding is under the domain of equality.
sprinting (¶5) running fast over a short distance The runners were sprinting around the track.
stagger (¶5) to arrange so things are not all at the same point We should stagger the starting positions.
invoked (¶5) called upon / appealed to Justice is invoked in social and political movements.
polity (¶5) a form of government / organised society A good polity must have several virtues.
curse (¶5) a source of misfortune/misery Being poor in a material world can be a curse.
allegedly (¶6) supposedly; claimed to be (but not proven) A crime was allegedly committed.
perpetrator (¶6) the person who commits a crime The perpetrator is allowed a trial by jury.
discern (¶6) to see/understand clearly The jury must discern what events took place.
