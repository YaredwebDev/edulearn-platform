📚 Unit 6 Summary (Key Facts)

  • Oscillation/periodic motion = to-and-fro motion about an equilibrium position.
  • Wave = disturbance that propagates carrying energy.
  • Wave characteristics: period, frequency, amplitude, wavelength, wave speed.
  • Mechanical waves need a medium; EM waves don't.
  • Transverse (perpendicular) vs longitudinal (parallel) waves.
  • Sound = longitudinal; needs a medium; speed varies by medium and temperature.
  • Superposition/interference; standing waves (nodes & antinodes).
  • Sound characteristics: pitch (frequency), loudness (amplitude, dB), timbre (quality).
