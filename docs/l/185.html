3.5 Animal and Plant Cells

◌ Not started

Similarities: cell membrane, nucleus, cytoplasm, mitochondria, Golgi apparatus.

Differences: - Cell wall, chloroplast, and large permanent vacuoles are found in plant cells. - Centriole and lysosome are found in animal cells. - Plant cells are usually larger and more easily visible under a light microscope. - The plant vacuole contains a solution of pigments, enzymes, sugars, organic compounds, mineral salts, oxygen, and carbon dioxide; helps regulate osmotic properties. - Chloroplasts are in the green parts of the plant, mainly leaves.
