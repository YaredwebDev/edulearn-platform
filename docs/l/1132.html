10.5 Data Table / Graph Reading

The unit shows the social media status of Ethiopia in 2021 (as a percentage):

Social media%
Twitter39.38
Facebook37.34
YouTube10.74

The same data drawn as a bar graph for easier reading:

Social media status of Ethiopia, 2021

How to read/answer:

  1. Read the title and the labels (platforms, %).
  2. Identify the highest (Twitter ~39%) and lowest (YouTube ~11%) values.
  3. Comment on the spread: Twitter and Facebook dominate, YouTube trails.
  4. Discuss what social media should do to benefit Ethiopians and how internet use can be improved (speaking/discussion questions in the unit).
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Learn the platforms and their percentages so you can interpret the graph in an exam and discuss Ethiopia's social-media landscape.
