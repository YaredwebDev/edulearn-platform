6.1 Introduction to Animal Genetics and Breeding

◌ Not started
  • Genetics = the study of heredity — the passing of genetic information & traits from parents to offspring. Includes the study of genes and their effects.
  • Genes = small sections of DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid) coded for specific traits. DNA is the molecule containing the genetic code, inherited by offspring; genes are found in chromosomes.
  • Elements within genes can enhance growth, health and nutrient-use efficiency → increase production and reduce environmental impact.
  • Animal genetics is one of the pillars of livestock development, alongside animal health, animal nutrition and husbandry issues (e.g., housing).
  • Animal breeding = mating selected animals to enhance particular characteristics in subsequent generations, usually to increase production & profitability; accumulates superior genes and eliminates less-desirable ones.
  • Objectives of animal breeding: increase animal yield/productivity; improve the desirable quality of animal products; produce disease-resistant animals.
