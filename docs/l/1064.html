8.7 Apologising (functional language)

Making apologies: I do apologize for…; I must apologize for…; I'd like to apologize for…; I'm so sorry for…; I shouldn't have…; It's all my fault; I'm ashamed of…; Please forgive me for…; I'm terribly sorry for…

Accepting apologies: That's all right; Never mind; Don't apologize; It doesn't matter; Don't worry about it; Don't mention it; That's OK; I quite understand; You couldn't help it; Forget about it; No harm done.

  • An apology = recognise you were wrong + take full responsibility + (often) promise to do better.
Note: ✋ Trap (multiple-choice): "I'm so sorry I'm late" (not "apologize"). "Please forgive me for interrupting" (forgive + person). "I would like to apologize for my fault." "Please accept my sincere apologies."

📝 Writing focus

  • Practise punctuating short paragraphs (capital letters, commas, full stops) — two sample texts in the unit (travel/transport & pollution).
  • Write a paragraph describing the process of using a traditional medicine or the value of plants.
  • Interpret a bar graph / pie chart.
  • Practise apologising in dialogues.
Note: ✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) give the main idea of each Moringa paragraph, (2) define the vocabulary, (3) explain compounding vs back-formation, (4) use the phrasal verbs, (5) choose much/many/little/few, (6) build clauses of reason & manner, (7) form the present perfect with its uses, (8) describe a graph, (9) apologise and accept apologies?
