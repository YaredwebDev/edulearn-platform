6.7 Electric Circuits in Our Surroundings

◌ Not started
  • Home electrical installations (household wiring); classifying appliances by power source (mains-electric, battery, heat powered). Visit/learn about the Addis Ababa Science Museum smart house as an example.
  • Resistor colour code: the first two bands give digits, the third is the multiplier, the fourth is the tolerance (gold = ±5%, silver = ±10%). e.g. a 1 kΩ ±5% resistor: the first three colour bands encode 1, 0, ×100.

    Activity 6.20: green(5), blue(6), yellow(×10⁴) with silver tolerance ⇒ 56×10⁴ Ω = 560 kΩ ±10%.

  • Be able to read the value of resistors and capacitors using the colour code.
