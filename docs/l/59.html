UNIT 9 · LESSON 9 OF 9 · GRADE 11 AGRICULTURE

🧮 Key facts to memorise

◌ Not started
  • Heifer 7–8 months; age at first service 15–18 months; gestation 265–300 days; first calf 2–2.5 yr; re-breed 4–8 weeks after calving.
  • Bos indicus (humped, tropical, low yield) vs Bos taurus (humpless, temperate, high yield).
  • Ethiopian breeds: Fogera (~915 L, 5.8% BF), Barca (~682 L), Arsi-Bale (~516 L, 5.4–5.8% BF), Horro (~543 L); all multi-purpose; no specialised local dairy breed; ~27 breeds.
  • HF = highest yield (>6,600 L), low butterfat (3.6%)/protein (3.2%); Jersey = richest milk (5% BF, 3.8% protein), heat-tolerant, less feed.
  • Choose breed by climate/adaptability, production objective, feeding system, age of maturity, cost. Cross-bred ~50% exotic preferable.
  • Individual selection: physical type (wedge body, high well-attached udder, 4 quarters), performance records, disease-free.
  • Feed = 60–70% of milk production cost. Roughage:concentrate ≈ 2:1. DM intake ≥3% BW (high producers >4%). 5 L water per L milk.
  • Milking: interval 12 h; duration 5–6 min machine / 7 min hand; order (heifers→older clean→mastitis-history→abnormal); new animals milked separately.
  • Lactation ~10 months/305 days; peak 4–10 weeks after calving; dry period 55–60 days; colostrum = first milk.
  • Cool milk to ≤4 °C. Pasteurise 63–65 °C/20–30 min or 72–75 °C/15–30 s. Cream 18% (single)/48% (double). Yoghurt: 1:1 culture → lactic acid. Cheese: solid=curd, fluid=whey. Butter 80–85% fat. Ghee = butter heated 110–120 °C.
  • Floor: rough concrete, 1.5% slope, non-slippery. Manure: 20–30 kg faeces + 10–14 kg urine/cow/day; pit holds 2–3 days.
  • Profit = income − expenses. Example net profit 54,750 birr/month.
  • Review answers: highest milk producer = Holstein Friesian (A); NOT a basis for choosing breed = attractive appearance (D); highest fat content product = Ghee (C); reproductive disease = Dystocia (B); cool milk to <4 °C (A).
