7.6 Conversion between Temperature Scales

Celsius ↔ Fahrenheit:

  • From the proportional relation: T_C/100 = (T_F − 32)/180
  • T_C = 5/9 × (T_F − 32) = (T_F − 32)/1.8 (7.3)
  • T_F = 9/5 × T_C + 32 = 1.8·T_C + 32 (7.4)
Note: Example 7.1: T_F = 50 °F → T_C = 5/9(50−32) = 10 °C.
Note: Example 7.2: T_C = 20 °C → T_F = 9/5(20)+32 = 68 °F.

Celsius ↔ Kelvin:

  • T_C = T_K − 273.15 and T_K = T_C + 273.15 (7.7)
Note: Example 7.3: T_C = 72 °C → T_K = 72 + 273.15 = 345.15 K.
Note: Example 7.4: T_K = 573.15 K → T_C = 573.15 − 273.15 = 300 °C.

Fahrenheit ↔ Kelvin: convert through Celsius (F → C → K).

Key relationships to remember:

  • The change in temperature of Celsius and Kelvin scales are equal (size of 1 K = size of 1 °C).
  • A temperature difference of 5 °C = 5 K.
