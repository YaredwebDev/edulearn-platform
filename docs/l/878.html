📚 Unit 6 Summary (Key Facts)

  • Food chain = linear; food web = interwoven. Trophic levels: producers → primary → secondary → tertiary consumers.
  • ~10% energy transfer rule; energy pyramids always upright; number/biomass pyramids can invert.
  • Water cycle: evaporation, transpiration, condensation, precipitation, runoff.
  • Carbon cycle: photosynthesis (fix CO₂) vs respiration/decomposition/combustion (release CO₂); greenhouse effect.
  • Nitrogen cycle: fixation → nitrification → assimilation → ammonification → denitrification.
  • Phosphorus cycle: phosphate ions; eutrophication.
  • Earthworms = friends of farmers; biofertilizers & vermicompost = eco-friendly; crop rotation with legumes fixes nitrogen.
