UNIT 5 · LESSON 8 OF 8 · GRADE 11 PHYSICS

🧮 Key facts to memorise

◌ Not started
  • Heat = energy in transit due to ΔT; flows hot→cold; none at thermal equilibrium.
  • Internal energy = kinetic (molecular motion) + potential (attractive forces) energies.
  • Conduction (solids), convection (fluids by movement), radiation (EM waves, no medium).
  • C = Q/ΔT (J/K); c = Q/(mΔT) (J/kg·°C); Q = mcΔT.
  • Water has high specific heat → moderate climates; land heats faster.
  • ΔL = αLΔT; β = 2α (area); γ = 3α (volume); liquids/gases volume only.
  • Water: volume min (density max) at 4 °C; expands between 0–4 °C as cooled.
  • Latent heat Q = mL; fusion solid↔liquid, vaporization liquid↔gas (T constant).
  • Triple point: all three phases coexist; critical point: liquid+gas merge; sublimation solid→vapour.
  • Calorimetry: heat lost = heat gained; balance mcΔT and mL terms.
  • Electrical method Q = Pt.
  • Methods: mixture (bolts in water), electrical heater, ice/steam latent heat.
  • Applications: cooking utensils low c; ocean high c stabilises climate; salt melts ice; steaming; water cycle.
