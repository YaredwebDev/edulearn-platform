8.2 Traditional Medicines of Ethiopia (listening/speaking content)

◌ Not started
Plant (Amharic) Habitat Parts used Uses
'Qontir' Deciduous bush land Leaves treats goiter, diarrhea, diabetes, sore gum, hemorrhage, loose teeth; stomach sickness
'Girar' Dry bush land Fruits, leaflets diarrhea, diabetes, sore gum, hemorrhage, loose teeth; stomach sickness
'Girar' Dry bush land Latex latex from pounded stem + honey → amebiasis and fire wounds
'Yemider berbere' Wetlands, forest floors, stream banks Leaves & flowers cures tonsillitis (chew flowers and spit on tonsillitis)
'Nech shinkur' Dry sandy plains, dried river courses Root treats cancer
Papaya Irrigable land, home garden Seed prevents/treats malaria, diarrhea, ascariasis (ground roots + boiled seeds with honey)
Eret Steep bare mountain slopes Root cures anthrax (pounded root + cold water + local alcohol)
Agam Open Acacia bush land Root shortens labour period before delivery

✍️ Must-know: Listening facts — traditional medicine is used by 80% of the population in developing countries (especially Africa); factors = accessibility, affordability, faith in the system, desire for an alternative. "Nech shinkur" (white garlic) treats cancer; papaya seed prevents/treats malaria.
