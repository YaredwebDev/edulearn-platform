3.11 Renowned Biochemists in Ethiopia

◌ Not started

A biochemist studies chemical processes/transformations in living organisms (DNA, proteins, cell parts), how chemical reactions happen in cells/tissues, and effects of products (food additives, medicines). Biochemistry covers immune systems, gene expression, isolation/analysis/synthesis of products, mutations leading to cancer, and lab management. Biochemists also research agriculture (herbicide–plant interactions), inhibition of growth, and toxicological effects. Unit asks you to list renowned Ethiopian biochemists and discuss their role.
