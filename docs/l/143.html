4.10 Genetic Testing and Counseling

◌ Not started
  • Genetic testing — examines the genetic material and tells an individual the likelihood/risk of passing genetic disorders to children; checks for DNA changes relevant to health. Diagnostic tests during pregnancy: amniocentesis and chorionic villus sampling.
  • Genetic counseling — process of checking a family's medical history/records, ordering and evaluating genetic tests, and helping parents understand and decide what to do next. Counselors help identify/interpret risks of inherited disorders, explain inheritance patterns, and suggest testing.
