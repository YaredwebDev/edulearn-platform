6.2 Vocabulary of the Unit (matric-ready)

Key vocabulary from the "Impact of Poverty" passage:

WordMeaningExample
perpetuatingmaking something continue (especially something bad)Cheap labour is perpetuating poverty.
entrenchedfirmly established and hard to changePoverty creates entrenched inequality.
reverseto change to the opposite; the opposite directionEducation can reverse poverty.
robuststrong and healthy; sturdyA robust economy reduces poverty.
prevalencehow common something isThe prevalence of disease is high in poor areas.
accustomedused to; in the habit ofPoor families are accustomed to hardship.
mercykindness/forgiveness shown to someoneHe begged for mercy.
pledgea solemn promiseThe government made a pledge to fight poverty.
wagesmoney paid for work (by the hour/day/week)Workers demanded fair wages.
recognizeto identify; to accept/acknowledgeWe must recognize the causes of poverty.
