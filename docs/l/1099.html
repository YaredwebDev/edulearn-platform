4.5 Grammar: Conditionals

4.5.1 The Zero Conditional (facts / general truths)

If + present, + present.

  • "If you heat ice, it melts."
  • "If it rains, the ground gets wet."

Used for things that are always true / scientific facts.

4.5.2 The First Conditional (probable future)

If + present, + will/can/may + V1.

  • "If the park opens early, tourists will come."
  • "If you protect the forest, animals will survive."

Used for likely/possible future situations.

Note: ✋ Trap: In the if-clause of a first conditional use the present, not "will": "If it rains, we will stay" — never "If it will rain."

📝 Writing focus: Short Report

A short report is a factual piece that:

  • Has a title.
  • States what was observed/done, when/where, and the findings/results.
  • Uses factual, clear language and past tense.
  • Ends with a short conclusion or recommendation.
