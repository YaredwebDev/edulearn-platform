  • Distance: d=√((x₂−x₁)²+(y₂−y₁)²).
  • Section (p:q): R=((px₂+qx₁)/(p+q),(py₂+qy₁)/(p+q)).
  • Midpoint: ((x₁+x₂)/2,(y₁+y₂)/2).
  • Slope m=(y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁)=tanθ; horizontal m=0, vertical no slope.
  • Forms: point-slope y−y₁=m(x−x₁); slope-intercept y=mx+b; two-point; general Ax+By+C=0.
  • Parallel ⟺ m₁=m₂; perpendicular ⟺ m₁·m₂=−1.
  • Circle standard (x−h)²+(y−k)²=r²; origin x²+y²=r²; general x²+y²+lx+my+n=0.
  • Triangle area: ½|x₁(y₂−y₃)+x₂(y₃−y₁)+x₃(y₁−y₂)|.
