UNIT 9 · LESSON 6 OF 6 · GRADE 9 ENGLISH

📝 Writing focus: Descriptive Essay

◌ Not started
  • Extended writing on the reading passage: define cultural diversity; describe the benefits of being aware of it; describe the challenges/disadvantages.
  • Descriptive essay using the present perfect: the textbook's model paragraph ("Merhawit has just walked outside with her grandmother...") shows how to use present perfect (has finished, has gathered, has just dropped, have not come, has moved in, has already sat down, has just finished) to describe an ongoing scene. Use the same pattern in your own paragraph.
  • Write about your community: describe where it is, how many people live there, livelihood, religion and ethnicity, natural/man-made heritages, and other features.

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) explain equality vs equity vs justice and give the school-funding and runner examples, (2) define all 10 passage vocabulary words, (3) use the phrasal verbs count/crack/cross/cut, (4) form and choose between simple past and present perfect, (5) use since/for correctly, (6) list causes/effects/prevention of discrimination, (7) write a descriptive essay?
