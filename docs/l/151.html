5.14 Harmful Traditional Practices

◌ Not started

Harmful traditional practices affect physical and mental health and are actions against people's rights. In Ethiopia ~20 harmful traditional practices are recorded, ~50% dealing with skin mutilation; FGM alone accounts for 73% of the practices. - Early marriage — forcing underage girls to marry (cultural/poverty); causes school dropout, pregnancy, long-term health risks. - Kidnapping/abduction — making a girl a wife unwillingly. - Gender-based violence — any unwanted sexual contact (abuse/harassment) or violence within relatives/marriage. - Female Genital Mutilation (FGM) — partial or total removal of external female genital organs.

✍️ Must-know (review): a commonly practised FGM in Ethiopia = excision/clitoridectomy/infibulation/circumcision. Solving underage marriage = awareness + law + education (all).
