UNIT 1 · LESSON 7 OF 7 · GRADE 11 PHYSICS

🧮 Key facts to memorise

◌ Not started
  • Physics gives society the understanding for medical imaging (CT, MRI, PET, ultrasound, laser surgery), transport (cars, planes, space shuttles) and electronics.
  • Physics communities: national/continental/worldwide organisations for science.
  • EPS objectives centre on promoting education, research & popularising physics in Ethiopia.
  • Scientific method = ordered steps to acquire knowledge based on experimental evidence.
  • The mission of physics = advance science, engineering & innovation for the benefit of all and society.
  • Recent discoveries: exoplanets, black holes, quantum cryptography, high-energy physics, gravitational waves, global warming, JWST.

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) give the benefits/influence of physics on society in transport, health, electronics and the economy; (2) explain what a physics community is and list the EPS objectives; (3) distinguish experiential (sensory perception, introspection, memory, testimony) from non-experiential knowledge and explain the scientific method; (4) state the mission of physics and list the career fields; and (5) name at least five recent physics discoveries? Re-answer the review questions too (e.g., ten technological benefits of physics; process skills for investigating phenomena; why many countries collaborate at the forefront of physics research).
