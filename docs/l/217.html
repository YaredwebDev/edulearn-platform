📚 Unit 6 Summary (Key Facts)

◌ Not started
  • Ecology = study of interactions among organisms and with their environment.
  • Abiotic vs biotic factors.
  • Ecological levels: organism → population → community → ecosystem → biosphere.
  • Ecosystem = community + non-living environment; self-supporting.
  • Biomes: tropical rain forest, desert, savanna, temperate grassland, boreal forest, temperate broad-leaf forest, tundra; aquatic (freshwater <1% salt, marine ~3%).
  • Ecological succession: primary vs secondary.
  • Ecological relationships: competition, predation, herbivory, parasitism, mutualism, commensalism.
