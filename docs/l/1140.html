2.2 Operations on Polynomial Functions

Addition & Subtraction: add/subtract coefficients of like terms (same power of x). Remove brackets for subtraction.

  • (f+g)(x)=f(x)+g(x); (f−g)(x)=f(x)−g(x).
  • Sum/difference of polynomials is a polynomial.
  • If degrees differ, degree of sum/diff = the larger degree; if same degree, the result may drop a degree (like terms cancel).

Multiplication: multiply each term of one by every term of the other (distributive property).

  • (fg)(x)=f(x)g(x).
  • If deg f = m and deg g = n (nonzero), then deg(fg) = m+n.
  • Product of polynomials is a polynomial; if either is the zero polynomial, the product is zero with no degree.

Division (long division): (f÷g)(x)=f(x)÷g(x), g(x)≠0.

  • Stop when the remainder has degree less than the divisor's degree.
  • Result written as f(x)/g(x) = q(x) + r(x)/g(x).
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Degree(fg) = degree f + degree g. Degree(f+g) = larger degree (or possibly lower if like terms cancel).
