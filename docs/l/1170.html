7.2 Division of a Line Segment

Section formula (internal, ratio p:q): The point R(x₀,y₀) dividing P(x₁,y₁)Q(x₂,y₂) internally in the ratio p:q is:

R = ( (p·x₂ + q·x₁)/(p+q) , (p·y₂ + q·y₁)/(p+q) )

Example: divide A(−7,4) and B(8,9) in ratio 3:2 (p=3,q=2): x=(3·8+2·(−7))/(3+2)=(24−14)/5=2; y=(3·9+2·4)/5=(27+8)/5=7 → R(2,7).

Midpoint formula: the midpoint M of P(x₁,y₁)Q(x₂,y₂) is

M = ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2)

Midpoint of a segment

Examples:

  • Midpoint of A(−1,−2), B(3,2) = ((−1+3)/2,(−2+2)/2)=(1,0).
  • If M(4,6) is the midpoint of AB and A=(−3,−2): (−3+x)/2=4 → x=11; (−2+y)/2=6 → y=14 → B(11,14).
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Section formula (p:q) uses (px₂+qx₁)/(p+q). Midpoint is the special case p:q=1:1 → averages of coordinates.
