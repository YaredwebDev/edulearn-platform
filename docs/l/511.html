8.3 Phrasal Verbs: come (multiple)

◌ Not started
Phrasal verb Meaning Example
came through to survive/recover from; to succeed The patient came through the surgery.
came in to enter; to arrive (for a result) The results came in this morning.
came out to be published/revealed; to emerge The report came out last week.
comes off to become detached; to happen successfully The label came off the bottle.
come up with to think of/produce (an idea) She came up with a good plan.
come over to visit; to move towards Please come over to my house.
come down to decrease; to move downwards The fever came down overnight.
came across to find by chance; to appear I came across the article online.
come on to begin; to hurry (encouragement) The pain came on suddenly.

✋ Trap: come up with = produce an idea; come through = survive/recover; come across = find by chance. Note the different tenses used (came/come) depending on the sentence.
