⚡ Quick Revision Box (Unit 2)

◌ Not started
  1. Chlorophyll is found in the granum; carbon fixation happens in the stroma.
  2. Palisade mesophyll has the most chloroplasts; stomata are more abundant on the lower epidermis.
  3. Xylem transports water; phloem transports food.
  4. Seed = matured ovule; fruit = matured ovary.
  5. Root is positively geotropic and hydrotropic; shoot is positively phototropic.
  6. Source of O₂ in photosynthesis = water (photolysis).
  7. Three conditions for germination = water, oxygen, warmth.
  8. Gymnosperm reproduction: cone (not flower); naked seeds.
  9. Dicot seed: 2 cotyledons, no endosperm; Monocot: 1 cotyledon, large endosperm.
  10. Ringing/girdling kills the plant because food transport through phloem is blocked.
