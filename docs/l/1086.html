2.1 Reading: Learning Strategies

(the core "story"/text of the unit)

The passage teaches how to learn effectively. Good learners:

  • Set goals and plan their work.

  • Stay motivated.

  • Manage time well.

  • Reward themselves after completing tasks.

✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Reading questions test (a) the main idea (how to study effectively), (b) the strategies named (goals, planning, motivation, time management, rewards), and (c) vocabulary in context.
