📚 Unit 4 Summary (Key Facts)

◌ Not started
  • Charges: + (protons) and − (electrons); conserved (q = ne) and quantized.
  • Charging: rubbing (opposite), conduction (same), induction (opposite).
  • Electroscope detects charge; discharge and grounding; lightning = induction discharge.
  • Coulomb's law: F = k·q₁q₂/r², k = 9×10⁹.
  • Electric field: E = F/q = k·q/r²; field lines + to −.
  • Current I = Q/t (A); voltage V = W/Q (V); resistance R = V/I (Ω); R = ρL/A.
  • Series: R_eq = sum; Parallel: 1/R_eq = sum of reciprocals.
  • Voltmeter in parallel; ammeter in series.
  • Safety: fuse, ground wire, GFI; shock damage depends on current, duration, path.
