📚 Unit 2 Summary (Key Facts)

  • Characteristics of life: cells, energy, response, growth, reproduction, excretion, ordered complexity, homeostasis, adaptation.
  • Classification = grouping by similarities; taxonomy = science of naming/classifying.
  • Taxonomic hierarchy: Kingdom → Phylum/Division → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species.
  • Three domains: Bacteria, Archaea, Eukarya.
  • Binomial nomenclature: genus + specific epithet; genus capitalized, species lowercase; italicized.
  • Five kingdoms (Whittaker, 1969): Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, Animalia.
  • Invertebrates vs vertebrates; plant groups (angiosperms, gymnosperms, bryophytes).
  • Renowned Ethiopian taxonomists: Mesfin Taddese, Sebsebe Demissew, Ensermu Kelbessa, Silesh Nemomissa, Abebe Getahun.
