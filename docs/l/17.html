3.3 Sugar Crops Production and Management

◌ Not started

Sugar crops are raw materials for sugar production; sugar cane and sugar beet are examples. In Ethiopia, sugar cane is by far the most significant sugar crop.

3.3.1 Sugar cane (Saccharum officinarum)

  • Tropical/sub-tropical crop grown mainly for sugar. Needs a long warm season. Cane juice is processed into sugar; some residue is animal feed.
  • By-products (must-know):
  • Molasses — the dark liquid from sugar-cane juice; used to produce ethanol.
  • Bagasse — the fibrous residue left after juice extraction; used for paper & plastics, or as firewood.
  • The green top (leaf) is used directly as animal feed.
  • Cropping cycle: 14–18 months from planting to first harvest. After the first harvest the plant regenerates from roots and is ready in ~12 months; the second harvest is called the ratoon crop (smaller than the first). Ratoon harvests are limited to 2–3 times, then new canes are planted. Needs fertile soils; on poor soils requires heavy fertilisation.
  • Land prep & planting: propagated vegetatively using stem cuttings from the tops of young plants (disease/pest free); cuttings ~30 cm with at least 2–3 buds; planted in rows ~1.6 m apart, placed end-to-end in a furrow, covered with 2–5 cm soil.
  • Fertilisation: depends on soil fertility; needs large amounts of nitrogen, but excess N delays maturity.
  • Weed/pest: critical periods ~3 weeks and ~14 weeks after planting (weed competition → high loss). Weeds removed manually or chemically. Insect pests: sugar cane borers, army worms, locusts, termites → pre-planting cultivation + insecticides. Diseases: fungal smut, bacterial Ratoon Stunting Disease → resistant varieties, removal of diseased plants, chemicals.
  • Harvesting: matures 14–18 months; remove leaves & top before cutting stem; sometimes controlled fire removes dry leaves. Stem transported for processing.
  • Ethiopian status (2022): 8 state-owned sugar factories — Wonji-Shewa, Matahara, Finchawa, Kessem, Tendaho, Arjo, Omo Kuraz II and Omo Kuraz III — producing ~400,000 metric tons annually. In 2023 demand exceeds production → need to increase production to meet local demand and export.

✍️ Must-know (sugar): sugar cane matures in 14–18 months; ratoon = regeneration harvest (smaller, limited to 2–3); molasses → ethanol; bagasse → paper/plastics/firewood; propagated from 30 cm stem cuttings with 2–3 buds; heavy N but excess delays maturity; 8 state factories.
