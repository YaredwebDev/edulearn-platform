8B.1 Making a debate

  • Debate the motion: "Sexual practice before marriage is good" (or bad). Use expressions for supporting/rejecting/refuting: I argue…, I propose…, my objection…, I agree/disagree…, in my opinion, in my point of view.

8B.2 Conducting an interview

  • Write 5–10 interview questions (e.g. What can the youth do to stop the spread of HIV/AIDS?). Use question/social expressions: What do you think…? How can you…? Can you…? Who do you think is responsible for…? In your opinion… Interview a partner, organise responses, and report to the class.

8B.3 Panel discussion

  • A panel discussion is a group meeting to discuss a public issue or solve a problem. Prepare the forum, use your interview information, present to panelists, gather views, and report the points of consensus.
