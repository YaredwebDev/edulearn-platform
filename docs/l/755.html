⚡ Quick Revision Box (Unit 4)

◌ Not started
  1. F = k·q₁q₂/r², k = 9×10⁹ N·m²/C².
  2. Electron charge = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C; 1 C = 6.25×10¹⁸ electrons.
  3. I = Q/t (A); V = W/Q (V); V = IR (Ohm's law).
  4. R = ρL/A; resistivity unit Ω·m.
  5. Series: R_eq = R₁+R₂+R₃; Parallel: 1/R_eq = 1/R₁+1/R₂+1/R₃.
  6. Series current same, voltages add; parallel voltage same, currents add.
  7. Voltmeter parallel (high R); ammeter series (low R).
  8. E = k·q/r²; field lines + to −, never cross.
  9. Charging by rubbing = opposite signs; conduction = same; induction = opposite.
  10. ~100 mA can be fatal; fuse melts on overload; GFI trips on tiny leaks.
