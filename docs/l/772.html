6.7 Primary Colors of Light and Human Vision

◌ Not started
  • The retina contains millions of cones (sensitive to color) and rods (sensitive to intensity).
  • Most people have three kinds of cones: most sensitive to red, green, and blue light.
  • Red, green, and blue are the primary colors (primary additive colors) because they are fundamental to human vision; all other colors can be produced by adding combinations of them.
  • Adding equal amounts of red, green, and blue produces white light.

✍️ Must-know: Primary (additive) colors = Red, Green, Blue. RGB added equally → white.
