Word formation — compounds

A compound is made by joining words: - noun + noun: pen friend, school teacher, flower pot, rain coat, baby sitter, firewood, waistline, fireworks, well-being, ice-cream. - adjective + noun: greenhouse, rainy season, sunny days, far sight, fast food, black box, dark room. - verb + noun: swimming pool, driving license, writing pad, dining room, washing machine. - Compounds may be one word (fireworks), hyphenated (well-being), or separate (ice-cream).

✍️ Must-know: Be able to form and classify compounds (noun+noun, adjective+noun, verb+noun). Examples from the book: pen friend, greenhouse, black box, dining room.
