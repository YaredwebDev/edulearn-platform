2.5 Barley (Hordeum vulgare)

◌ Not started
  • Origin: Middle East. Used as human food; residue for livestock; used for malting (controlled germination producing enzymes that turn cereal starch to sugars, then fermented to alcohol). Growth requirements similar to wheat.
  • Production: altitude ~2200–3000 m; well-drained loam.
  • Land prep: 2–3 cultivations (like wheat).
  • Planting: broadcast or rows (25 cm), depth ~5 cm.
  • Fertilisation: ~60 kg N + 26 kg P per ha for food barley; for malting barley nitrogen must be reduced (high N lowers malt quality).
  • Weed/pest/disease: most weeds/diseases of wheat also affect barley; similar control (resistant varieties, rotation, changing planting time). Insects: army worm, grasshopper, barley shoot fly.
  • Harvesting: matures 3–4 months; indicator = drying & yellowing. National average ≈ 19 quintals (1.9 t)/ha. Source of carbs, protein, fat, fibre.
