10.6 Confusing Words (spelling/pronunciation/meaning)

  • confidant/confident — confidant = a trusted person (n); confident = sure (adj).
  • farther/farthest/further — farther = physical distance; further = additional.
  • anyone/no one/someone — anyone (any person); no one (nobody); someone (some person).
  • advice/advise — advice (n); advise (v).
  • later/latter/latest — later (afterwards); latter (second of two); latest (most recent).
  • access/axis/excess — access (entrance/right to use); axis (central line); excess (too much).
  • beside/besides/till — beside (next to); besides (in addition); till (until).
  • a little/little/the little — a little (some); little (not enough); the little (the small amount).
  • elder/older — elder (of family/relatives); older (age, general).
  • raises/rises/races — raises (lifts/causes to rise, transitive); rises (goes up, intransitive); races (competitions).
  • breath/breathe — breath (n); breathe (v).
Note: ✋ Trap: "She is confident of her success" (not confidant). "I don't want any further explanation." "Is there anyone in the class?" "The doctor advised the patients." "his latest visit." "All students should have access to a good library." "Besides football, he plays cricket." "A little knowledge is a dangerous thing." "He is my elder brother." "The sun rises in the east." "Make sure to breathe deeply."
