3.2 Vocabulary of the Unit (matric-ready)

Key vocabulary from the "Traffic Accidents" passage:

WordMeaningExample
crowdingtoo many people/vehicles in one placeCrowding on the road slows traffic.
indulgeto allow oneself to enjoy something (often excessively)Don't indulge in speeding.
endangeringputting (someone) in dangerDrunk driving is endangering lives.
outcomethe result of an eventThe outcome of the crash was serious.
refrainto stop yourself from doing somethingDrivers should refrain from using phones.
scaryfrighteningNear-misses on the road are very scary.

Synonyms & antonyms (a whole question type in Grade 9):

  • Synonyms (same meaning): big/large, happy/glad, fast/rapid, end/finish, start/begin.
  • Antonyms (opposites): fast/slow, safe/dangerous, careful/careless, obey/break (rules).
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: In exams, when asked for a synonym, pick a word close in meaning; for an antonym, pick the opposite. Use the context of the sentence.
