1.3 Major Discoveries That Revolutionized Biology

#ScientistDatesKey contribution
1Aristotle384–322 BCFirst classification system: "Ladder of Life"; classified organisms as herbs, shrubs, trees, and by ability to move
2Galen129–161 ADTransformed medical science; distinguished veins and arteries; recognized larynx produces voice
3Antonie van Leeuwenhoek1632–1723Built strong lenses (up to ~500×); discovered bacteria and the cell vacuole
4Carl Linnaeus1707–1775System for naming/classifying organisms; genus + species (e.g., Homo sapiens)
5Charles Darwin1809–1882Theory of evolution by natural selection; On the Origin of Species (1859)
6Gregor Mendel1822–1884Laws of genetic inheritance using peas; coined "dominant" and "recessive"; father of genetics
7Louis Pasteur1822–1895Father of medical microbiology; germ theory of disease; pasteurization; anthrax and rabies vaccines
8Robert Koch1843–1910Father of modern bacteriology; identified causes of tuberculosis, cholera, anthrax
9Jane Goodall1934–Ethologist; studies of chimpanzees (omnivores, tool users); animal rights leader
10Barbara McClintock1902–1992Geneticist; studied maize chromosomes; discovered transposition (jumping genes); Nobel Peace Prize 1983
11Watson & Crick1928– /1916–2004Discovered the structure of DNA (double helix); Nobel Prize 1962
12Wilmut & Campbell1944– /1954–2012Cloned the first mammal, Dolly the sheep (1996), by nuclear transfer
✍️ Must-know: Mendel = father of genetics; Pasteur = father of medical microbiology / germ theory; Koch = father of modern bacteriology.
