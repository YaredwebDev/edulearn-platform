📚 Unit 1 Summary (Key Facts)

  • Biology = scientific study of living things (bios = life, logos = study).
  • Characteristics of life: cells, energy, response to stimuli, growth, reproduction, excretion, ordered complexity, homeostasis, adaptation.
  • Scientific method: observation → question → hypothesis → experiment → conclusion → communication.
  • Tools of a biologist: hand lens, microscope, slides/cover slips, autoclave, incubator, petri dishes, culture tubes, flasks, balance, etc.
  • Microscope: light (simple/compound) vs electron; magnification = eyepiece × objective.
  • Laboratory safety is essential before any activity.
