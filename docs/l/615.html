◌ Not started
  • Statistics = collecting, organizing, presenting, analyzing, interpreting data.
  • Qualitative vs quantitative; continuous vs discrete.
  • Primary vs secondary data.
  • Population (whole) vs sample (subset); census.
  • Frequency distribution table; histogram (V on x, f on y).
  • Mean x̄ = Σx/n = Σfx/Σf; properties of mean.
  • Median = middle of ordered data (avg of two middles for even n).
  • Mode = most frequent (may be non-unique or absent).
  • Range = max − min.
  • Variance σ² = Σ(x−x̄)²/n; σ = √variance.
  • Adding constant: variance unchanged; multiplying by c: variance × c², σ × |c|.
  • Probability P(E) = n(E)/n(S).
  • Sample space, event, equally likely, tree diagrams.
