15.1 Definition of Basic Terms in Human Nutrition

◌ Not started
  • Nutrition = the science of ingestion, digestion, absorption, assimilation & biosynthesis of nutrients in the body.
  • Food = any nutritious substance that is culturally acceptable, eaten & drunk to maintain life & growth.
  • Diet = the way people feed themselves & the foodstuffs they use — eating patterns of individuals/groups and the sequence of meals in a day (2, 3 or 4 meals/day). Influenced by tradition & religion, economic position, place in society, and natural surroundings.
  • Nutrients = chemical substances needed to live & grow. They provide energy, repair body tissues, support growth, and aid normal body functioning. Some needed in large quantity, others minute.
  • Macronutrients — needed in large amounts to maintain body structure & systems: carbohydrates, fat, protein.
  • Micronutrients — needed in small quantities for a healthy life: minerals & vitamins (used to make enzymes, hormones & other essential substances).
  • Healthy eating = eating a variety of foods giving all essential nutrients; a healthy diet provides sufficient nutrients, balance & variety.
  • Balanced diet = a diet giving the body the right proportion of nutrients & calories, with desired proportions of carbohydrates, proteins & micronutrients.
