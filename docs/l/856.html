2.11 Renowned Ethiopian Botanists

Prof. Sebsebe Demissew:

  • Professor of Plant Systematics and Biodiversity, Addis Ababa University; Executive Director of the Gullele Botanic Garden.
  • Awarded the Kew International Medal (2016); elected Foreign Member of the Royal Society (2018) — the first sub-Saharan African scientist in its 357-year history.
  • Awarded the "Cuatrecasas Medal" (2021) by the Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History for conserving and recording Ethiopian flora.
