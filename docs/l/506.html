7.3 Phrasal Verbs: drop, dry, eat

◌ Not started
Phrasal verb Meaning Example
drop in to visit briefly (informally) She dropped in on her grandmother.
drop off to deliver/to leave (someone/something) He dropped off supplies at the hospital.
drop out to quit a course/activity He dropped out of school.
dried off became dry (on the surface) The clothes dried off in the sun.
dried out became completely dry The soil dried out in the heat.
eat up to finish eating; to consume The children ate up all the food.

✋ Trap: drop in = pay a short visit; drop off = deliver/leave; drop out = quit/withdraw. dried off (surface) vs dried out (completely/throughout).
