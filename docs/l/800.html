5.13 Epidemiology of STIs in Ethiopia

◌ Not started
  • Epidemiology = study of the transmission, causation, outbreak, surveillance, monitoring and control of a disease.
  • ~1.4 million people are infected with STIs every day globally. In Ethiopia the highest reported rates are among 15–24-year-olds.
  • Risks of STI epidemic: unprotected sex, multiple sexual partners, history of STIs, forced sexual activity, alcohol/recreational-drug misuse, injecting drugs (needle sharing), and being young (half of new STIs are in people aged 15–24).

✍️ Must-know (review): gonorrhea & syphilis are bacterial STIs; genital herpes → painful blisters/burning; untreated syphilis affects the brain & heart; chancroid, scabies & molluscum contagiosum are other STIs; condom use, fewer partners and delaying sex all lower STI risk.
