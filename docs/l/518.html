9.5 Speaking: Causes, Effects and Prevention of Discrimination

◌ Not started

The unit gives a table on discrimination in society. Know these categories:

Causes of discrimination: age, disability, ethnicity, origin, political belief, race, religion, sex/gender, language, culture. Effects of discrimination on society: unequal societies, social/environmental/economic/cultural problems, and unity problems. Ways of preventing discrimination: respect cultural and racial differences in schools, workplaces and communal activities; be good in conduct and speech; refuse to initiate, participate in, or condone discrimination and harassment; avoid race-based or culturally offensive talks, humour or stories.

✍️ Must-know: In Ethiopia, policy holds there should be no educational discrimination among citizens (by ethnicity, nationality, age, gender, race, economic condition, disability or religion). This is a speaking/discussion topic.
