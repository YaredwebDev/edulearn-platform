10.3 Phrasal Verbs (master list — also see Glossary)

  • hold on — wait; hold tightly; blow up — fill with air; explode; bring up — raise a child; call off — cancel; carry on — continue; come across — seem; find by accident; come up with — think of (a solution); fall apart — break into pieces; get along — be friendly; get away — leave/escape; get over — recover; give up — stop trying; go on — continue.
  • Also practise: hold off/on/out/up; take back/after/into account/part in/up/over/down/on/away.
✍️ Must-know: ✋ Must-know: "call off" = cancel; "come up with" = think of; "put off" = postpone; "put up with" = tolerate; "carry on" = continue; "give up" = stop. These are the highest-frequency ones for exams. Full A–Z list in the Glossary.
