11.5 Environmental Management

◌ Not started
  • Environment = the climatic, biological & chemical conditions that may affect the growth & welfare of organisms.
  • Humans interact with the environment and should use resources in ways that do not harm future use — such practices are environmental management practices.
  • Main objectives: protect human health & wellbeing; conserve & preserve all life forms & habitats. Major goal: prevent the release of harmful chemicals into the environment.
  • Aims of environmental management: prevent air pollution (improve management of agricultural wastes like manure); minimise water pollution to meet quality standards (reduce erosion from agricultural areas); reduce health & environmental risks of agricultural pesticides by proper use; sound solid-waste management (e.g. animal manure); identify core processes of major outputs in agriculture & their environmental impacts; ensure farmers meet environmental regulations.
