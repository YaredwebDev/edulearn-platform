7.4 Ratio of Perimeters of Similar Plane Figures

◌ Not started

Definition: If two figures are similar, the ratio of their perimeters equals the ratio of their corresponding side lengths (the scale factor).

Example: △ABC has sides 6, 8, 10 (P = 24); △DEF has sides 3, 4, 5 (P = 12). Ratio of perimeters = 24/12 = 2 = scale factor.

✍️ Must-know: P₁/P₂ = s₁/s₂ (perimeters scale by the scale factor).
