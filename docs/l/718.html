📚 Unit 7 Summary (Key Facts)

◌ Not started
  • Temperature = degree of hotness/coldness = measure of average kinetic energy.
  • Room temp 18–22 °C; body temp 37 °C.
  • Global warming & greenhouse gases (CO₂, methane, nitrous oxide, fluorinated gases, water vapor).
  • Thermometer = measures temperature; three scales (Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin).
  • Kelvin = SI unit; absolute zero = 0 K = −273.15 °C.
  • Types: mercury, alcohol, resistance, thermocouple, thermistor, radiation.
  • Conversions: C↔F, C↔K.
  • Thermal expansion; linear expansion ΔL = αL₀ΔT; coefficient of linear expansion.
