📚 Unit 5 Summary (Key Facts)

◌ Not started
  • Digestion: mouth (amylase) → stomach (HCl + pepsin) → small intestine (villi absorb: sugars/amino acids → capillaries, fats → lacteals).
  • Heart: 4 chambers, valves, double circulation (pulmonary + systemic); BP 120/80; Lub-Dup sounds.
  • Blood: O universal donor, AB universal recipient; Rh incompatibility → erythroblastosis fetalis.
  • Breathing: inspiration (diaphragm contracts) / expiration; gas exchange in alveoli by diffusion.
  • Excretion: nephron filtration → reabsorption → secretion; kidney makes EPO + vitamin D.
  • Immunity: innate (non-specific) vs adaptive (specific, memory, vaccination).
  • Ethiopian medical pioneers: Prof. Asrat Woldeyes, Dr. Widad KidaneMariam.
