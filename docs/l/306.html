6.1 Introduction — Classification of organic compounds

◌ Not started

Oxygenated hydrocarbons include alcohols, ethers, aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids and esters. Aldehydes and ketones both contain a carbonyl group (C=O); aldehydes have pungent odours, ketones smell sweet. Carboxylic acids and esters occur in many fruits/flowers; many carboxylic acids are food additives (jams, jellies, candies, pickles).

💡 Ethiopian context: ethanol (blended with gasoline for cars) and locally prepared drinks are covered below — Katikalla, Tella, Tej, plus beer/wine.
