10.6 Speaking: Expressing Opinions (Polite Agreement / Disagreement)

Phrases to express opinions, ask for opinions, agree and disagree politely:

Expressing opinionAsking for opinionAgreementDisagreement
I think... / I believe... / I feel... / In my opinion... / I would say...What do you think...? What are your thoughts on...? How do you feel about...? What's your opinion on...?So do I. / Me too. / Definitely. / I agree. / I couldn't agree more.I disagree! / I don't agree. / I'm not sure I agree with you on... / I'm sorry but I don't agree. / I'm afraid I disagree.

Model — polite agreement: "In my opinion, the different regions of Ethiopia have special staple foods... the Kocho in Gurage and the Tihlo in Adigrat are delicious..." (from the textbook).

Model — polite disagreement: "The cultural foods may be enjoyable to many Ethiopians, but I think they may not be delicious to tourists who haven't experienced such foods..."

Note: ✋ Trap: Be polite — use "I'm afraid I disagree" rather than blunt "You're wrong." In an exam, show both a clear opinion and a polite reason.

📝 Writing focus: Expository Essay on the Uses of the Internet

The unit gives a diagram of internet uses — video conference, online shopping, file sharing, online banking/communication — and a sample essay ("Importance of the Internet") that defines the internet and describes its role in communication.

Structure your essay:

  1. Introduction — the internet is now essential in everyday life.
  2. Body — describe each use (communication, information, video conference, online shopping, file sharing).
  3. Conclusion — the internet is a great resource if used wisely.

Sentence-level opinion practice: For each statement, write a polite agreement or disagreement paragraph ("Internet use is important to our country. I think it is important..." / "Many people use social media dishonestly. In my opinion...").

Note: ✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) list the advantages and disadvantages of computers/the internet, (2) define all 10 passage vocabulary words (including boon/bane), (3) use phrasal verbs with cut/do/dress, (4) form and choose among the four tenses, (5) read the social-media bar graph and state the highest/lowest values, (6) express opinions politely, agreeing/disagreeing, (7) write an expository essay on the uses of the internet?
