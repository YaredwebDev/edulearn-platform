2.4.1 The Simple Past Tense

  • Regular verbs: add –ed (walk→walked, work→worked).
  • Irregular verbs: change form (go→went, take→took, come→came, see→saw, buy→bought).
  • Signal words: yesterday, ago, last (week/month/year), in 2000, the other day, then, when.
  • Uses: a completed action at a definite past time: "I visited Lalibela last year." Past habits/sequence of events: "He woke up, ate, and left."
  • If-clause type 2 uses past simple for an unreal present condition: "If I had money, I would travel." (See Unit 7 conditionals.)

✋ Trap: Do not use present perfect with a definite past time word (yesterday/ago/last). Use simple past.

2.4.2 The Four Sentence Structures (important for writing & identifying)

  1. Simple sentence — one independent clause (one main verb + subject). - Subject + Verb: "Birds fly." / S+V+O: "She reads books." / S+V+C: "He is happy." / S+V+O+O: "She gave me a gift."
  2. Compound sentence — two or more independent clauses joined by a coordinating conjunction (FANBOYS: for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so) or a semicolon. - "I was tired, but I kept walking." / "He is smart; he works hard."
  3. Complex sentence — one independent clause + one or more dependent (subordinate) clauses joined by a subordinator. - Subordinators: although, even if, as if, after, when, since, as, while, because, before, if, unless. - "Although it rained, we went out." (dependent + independent)
  4. Compound-complex sentence — at least two independent clauses + at least one dependent clause. - "I wanted to go, but I stayed home because I was tired."

✍️ Must-know: Be able to classify any sentence as simple / compound / complex / compound-complex. Identify coordinators (FANBOYS) for compound and subordinators for complex.

2.4.3 Summary Writing (rules to memorise)

A good summary: - is in paragraph form; - opens with a sentence stating the writer's most essential idea; - does not include every idea from the original; - understands the main idea of each paragraph and rewrites it briefly in your own words; - is about one-third of the original length; - does not insert your own opinions, interpretations, deductions, or comments; - ends with a sentence that wraps up/concludes.

✍️ Must-know: Summarise = paraphrase the main ideas in your own words, keep it ~1/3, no personal opinions, conclude with a wrap-up sentence.
