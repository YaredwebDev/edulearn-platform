UNIT 6 · LESSON 20 OF 20 · GRADE 11 BIOLOGY

6.20 🧮 Key formulas & numbers to memorise

◌ Not started
  • Quadrat estimate = (A/a) × n. Mark–recapture = (M×n)/X.
  • Growth rate r = B – D; dN/dt = rN (exponential); dN/dt = rN((K−N)/K) (logistic).
  • J-shaped curve = exponential; S-shaped curve = logistic; carrying capacity K.
  • Crude birth rate = live births per 1000 people/year. Census ~every 10 years.
  • Ethiopia population: 42.6 M (1984) → 53.5 M (1994) → 73.5 M (2007) → 83.7 M (2012) → >110 M now; 45% under 15.
  • 24 national parks, 2 wild sanctuaries, 6 wildlife reserves, 5 community conservation areas in Ethiopia.
  • Acid rain: 2SO₂+O₂+2H₂O→2H₂SO₄; 4NO₂+O₂+2H₂O→4HNO₃.
  • Ozone layer in stratosphere; depleted by CFCs (chlorine catalytic cycle).

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) define population ecology, size, density and the three dispersal patterns; (2) use the quadrat & mark-recapture formulas; (3) differentiate de facto vs de jure census; (4) distinguish exponential (J) vs logistic (S) growth and state what carrying capacity is; (5) define all demographic parameters and interpret the three population-pyramid types and three survivorship curves; (6) give density-dependent vs density-independent examples; (7) classify renewable vs non-renewable resources; (8) define conservation and list Ethiopian wildlife/plant/soil/water conservation areas & mechanisms (incl. in-situ vs ex-situ and Green Legacy); (9) explain the impact of traffic accidents on animals (Geralle 29%); (10) describe pollution, climate change/global warming, ozone depletion, acid rain, biodiversity loss, bioaccumulation & resource depletion with causes/effects and examples (DDT thin eggshells); and (11) describe the Konso & Qero/Guassa indigenous conservation practices? Re-answer the review too (mark-recapture 100/40 → 250 birds; Type III survivorship; census every 10 years; birth rate 16/1000, death rate ~2.8/1000; soil-preserving = crop rotation; chief ozone depleter = nitrogen oxides & fluorocarbons; most abundant water pollutant = pesticides; densest = clumped; logistic NOT J-shaped; invasive-species & ex-situ conservation).
