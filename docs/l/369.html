Word Meaning Example
adulterate to make impure by adding inferior material Never adulterate the honey.
proboscis the long sucking mouthpart of an insect Bees suck nectar with their proboscis.
nectar the sweet fluid bees collect from flowers Bees gather nectar from flowers.
foraging searching for food Foraging bees visit many flowers.
honey-comb the hexagonal wax cells where bees store honey The hive was full of honey-comb.
spoils becomes bad / loses quality Honey never spoils.
stench a bad smell Honey removes the stench of a wound.
inflammation swelling and pain in body tissue Inflammation causes chronic disease.
therapeutic having medical healing value Honey has therapeutic value.
laxative a medicine that empties the bowels Honey works as a laxative.
heartburn pain caused by indigestion Honey relieves heartburn.
wits intelligence / quickness of mind Honey sharpens the wits.
disinfectant something that kills bacteria Honey acts as a disinfectant.
assimilated absorbed into the body Glucose is easily assimilated.
caries tooth decay Sugar causes dental caries.
antibiotic killing/inhibiting bacteria Honey has antibiotic properties.
