4.3 Renowned Ethiopian Geneticist

◌ Not started

Dr. Melaku Worede: - A geneticist and agronomist; used science to help poor farmers and save Africa's seeds from extinction. - Awarded the "Right Livelihood Award" (1989) for preserving Ethiopia's genetic wealth. - Key role in establishing the Plant Genetic Resource Centre in Addis Ababa (Director from 1979, 14+ years). - Developed and grew locally adapted native seeds (landraces) (e.g., durum wheat) without commercial fertilizers; native seeds exceeded high-input counterparts by 10–15% and original farmers' cultivars by 20–25% in yield.
