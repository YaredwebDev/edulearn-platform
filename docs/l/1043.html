Word formation — compound words with hyphens & prepositions

  • Hyphenated compounds: commanders-in-chief, editors-in-chief, check-in, up-to-date, forty-four, well-being, brother-in-law.
  • ✋ Plural rule: the main noun takes the plural in hyphenated compounds: commanders-in-chief, editors-in-chief (not "commanders-in-chiefs").
  • Compounds with prepositions: after-life, back-dates, background, by-pass, downstairs, forehead, in-group, in-service.
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: In compounds like "commander-in-chief," the plural goes on the first word (commanders-in-chief). Watch the correct hyphen placement: up-to-date (not "uptodate"), check-in, forty-four.
