UNIT 4 · LESSON 8 OF 8 · GRADE 11 PHYSICS

🧮 Key facts to memorise

◌ Not started
  • Four fundamental forces: strong, electromagnetic, weak, gravitational.
  • F_net = ma; 1 N = 1 kg·m/s²; weight W = mg (g ≈ 9.8 m/s²).
  • Newton's laws: 1st (inertia), 2nd (F=ma), 3rd (action–reaction on different bodies).
  • FN = mg (horizontal), FN = mg cos θ (incline); slide force = mg sin θ.
  • Friction: fs ≤ μs FN; fk = μk FN; μs > μk; μs = tan θ (limiting incline angle).
  • Equilibrium: ΣF = 0 (a = 0; may be moving at constant velocity).
  • Work W = F S cos θ; variable force = area under F–S graph; spring ½kx².
  • KE = ½mv²; PE_g = mgh; PE_spring = ½kx²; ME = KE+PE.
  • Work-energy theorem: W_net = ΔKE.
  • Power P = W/t = Fv; unit watt.
  • Conservation of ME: KE_i+PE_i = KE_f+PE_f (no non-conservative forces).
  • Momentum P = mv; Impulse J = FΔt = ΔP; F = ΔP/Δt.
  • Conservation of momentum: m1u1+m2u2 = m1v1+m2v2.
  • Elastic: momentum + KE conserved; inelastic: only momentum; perfectly inelastic: stick together.
  • Centre of mass: x_cm = Σmixi/Σmi.
  • Airbags/crumple zones reduce force by increasing impact time.

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) state the four fundamental forces and Newton's three laws; (2) use F=ma and W=mg; (3) solve incline problems (FN=mg cosθ, mg sinθ, friction); (4) solve Atwood machine, elevator-tension, and connected-mass problems with free-body diagrams; (5) state first condition of equilibrium and solve suspended-object/traffic-light problems; (6) compute work (positive/negative/zero), KE, PE, spring energy and power, and apply the work-energy theorem; (7) solve problems with friction dissipating mechanical energy; (8) use conservation of mechanical energy (frictionless incline, spring); (9) compute momentum and impulse, apply FΔt=ΔP and conservation of momentum; (10) classify and solve elastic, inelastic and perfectly inelastic collisions, recoil and ballistic-pendulum problems; and (11) compute the centre of mass? Re-answer the end-of-unit questions (bathroom scale in elevator, incline critical angle θc=tan⁻¹μs, blocks in contact pushed by 18 N, conservation statements, momentum & KE comparisons, zigzag mountain path, centre of mass).
