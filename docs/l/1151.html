3.6 Applications

3.6.1 Compound Interest

Compound interest (Thm 3.5): A(t) = P(1 + r/n)^(nt)

where A(t)=amount after t years, P=principal, r=annual interest rate, n=compounds per year, t=years.

Continuously compounded: A(t) = Pe^(rt) (as n→∞).

Example: Birr 20,000 at 7% for 5 years:

  • annually: 20000(1.07)⁵ ≈ 28,051.03
  • semi-annually: 20000(1.035)¹⁰ ≈ 28,211.98
  • quarterly: 20000(1.0175)²⁰ ≈ 28,295.56
  • monthly: ≈ 28,352.51
  • daily: ≈ 28,380.40

More compounding → slightly higher amount.

Compound interest growth

3.6.2 Population Growth

P(t) = P₀e^(kt), k>0 (exponential growth), P₀ initial population, k growth rate.

Example: P₀=110 million, k=2.3%=0.023.

  • In 2018 (t=5): P(5)=110,000,000·e^(0.115) ≈ 123,406,078.
  • Doubling time: 2P₀ = P₀e^(0.023t) → e^(0.023t)=2 → 0.023t=ln2 → t=ln2/0.023 ≈ 30.14 years.

3.6.3 The pH Scale

pH = −log[H⁺] where [H⁺] is hydrogen-ion concentration in moles/liter.

  • pH=7 neutral; pH<7 acidic; pH>7 basic.

Example: Blood [H⁺]=4.53×10⁻⁸ → pH=−[log4.53 + log10⁻⁸]=−[0.6561−8]=7.344>7 → basic.

Example: Acid rain pH=2.4 → log[H⁺]=−2.4 → [H⁺]=antilog(−2.4)=3.98×10⁻³ M.

✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Compound interest A=P(1+r/n)^(nt) (→Pe^(rt) continuous); population P=P₀e^(kt); pH=−log[H⁺]. These are the three main application formulas.
