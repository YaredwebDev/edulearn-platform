5.7 Magnetic Force Between Two Parallel Current-Carrying Wires

◌ Not started

Two parallel current-carrying wires produce magnetic fields that interact, producing a force between them. The magnitude of the force on each wire is equal but opposite in direction.

  • Same direction currents → wires ATTRACT.
  • Opposite direction currents → wires REPEL.

✍️ Must-know: Parallel wires: same-direction currents attract; opposite-direction currents repel.
