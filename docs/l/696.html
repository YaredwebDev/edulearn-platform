5.2 Simple Machines at Home

◌ Not started
  • Simple machines reduce the amount of force required to do work; they change the direction or magnitude of a force and offer mechanical advantage.
  • They are the building blocks of more complex machines. Break a complex machine down to its smallest parts and you find simple machines: pulleys, wheels, levers, wedges, and screws.
  • Functions: change direction, multiply speed/distance, multiply force.
