15.2 Nutrients and Their Functions

◌ Not started

Six main nutrient classes: carbohydrates, lipids (fats & oils), proteins, vitamins, minerals, water. Main functions: build & maintain body cells, regulate body functions, provide energy. - Carbohydrate, fat/lipid, protein provide energy; protein for body building & repair; minerals & vitamins regulate body function & defend against disease; water for optimal hydration.

15.2.1 Carbohydrates

  • Sugars, starches or fibres providing energy to all cells/tissues. 1 g carbohydrate → 4 kilocalories. Most calories in human diets come from carbohydrates. Should be 45–65% of daily calories. Mainly as starches & sugars. Free sugar limited to 5% of total energy in children, 10% in adults.

15.2.2 Lipids

  • All fats & oils in the diet ("oil" = liquid, "fat" = solid at room temperature). Concentrated energy source: 1 g fat/oil → 9 kilocalories. Fat should be 20–35% of daily calories. Digestion yields fatty acids & glycerol. Fats make diet more palatable; store energy & play structural roles; excess calories (from CHO, protein, lipid) stored as fat. Best fats are plant-based uncooked oils.

15.2.3 Proteins

  • Ensure growth & development of muscles, bones, hair, skin; form antibodies & hormones; fuel source when needed. 1 g protein → 4 kilocalories. Should be 10–35% of daily calories. Sources: red meat, eggs, dairy, fish; beans, legumes, soy, nuts, grains.
  • Made of amino acids. Complete proteins contain all 20 amino acids required (animal products: eggs, milk) — supply amino acids the body can't make. Incomplete proteins from plant foods lack some required amino acids, but still valuable in a healthy diet.

15.2.4 Vitamins

  • Boost the immune system, strengthen teeth & bones, aid calcium absorption, maintain healthy skin, metabolise proteins & carbohydrates, help brain & nervous system. Two groups:
  • Fat-soluble (stored, needn't be eaten daily): vitamins A, D, E, K.
  • Water-soluble (not stored, eaten more frequently): vitamin B & C.
  • Vegetables, fruits & meat are rich sources of all vitamins. Restricted diets may need supplements.

15.2.5 Minerals

  • Second type of micronutrient; two groups:
  • Major/macro-minerals — RDA >150 mg/day: magnesium, calcium, phosphorus, potassium. Help balance water, healthy skin/hair/nails, bone health.
  • Trace minerals — microgram quantities; catalytic role in enzymes; RDA <150 mg/day: iron, selenium, zinc, manganese. Strengthen bones, prevent tooth decay, reduce blood clotting, carry oxygen, support immunity.
  • Sources: iodized table salt, nuts & seeds, vegetables, leafy greens, fruits, poultry, egg yolks, whole grains, fortified bread & cereals.

15.2.6 Water

  • A person survives only a few days without water; slight dehydration → headaches, impaired physical & mental function. Body is mostly water; every cell needs water. Flushes toxins, absorbs shock, transports nutrients, prevents constipation, lubricates & rehydrates. Best: natural, unsweetened water.
