6.8 Impact of Traffic Accidents on Wild and Domestic Animals

◌ Not started
  • Road traffic accidents are a huge global public-health/development problem. Developing countries (incl. Ethiopia) account for many global road-traffic deaths.
  • Cars are also a major cause of death for both wild and domestic animals, including those in/near conservation areas crossing highways. Road ecology studies show car accidents are a major cause of wildlife population reduction.
  • Example: a 2017 study by Addisu Asefa et al. in Geralle National Park found 29% of wildlife mortality was due to car accidents.
  • Ways to reduce accidents on animals: design structures that allow safe animal passage, construct underpasses to promote habitat connectivity & natural movement, consider natural habitats when roads are built, and drive slowly.
