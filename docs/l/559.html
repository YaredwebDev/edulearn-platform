6.5 Applications

◌ Not started

Bucket (frustum of cone): bottom radius 10, top radius 13, slant height 20. - height h=√(20²−3²)=√391. Volume=⅓π·√391·(13²+10²+130)=⅓π·399√391≈8.26 liters (1 L=1000 cm³). - LSA=20π(13+10)=460π; plus bottom π(10²)=100π → TSA=560π≈1758.4 cm². - Cost at 50 Birr/100cm² = 1758.4·50/100 = 879.2 Birr.

Water tank (cylinder): hold 100,000 L=100 m³, height 8 m → πr²·8=100 → r≈2 m. Paint lateral surface (2cm thickness) → volume of paint = πh(R²−r²); painter cost = LSA × rate.

✍️ Must-know: 1 liter = 1000 cm³. Apply the frustum/cylinder formulas to real objects (bucket, tank, pipe, cone).
