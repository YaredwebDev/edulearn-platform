6.3 Propagation of Waves and Energy Transmission

  • Wave: a disturbance propagating in space or matter.
  • Mechanical waves: require a material medium to travel (vibrations of particles of the medium). Examples: sound waves, water waves, waves in strings.
  • Electromagnetic waves: do NOT require a medium; composed of vibrating electric and magnetic fields; travel through a vacuum. Examples: light, radio waves, X-rays.

By direction of vibration:

  • Transverse wave: direction of propagation is perpendicular to the direction of particle vibrations. Examples: all electromagnetic waves, waves on strings.
  • Longitudinal wave: direction of propagation is parallel to the direction of particle vibrations. Examples: sound waves, pressure waves.
  • Produces compressions (particles pushed together) and rarefactions (particles move apart). Compressions = longitudinal version of a crest; rarefactions = version of a trough.
