9A Listening Skills: Indigenous Conflict Resolution

◌ Not started

9A.1 The concept

  • Listening points (true/false): conflict is not confined to only some human relations (it's an inevitable part of human life); absence of conflict does not signal meaningful interaction; different societies resolve conflicts differently; conflicting parties can be individuals, groups, or societies.
  • Some Ethiopian societies & their resolution systems: Silte, Afar, Amhara, Shinasha (Sinasha), Gambela, Gamo — identify which resolution type each uses (Gadaa among Oromo, Shimgillina among Amhara, etc.).

9A.2 The role

  • True/false: conflicts can sometimes be solved without government intervention; indigenous resolution (not copying western governance) sustains Ethiopian life; elderly intervention success is over 60%; people often prefer indigenous over modern; indigenous resolution comes in non-violent social situations.
  • Factors affecting indigenous conflict resolution: modernity, civilization, lack of proper documentation. Discuss criteria for success/failure and why indigenous resolution is needed.
