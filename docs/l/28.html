5.3 Nutrient Requirements of Farm Animals

◌ Not started
  • Nutrients = elements/compounds/groups used for nourishment & performance. Basic nutrients: carbohydrates, fats, proteins, minerals, vitamins, water.
  • Nutrient requirement = minimum amount needed for maintenance (no net gain/loss), growth, reproduction, lactation, work and good health.
  • Water: >half of an animal's body; vital for digestion, blood circulation, waste elimination; medium in which nutrients dissolve; regulates temperature. Fresh, clean water essential.
  • Carbohydrates: main energy source in feed; from grains (wheat, maize, sorghum), crop residues, molasses, forages, hay.
  • Fats: storehouse of energy; insulate body. Low in pasture; oilseed cakes up to ~10% fats/oils. Energy for growth/activity mainly from carbohydrates & fats.
  • Proteins: polymers of amino acids (building blocks); essential for growth, maintenance, reproduction, tissue formation; plant (oilseed cakes, pulses, legumes) or animal (fish meal, meat scraps) origin. No protein storage in the body → must be supplied daily; excess is deaminated and converted to fat.
  • Minerals: inorganic elements in small amounts; ensure proper body functions, bones/teeth/tissues, regulate chemical processes. Macro (needed in large amounts): Ca, P, Mg, K, Na. Micro/trace (small amounts): Cu, I, Fe, Mn, Zn. Deficiency → metabolic disorders; excess → toxicity.
  • Vitamins: organic, small amounts; for metabolism, growth, reproduction, red-cell maturation, digestion, bone/teeth formation, immunity. Fat-soluble: A, D, E, K. Water-soluble: B and C (cannot be stored → needed daily).

✋ Trap (nutrient deficiency signs): Carbohydrates → reduced feed intake, low weight gain, prolonged fattening, drop in milk. Fats → poor skin/hair coat, inability to maintain pregnancy, poor fat-soluble-vitamin absorption. Protein → low weight gain, stunted growth, poor product quality. Minerals → reduced fodder intake, chewing/suckling wood & metal, stiff joints, weakened bones/teeth, drop in milk. Vitamins → rough/loose hair coat, coughing, nasal discharge, watery eyes, diarrhoea, staggering, scaly skin, pneumonia. Water → reduced feed intake, low weight gain & milk.
