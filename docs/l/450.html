7B Speaking Skills

◌ Not started

7B.1 Asking for & giving permission

  • Common expressions: Can I…? / Could I…? / Do you mind if…? / Is it okay if…? / I wonder if I could…? / Would you mind if…? Giving: Sure, no problem; Go ahead; Yes, that would be possible; Yes, it is fine. Refusing politely: No, sorry. I need it. Practise in dialogues.

7B.2 Expressing praise

  • Giving praise: Good job! Well done! Fantastic! Perfect! That's great! Nice work! Excellent! That's really remarkable.
  • Receiving praise: How kind of you to say so; It's nice of you to say so; Thank you so much; Thanks a lot; Thanks for your compliment; I'm glad you like it.
  • Maxims of good praise — the three S's: be Sincere, be Specific, be Spontaneous (praise as soon as you notice something good). Praise is timely & sincere; responding to praise keeps reciprocity in conversation.

7B.3 Public speaking

  • Deliver a ~10-minute speech on "Heroic deeds of Ethiopian patriots at the Battle of Adwa." Gather information from elderly people/veterans/officials; organise into sections; practise; use visuals; prepare to answer questions.

7B.4 Debate

  • Debate proposition: Patriotism is declining in different walks of life of Ethiopians.
  • Generate facts/figures for and against; frame ideas for your position; use connectors (similarly, on the same note, in agreement with, contrarily, in support of, to back the argument, equally important, nevertheless, with this in mind, provided that, for example, all things considered, given these points).
  • Sample opposing idea table: supporting (widespread selfishness, ethno-centricity, internal conflicts) vs opposing (presence of generous people / strong national feeling, poverty reduction, athletics victory).
