1.1 Reading: Life in a Big City

(the core "story"/text of the unit)

The unit opens by contrasting life in a big city with life in the countryside / small town. Be ready to answer questions about the differences and about the vocabulary in context.

Key ideas of the passage:

  • A big city is busy, crowded, fast-paced and bustling — full of noise, movement and activity.

  • Life in a small town or village is quieter, slower, closer to nature and more peaceful.

  • City life offers opportunities (jobs, schools, services) but also problems (traffic, pollution, overcrowding).

✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Reading-comprehension questions test (a) comparison of city vs rural life, (b) cause–effect (crowding → problems), and (c) the vocabulary in context below. Learn each word with its meaning and a sentence.
