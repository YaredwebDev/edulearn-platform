⚡ Quick Revision Box (Unit 2)

  1. Seven fundamental quantities: length (m), mass (kg), time (s), temperature (K), current (A), amount of substance (mol), luminous intensity (cd).
  2. Meter = light in vacuum in 1/299792458 s; second = 9 192 631 770 vibrations of caesium-133; kg = Planck constant based.
  3. Add/subtract → fewest decimal places; multiply/divide → fewest sig figs.
  4. 1 km = 1000 m; 1 m = 100 cm = 1000 mm; 1 cm = 10 mm.
  5. 1 hour = 3600 s; 1 day = 24 hr; 1 tonne = 1000 kg; 1 quintal = 100 kg.
  6. Scalar: distance, mass, time, temperature, energy. Vector: displacement, velocity, acceleration, force.
  7. Derived: speed (m/s), density (kg/m³), force (N = kg·m/s²), work (J), pressure (Pa).
  8. 1 km/hr = 10/36 m/s ≈ 0.278 m/s.
  9. Lab: never work alone; never taste chemicals; report all accidents.
  10. Prefixes: k=10³, c=10⁻², m=10⁻³, µ=10⁻⁶, n=10⁻⁹, G=10⁹, T=10¹².
