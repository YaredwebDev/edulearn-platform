UNIT 1 · LESSON 6 OF 6 · GRADE 11 BIOLOGY

🧮 Key ideas to memorise

◌ Not started
  • Biology + Technology = biotechnology (uses biological systems to make products; yeasts → bread/beer)
  • Nature-inspired pairings: nest←swallow, weaving←spider web, building climate←termite mound (East Gate Center, Harare), aircraft←birds/bats, camera←human eye, needle←mosquito proboscis, sensors←neurons, saw←teeth, bulletproof vest←spider silk, ceramics←shellfish exoskeleton, robotic arm←elephant trunk, bullet train←kingfisher beak
  • Devices: thermometer (temperature), urine test (HCG → pregnancy), diabetic test (blood glucose), HIV test, microscope (magnify), CT/PET/CITS (disease/cancer imaging), GPS (geographic data), body-fat calculator
  • Biology study fields: biochemical, biomedical, biophysical, environmental, bioinformatics, biogeographical
  • Technology's two main harms: pollution and depletion of natural resources
  • Plants: primary producers, decentralised function, ethical to care/plant
  • Animals: sentient → 3 Rs = Reduction, Refinement, Replacement
  • Ethical issues include organ commercialisation, assisted reproduction, manipulation of body systems

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) give 6+ concrete examples of technology imitating nature with the exact natural source; (2) define biology, technology, biotechnology and synthetic biology; (3) state the benefits of biology to technology and the fields that use technology in biology; (4) name the function of each listed device (thermometer, HCG urine test, diabetic/HIV test, microscope, CT/PET/CITS, GPS, body-fat calculator); (5) list positive AND negative impacts of biology and technology (effluents, pesticides/fertilisers → toxicity/biohazards); (6) define ethical issues in biology and list examples; (7) distinguish ethical vs unethical treatment of plants; and (8) explain the three Rs (Reduction, Refinement, Replacement)? Also re-answer the unit-one review: true/false (e.g. #7 "Care for plants is unethical" is false; #8 replacement with biosynthetic tissue is true) and the MCQs (biotechnology→biological systems; manufacturing biological elements→synthetic biology; geographic data→GPS; sum of techniques→technology; thermometer; effluents; fertilisers→toxicity/biohazards; designing from nature→imitation; plants→decentralised systems).
