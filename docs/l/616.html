1.1 Importance of Physics to Society

◌ Not started

Physics provides the basic understanding needed to improve our quality of life. The textbook asks: What is the benefit of physics for society? and Which technologies are directly related to physics? - In transport: automobiles, airplanes and space shuttles could not be built without physics. - In health: new instrumentation & techniques such as computer tomography (CT), magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), positron emission tomography (PET), ultrasonic imaging, and laser surgery. - In electronics & technology: electricity, electronics, machines and most modern technology rest on physics principles. - In the economy and military: physics informs technology used in industry; it also has military uses (e.g., the design of weapons — many of the scientists behind the atomic bomb were physicists, and physicists today help create nuclear weapons).

✍️ Must-know: physics underpins medicine (CT, MRI, PET, ultrasound, laser surgery), transport (cars, planes, space shuttles), electronics, and even military/nuclear technology.
