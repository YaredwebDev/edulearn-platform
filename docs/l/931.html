⚡ Quick Revision Box (Unit 5)

  1. Energy values: carbohydrate 16 kJ/g, protein 17 kJ/g, lipid 37 kJ/g.
  2. Marasmus = carbohydrate deficiency; Kwashiorkor = protein deficiency; Anemia = iron; Rickets = vitamin D; Scurvy = vitamin C.
  3. BMI = weight(kg)/height²(m²); ≥30 = obese; <18.5 = underweight.
  4. Cigarette: tar (bronchitis, cancer), carbon monoxide (binds hemoglobin), nicotine (addictive).
  5. Alcohol and heroin = depressants; cannabis = THC; khat = cathinone.
  6. Wasting = low weight-for-height; stunting = low height-for-age; underweight = low weight-for-age.
  7. HIV infects T-lymphocytes; malaria caused by Plasmodium, vector = Anopheles mosquito; TB caused by Mycobacterium tuberculosis.
  8. Endemic (local, always present) vs epidemic (sudden, area) vs pandemic (worldwide, COVID-19).
  9. Vitamin D made by skin in sunlight; only vitamin the body can make.
  10. Fiber prevents constipation, keeps colon healthy (not an energy source).
  11. Non-infectious diseases: sickle cell anemia, cancer, allergies, diabetes, podoconiosis.
