7.1 Reading: Community Service Experience

(the core "story"/text of the unit)

The passage tells the story of a student who did community service — visiting and helping elderly people at Felege Hiwot Hospital in Bahir Dar.

Key ideas:

  • The narrator and classmates volunteered to help the elderly in hospital.

  • They were dedicated and devoted to the task.

  • They felt fortunate and bettered (improved) through the experience.

  • Service gave them a sense of purpose and improved the lives of others.

✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Questions test (a) where the service happened (Felege Hiwot Hospital, Bahir Dar), (b) who was helped (the elderly), (c) how the volunteers felt, and (d) vocabulary in context.
