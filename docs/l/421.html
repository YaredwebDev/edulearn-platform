2C Reading Skills: Features of Contemporary Civilization

◌ Not started
  • A civilization = a complex human society, usually of different cities, with characteristics of cultural & technological development. The word relates to Latin civitas = "city", so its most basic definition is "a society made up of cities". In the past anthropologists used "civilization/civilized" ethnocentrically — judging their own culture superior and others "savage/barbaric/backward"; this is why defining it is contested/troublesome.
  • Criteria anthropologists agree on: urban settlements (not nomadic); division of labour (specialisation) → class structure & government; a surplus of food (from tools for growing crops); writing, trade, artwork & monuments, and development of science & technology.
  • Not all meet every criterion — e.g. the Incan Empire had government, social hierarchy, art and architecture but no written language — yet is considered a civilization.

✍️ Reading skills practised: answer in your own words; determine purpose/main idea; identify pronoun references ("they", "this", "his/her", "it"); infer vocabulary from context (designation, contested, anthropologist, inferior, savage, flux, surplus, hierarchy). Know the vocabulary-learning strategies.
