🧮 Key Equations & facts to Memorise

◌ Not started
  • Dipole moment μ = δ × d; 1 D = 3.33564 × 10⁻³⁰ C·m
  • Bond order B.O. = ½[(# bonding e⁻) − (# antibonding e⁻)]
  • Lattice energy: U = ΔfH° − (ΔH_sub + bond/½ terms + IE + EA) [Born–Haber]
  • U ∝ (q₁q₂)/r
  • H₂ bond length ≈ 74 pm; valence-electron counts (NF₃ 26, CO₃²⁻ 24, NO₂⁻ 18)
  • H₂O μ = 1.87 D; cis-C₂H₂Cl₂ μ = 1.89 D; CO₂, CCl₄, trans-C₂H₂Cl₂ μ = 0
  • Melting points: Li 180 vs Be 1287 °C; gallium 29.8 °C
  • % s character: sp 50%, sp² 33⅓%, sp³ 25%
