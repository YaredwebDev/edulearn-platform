6.3 Naming alcohols (IUPAC)

◌ Not started
  • Common name: alkyl group name + "alcohol" (methyl alcohol, ethyl alcohol, isopropyl alcohol).
  • IUPAC: replace the terminal "e" of the alkane with "ol" (methane → methanol; ethane → ethanol). For chains of 3+, number the longest chain containing –OH from the end nearest the –OH; give the position of –OH and substituents. Examples: propan-1-ol vs propan-2-ol; 3-methylbutan-1-ol (careful: numbering from –OH end).
  • Polyhydric: keep the "e" of the alkane and add "ol" with a multiplicative prefix: 1,2-ethanediol; 1,2,3-propanetriol.
  • General formula for monohydric series: CnH2n+1OH.
