📝 Writing focus: Short Report

◌ Not started

A short report is a factual piece that: - Has a title. - States what was observed/done, when/where, and the findings/results. - Uses factual, clear language and past tense. - Ends with a short conclusion or recommendation.

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) name 4 Ethiopian national parks and one fact about each, (2) define all 10+ vocabulary words with a sentence, (3) use 8+ phrasal verbs (burn/call/calm/care/carry), (4) add the prefixes ante-/anti-/en-/dis-/un-, (5) write zero and first conditionals correctly, (6) structure a short report?
