UNIT 5 · LESSON 7 OF 7 · GRADE 11 AGRICULTURE

🧮 Key facts to memorise

◌ Not started
  • Feed cost = 50–80% of operating cost; natural pasture supplies >60% of feed; roughages >50% of feed fed; crop residues supply greatest volume.
  • Feeds: roughages (>18% crude fibre, <60% TDN, bulky, less digestible) vs concentrates (<18% crude fibre, >60% TDN, highly digestible). Protein-rich concentrate >18% CP.
  • Dry roughage (hay, straw, stover, bagasse) vs succulent roughage (pasture, silage).
  • Energy-rich concentrates: cereal grains, roots/tubers, bran, middlings, molasses. Protein-rich: oilseed cakes, soybean/cottonseed cake, fish/meat/blood meal.
  • Nutrients: carbohydrates (energy), fats (energy store, insulation), proteins (amino acids, no storage → daily), minerals (macro Ca/P/Mg/K/Na; micro Cu/I/Fe/Mn/Zn), vitamins (fat-soluble A,D,E,K; water-soluble B,C), water.
  • Water > half of body; maintenance ration ≈ half the feed.
  • Hay: dry to 15–20% moisture (curing); bales or tripod.
  • Silage: anaerobic fermentation; lactic acid lowers pH ≤4; 2–4 weeks; feed to ruminants only.
  • Compound feed: cereals + agro-industrial by-products + premixes; quality control.
  • Pearson Square: differences across diagonal → portions; for 100 kg maize+SBM@16%: 80 kg maize + 20 kg SBM.

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) list Ethiopia's four feed resources and their relative importance; (2) classify a set of feeds into roughages vs concentrates (dry vs succulent; energy-rich vs protein-rich concentrates); (3) name the six basic nutrients with sources, functions and deficiency signs, and distinguish macro vs micro minerals and fat- vs water-soluble vitamins; (4) define maintenance vs production ration and give the characteristics of a good ration; (5) balance a ration for two feeds using the Pearson Square method (including a full numerical example); and (6) describe hay making (curing to 15–20% moisture), silage making (anaerobic, pH≤4, 2–4 weeks) and the main stages of compound-feed manufacturing? Re-answer the review too (most common feed = natural pasture; straw is a crop residue not an agro-industrial by-product; Pearson Square is the well-known balancing method; minerals & vitamins are the micronutrients; animal nutrition = science of feed prep & feeding; molasses is not a roughage; roughages are NOT highly digestible; hay's shortcoming = weather dependence).
