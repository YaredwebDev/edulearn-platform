1.3 Impacts of Biology and Technology on Society and the Natural World

◌ Not started

Impacts = factors that pose positive or negative effects on society and the natural world. Biological information and technological devices can strongly influence/control both.

1.3.1 Impacts of biology on society & nature (effects from advancing biological knowledge)

  • Ensuring food security (increased productivity).
  • Medicine & disease treatment → improved health and longevity.
  • Better supply of energy and clean water.
  • Increased industrial production through microbial action.
  • Creating antibiotics to treat bacterial infections.
  • Devices made from biological materials to serve/influence/treat/control society and nature (positively or negatively).
  • Negative: production and use of biological weapons destroys the natural world; imposing a predetermined limit to have only boys (XY males) / avoid girls (XX females) threatens society & nature → loss of females.

1.3.2 Impacts of technology on society & nature

Technology has solved many societal problems, but industrialisation and technological advancement also cause misuse and destruction of the environment in two main ways: pollution and depletion of natural resources. - Negative examples: industrial emissions & effluents pollute clean air and drinking water; biochemical agents, pesticides and fertilizers cause toxicity and biohazards.

✍️ Must-know: industrial wastes = effluents (MCQ); soil fertilizers can cause toxicity/biohazards; technology damages the world mainly through pollution and depletion of natural resources.

Inquiry 1.4: invite health & agriculture professionals to discuss the impacts of biology and technology and their positive/negative effects.
