UNIT 4 · LESSON 3 OF 6 · GRADE 11 CHEMISTRY

Conditions needed for a chemical reaction

◌ Not started

Collision theory: chemical reactions take place when reactant particles collide. Rate ∝ number of collisions per second (collision frequency). But not every collision leads to reaction — a collision is productive only if it is effective: 1. Proper orientation — the reacting parts must meet with the correct geometry; otherwise the collision is ineffective. 2. Sufficient energy — activation energy (E_a) — the collision must supply at least the minimum energy needed to break bonds in reactants and form new bonds in products. E_a is that minimum energy. 3. Raising the temperature raises the average kinetic energy → more frequent and more energetic (effective) collisions → faster reaction.

✍️ Must-know: effective collision requires BOTH enough energy (≥ activation energy) AND proper orientation. An ineffective collision occurs when energy is too low or orientation is wrong.
