6.4 Phrasal Verbs: clear, come, clog, close

◌ Not started
Phrasal verb Meaning Example
clear up to become clear; to solve/explain The sky cleared up after the rain.
come down to decrease; to descend Prices came down last month.
come about to happen; to occur How did the change come about?
clog up to block; to become blocked Rubbish can clog up the drains.
come across to find by chance; to give an impression I came across an old photo.
come apart to break into pieces The old book came apart.
close down to stop operating The factory closed down.
come back to return He came back from the market.
close off to block the entrance to Police closed off the road.

✋ Trap: come down = decrease/descend; come about = happen; come across = find by chance (or "give the impression of being"). close down = cease operating.
