1.2 Vocabulary of the Unit (matric-ready)

◌ Not started

Key vocabulary from the "Life in a Big City" passage:

Word Meaning Example
pleasant enjoyable; giving pleasure City parks make urban life more pleasant.
reap to receive/get (as a benefit or result); harvest Farmers reap what they sow.
runs (here) to last/continue; operates The bus service runs all day.
settled made one's permanent home; calm/established They settled in the city after years of travelling.
hustle busy, noisy activity; to push/shove quickly The morning hustle on the streets is normal in Addis Ababa.
bustling full of busy activity; lively and crowded The market is bustling with people.
impure not clean; mixed with something bad Impure water can cause disease.
oodles (informal) a very large amount; lots The city has oodles of entertainment.
precious of great value; loved/important Fresh air is a precious resource in a crowded city.

✍️ Must-know: "reap" is often used figuratively — you reap a benefit or reap the consequences of your actions. "oodles" is informal and means "a huge amount."
