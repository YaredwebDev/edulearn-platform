6.2 Sum of Exterior Angles of a Convex Polygon

◌ Not started

Definition: An exterior angle is the angle outside the polygon formed by one side and the extension of an adjacent side.

Sum of the exterior angles of any polygon = 360°.

Reasoning: interior + exterior at each vertex = 180° (a straight line), so (sum of interior) + (sum of exterior) = n×180°. Since sum of interior = (n−2)×180°, the exterior sum = n×180° − (n−2)×180° = 360°.

Example: A triangle with interior angles 60°, 40°, and 80° has exterior angles 120°, 140°, 100°; their sum is 360°.

✍️ Must-know: The sum of exterior angles is always 360° regardless of the number of sides.
