3.3 Word Formation: Conversion

Conversion = using a word as a different part of speech without changing its form (sometimes with a spelling change).

Noun → Verb:

  • advice (n) → advise (v)
  • report (n) → report (v)
  • examination (n) → examine (v)
  • revision (n) → revise (v)
  • repeat (n) → repeat (v)
  • produce (n) → produce (v)
  • clarity (n) → clarify (v)
  • maintain (n) → maintain (v)

Adjective → Verb: pronounceable→pronounce; maximum→maximise; sensitive→sensitise; reason→reason; define.

Adjective → Noun: dangerous→danger; childish→childishness; national→nation/nationality; confident→confidence; functional→function.

✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Conversion changes a word's part of speech (often adding/altering a suffix). Be ready to supply the noun, verb, or adjective form: e.g. advise/advice, examine/examination, confident/confidence, national/nation/nationality.
