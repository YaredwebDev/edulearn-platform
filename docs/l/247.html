4.2.1 Mitosis

◌ Not started

Mitosis = a type of nuclear division where duplicated chromosomes of a single mother cell are distributed between two identical daughter cells, having the same number and kind of chromosomes as the parent nucleus.

  • A diploid (2n) mother cell gives rise to two diploid (2n) identical daughter cells.
  • Term coined by Walther Flemming (1882).
  • Occurs in somatic (body) cells.

Stages of mitosis (Karyokinesis)

  1. Prophase (first phase): each duplicated chromosome (two sister chromatids) pairs up; nuclear membrane breaks down, nucleolus disappears; chromosomes shorten/thicken and become visible; centrosomes move to opposite poles and spindle fibres emerge.
  2. Metaphase (arrangement phase): spindle fully developed; chromosomes line up along the metaphase plate (equatorial plane); sister chromatids attach to spindle fibres from opposite poles.
  3. Anaphase (migrating phase): cohesion proteins (centromere) break down; sister chromatids are pulled apart to opposite poles by the mitotic spindle.
  4. Telophase (reverse of prophase): nuclear membrane reappears around each set of chromosomes (two new nuclei); mitotic spindle breaks down.

Cytokinesis

  • Animal cells: cytoplasm pinches/constricts in the middle → two daughter cells separate.
  • Plant cells: hard cell wall prevents pinching; a new wall (middle lamella) is laid down between the two daughter cells.

Importance of mitosis: growth, repair and replacement of dead/damaged cells; unicellular organisms increase population; after fertilization the zygote (2n) develops into a multicellular organism by repeated mitotic division (cleavage: 2-cell → 4-cell → 8-cell → 16-cell stages).

Key terms: - Spindle fibre: long protein fibre from the centrioles; pulls separated chromosomes toward the poles during anaphase. - Homologous chromosomes: paired maternal and paternal chromosomes (represent a parent for a unit character); humans have 23 pairs (46 total) in diploid body cells. - Sister chromatids: two copies of one chromosome linked at the centromere.

✍️ Must-know: Mitosis = 2 identical diploid daughter cells; used for growth and repair; PMAT (Prophase, Metaphase, Anaphase, Telophase).
