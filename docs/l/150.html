5.6 Human reproductive systems

◌ Not started

Function: produce & deposit sperms (male) and egg cells (female). Internal fertilization by sexual intercourse: the penis is inserted into the vagina and sperm pass through the cervix into the uterus and fallopian tubes to fertilize the ovum.

Male reproductive system (external genitalia: penis, testes, scrotum; internal: prostate, vas deferens, urethra): - Penis — the copulatory organ with urinary & sexual functions (erection & ejaculation). Erection = blood flows into the corpus cavernosa & corpus spongiosum; arteries dilate, veins compress → blood "trapped." - Urethra — tube from bladder to the meatus; carries urine and (in males) expels semen during ejaculation. - Scrotum — sack of thick skin protecting the testes and controlling temperature for sperm creation. - Testes — oval pair in the scrotum producing testosterone and sperm (spermatogenesis).

Male structures & functions (Table 5.4): | Structure | Function | |---|---| | testes | produce sperm cells; produce testosterone | | seminiferous tubules | produce immature sperm cells | | epididymis | matures & stores sperm in coiled tubules | | vas deferens | carries sperm from epididymis to the urethra junction | | seminal vesicle | secretes fructose (energy for sperm) into semen | | prostate gland | secretes alkaline buffer (protects sperm from acidic vagina) | | Cowper's gland | secretes mucus-rich fluid protecting sperm from acids in the urethra | | urethra | carries semen during ejaculation; carries urine out | | penis | deposits sperm into the vagina; contains the urethra |

Female reproductive system (ovaries, fallopian tubes, uterus, vagina, accessory glands, external genitalia) functions: produce eggs, secrete sex hormones (estrogen), provide a site for fertilization, gestate a fetus, and give birth. - External: labia majora (large lips; enclose/protect; hair & glands at puberty), labia minora (small lips; surround openings to vagina & urethra), Bartholin's glands (near vaginal opening; secrete mucus), clitoris (small sensitive protrusion comparable to the penis; covered by the prepuce; can become erect). - Internal: vagina (canal joining cervix to outside; the birth canal), uterus/womb (hollow pear-shaped organ housing the fetus; parts = cervix [lower, opens into vagina] + corpus [main body]), ovaries (small oval glands each side producing eggs & hormones), fallopian tubes (carry ova from ovary to uterus; site of fertilization).

Female structures & functions (Table 5.5): | Structure | Function | |---|---| | ovaries | produce estrogen & progesterone; site of ova development & ovulation | | fallopian tubes (oviducts) | carry the ovum to the uterus; usually site of fertilization | | fimbria | sweep the ovum into the oviduct after ovulation | | uterus (womb) | pear-shaped organ where embryo/fetus develops; involved in menstruation | | cervix | separates vagina from uterus; holds fetus in place; dilates at birth | | vagina | extends cervix→outside; passage for sperm & menstrual flow; birth canal |

✍️ Must-know (review): NOT part of male system = uterus; NOT part of female system = sperm duct; male sex cells = sperm, made in the testes; female sex cell = egg.
