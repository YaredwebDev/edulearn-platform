5.16 Effects of Alcohol, Chewing Khat, Cannabis and Other Drug Use

◌ Not started

These affect STI transmission and unwanted pregnancy and damage the body.

5.5.1 Effects of alcohol

  • Normal blood-alcohol limit for euphoria = 0.1%; higher concentrations (0.25–0.30%) cause sleepiness & confusion; gulping more causes coma and death.
  • Prolonged excessive intake increases dementia; damages the brain (vitamin deficiencies) and liver (cirrhosis); causes "J-shaped legs," colorectal/colon cancer, violence, traffic accidents, death.
  • Alcoholic drinks cause ~6.2% of male and 1.1% of female deaths globally.
  • Alcohol drinking is associated with risky sex and HIV transmission.
  • Long-term: brain communication pathways disrupted (mood, thinking, coordination); cardiomyopathy, arrhythmias, stroke, high blood pressure; liver steatosis/hepatitis/fibrosis/cirrhosis; pancreatitis.

5.5.2 Effects of chewing Khat

  • Khat = shrub (Catha edulis); leaves chewed as a recreational stimulant (psychoactive) because they contain cathinone. Chewed in Ethiopia/Horn of Africa (in Eastern Ethiopia by ~30% of adolescent girls, >70% of boys); ~20 million users worldwide.
  • Immediate effects: alertness, arousal, concentration, confidence, constipation, euphoria, friendliness, hyperactivity, raised blood pressure & heart rate, insomnia, psychosis, suppressed appetite, talkativeness.
  • Long-term effects: depression, hallucinations, impaired inhibition, increased risk of myocardial infarction (heart attack), psychosis, oral cancer leading to death; hypertension, stomatitis, esophagitis, gastritis, constipation, acute coronary syndrome, diabetes, hepatitis, cirrhosis, impotency/loss of sexual drive (in male users); increased respiration, arrhythmias, dilated pupils, tooth decay & gum disease.

5.5.3 Effects of drug use

Drugs = biochemical components (mostly from plants); can affect the body/brain intermittently or cause permanent harm. - Marijuana — dried leaves/flowers of Cannabis sativa; harms the brain, breathing system, heart, child development & muscle coordination. - Cocaine — addictive stimulant from Erythroxylum coca; harms cardiovascular, breathing, gastrointestinal and nervous systems. - Heroin — very addictive, made from morphine (from the opium poppy Papaver somniferum). - Inhalants — household/workplace chemicals inhaled to get high; change brain function. - General side effects of drugs: higher risk of illness/infection; heart problems (abnormal rates, heart attacks, collapsed veins); nausea & abdominal pain → appetite/weight changes; liver strain/damage/failure; seizures, stroke, confusion, brain damage; memory/attention/decision-making problems; higher risk of injuries, accidents and domestic violence; high HIV/syphilis/genital-ulcer prevalence due to unprotected sex fuelled by drugs.

✍️ Must-know (review): gulping more alcohol causes coma and death; chewing khat causes hypertension/stomatitis/esophagitis/gastritis/constipation + acute coronary syndrome + metabolic disorders/impotency/diabetes/hepatitis/cirrhosis (answer: all); dangerous drugs = marijuana, cocaine, heroin (all).
