10.2 Vocabulary of the Unit (matric-ready)

◌ Not started

Vocabulary taken directly from the reading passage:

Word Meaning Example (context)
counter (¶1) the opposite point; a counter-argument For each positive aspect there is a negative counter-point.
vigorous (¶2) energetic; strong Gone are the days of vigorous handwriting practice.
legible clear enough to be read Assignments are now neat and legible.
scope (¶2) room/opportunity; range There is little scope for spelling errors.
pervasive (¶4) spreading widely through everything Computers are becoming pervasive in our lives.
caste (¶5) a social class/group based on birth It builds a global family instead of groups based on community and caste.
garner (¶6) to gather/collect; to gain People use the internet to garner support.
dexterity (¶7) skill of the hands; nimbleness Video games can improve reaction time and dexterity.
deteriorating (¶8) becoming worse Communicative skills are deteriorating.
boon (¶8) a benefit; a blessing (opposite: bane) Used wisely, the computer is a boon, not a bane.

✍️ Must-know: boon (blessing) vs bane (curse/source of harm) is a key contrast in the passage. deteriorating = getting worse. pervasive = widespread throughout.
