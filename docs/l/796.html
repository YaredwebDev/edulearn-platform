5.9 Fertilization and pregnancy

◌ Not started
  • Fertilization = union of egg & sperm in the ampulla of the fallopian tube to produce a zygote (23 + 23 chromosomes). The zygote divides as it moves to the uterus; ~6 days after fertilization the embryo attaches to the uterine lining (implantation), completed within ~9–10 days.
  • Pregnancy (gestation) = fetus developing in the womb; lasts about 40 weeks (280 days) from the last menstrual period. More than one fetus = multiple pregnancy (twins/triplets).
  • Signs & symptoms of pregnancy: missed periods, tender breasts, morning sickness (nausea/vomiting), hunger, frequent urination; confirmed by a pregnancy test.
