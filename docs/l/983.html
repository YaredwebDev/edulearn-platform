⚡ Quick Revision Box (Unit 1)

  1. Chemistry = properties, composition, structure, transformations + energy of substances.
  2. Five branches: Physical, Organic, Inorganic, Analytical, Biochemistry.
  3. Chemistry = central science.
  4. Overlaps: Biochemistry (bio), Geochemistry (geology), Chemical physics (physics), Medicinal chemistry (medicine).
  5. Drugs: analgesics = painkillers; antibiotics = infection; antiseptics = wounds; disinfectants = surfaces; anesthetics = operations; tranquilizers = mental calm.
  6. GERD = 6,450 MW on the Blue Nile, largest hydro project in Africa.
  7. Abijata Soda Ash produces trona; Ziway produces caustic soda (NaOH).
