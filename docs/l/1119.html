8.3 Phrasal Verbs: come (multiple)

Phrasal verbMeaningExample
came throughto survive/recover from; to succeedThe patient came through the surgery.
came into enter; to arrive (for a result)The results came in this morning.
came outto be published/revealed; to emergeThe report came out last week.
comes offto become detached; to happen successfullyThe label came off the bottle.
come up withto think of/produce (an idea)She came up with a good plan.
come overto visit; to move towardsPlease come over to my house.
come downto decrease; to move downwardsThe fever came down overnight.
came acrossto find by chance; to appearI came across the article online.
come onto begin; to hurry (encouragement)The pain came on suddenly.
Note: ✋ Trap: come up with = produce an idea; come through = survive/recover; come across = find by chance. Note the different tenses used (came/come) depending on the sentence.
