5.4 The axial and appendicular skeletons

◌ Not started
  • Humans are born with over 300 bones; many fuse by maturity, leaving on average 206 bones in the adult.
  • Bone = protein collagen + molecule calcium phosphate (strong, lightweight).
  • Four bone-cell types: Osteoblasts (form new bone), Osteocytes (mature bone cells), Osteoclasts (break down bone to shape it), Osteoprogenitor cells (repair fractures).
  • Types of bone by shape: Long (femur, tibia — longer than wide), Short (carpus/wrists — squat/cubed), Flat (cranium/skull, ilium/pelvis, sternum, ribs — flattened), Irregular (vertebrae, sacrum, coccyx, temporal, mandible, palatine, nasal), Sesamoid (small sesame-seed-like, embedded in muscles).
  • Functions of bones: (1) support body & help movement; (2) protect internal organs (ribs protect heart/lungs, skull protects brain); (3) produce blood cells (platelets, RBCs, WBCs in bone marrow); (4) store & release fat; (5) store & release minerals (calcium ~99%, phosphorus, vitamin D).

Axial skeleton = skull, trunk and pelvis of the medial core. Its 80 bones (Table 5.2): | Part | Bones | Function | |---|---|---| | Skull (cranium 8 + facial 14) | 22 | holds/protects the brain | | Ossicles (both ears, 3+3) | 6 | hearing (auditory) | | Hyoid (neck) | 1 | holds the head | | Thoracic cage (ribs) | 24 | protects heart & lungs | | Thorax (sternum) | 1 | binds/abdominal | | Vertebral column | 26 | holds up the body | | Total axial | 80 | |

Skull = cranium bones joined at joints (sutures) + facial bones (mandible/jaw, maxilla/upper jaw, zygomatic/cheekbone, nasal). Rib cage (thoracic cage) = 12 pairs of ribs + costal cartilage + sternum.

Appendicular skeleton = 126 bones — limbs, shoulder (pectoral) and pelvic girdles, and joined appendages (involved in locomotion & manipulation). Table 5.3: shoulder girdles 4, pelvis 2, arms & forearms 6, thighs & legs 8, hands 54, feet & ankles 52 = 126. Total = 80 + 126 = 206 bones.
