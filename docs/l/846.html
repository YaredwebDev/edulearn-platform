2.1 Characteristics of Plants

Plants share many characteristics with other living things but also have unique features:

CharacteristicDetail
Living thingsThey grow, reproduce, and respond to environmental changes
MulticellularMade of many eukaryotic cells with a well-defined nucleus and membrane-bound organelles
Rigid cell wallMade primarily of cellulose (in addition to the cell membrane)
Autotrophic (producers)Contain the green pigment chlorophyll to absorb sunlight and make their own food
SessileCannot move by themselves; anchored by roots (but leaves turn toward light, roots orient toward water)
Asexual & sexual reproductionLower plants (mosses, liverworts) reproduce asexually by spores; higher seed-plants (gymnosperms, angiosperms) reproduce sexually by gametes
