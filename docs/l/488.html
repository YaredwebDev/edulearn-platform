4.2 Vocabulary of the Unit (matric-ready)

◌ Not started

Key vocabulary from the "National Parks" passage:

Word Meaning Example
amazes causes wonder/surprise The scenery amazes every visitor.
stunning extremely beautiful/impressive The park has stunning landscapes.
scenery the natural features of a landscape The Bale Mountains offer lovely scenery.
extraordinary very unusual/remarkable It protects extraordinary wildlife.
heath an area of open low-growing shrubs The highland heath shelters birds.
inhabiting living in (a place) Animals inhabiting the park are protected.
expanse a large open area A vast expanse of grassland stretches ahead.
ferry a boat carrying passengers/vehicles across water We crossed the river by ferry.
peak the pointed top of a mountain We reached the peak of Ras Dejen.
soar to rise high/fly high The eagles soar over the valley.

✍️ Must-know: "soar" = rise quickly/high (birds, prices, temperatures). "peak" = mountain top; "expanse" = a wide open area.
