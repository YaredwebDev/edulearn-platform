5.3 Dynamic chemical equilibrium

◌ Not started
  • At the start the forward rate (rf) is high and the reverse rate (rr) is ~0; as the reaction runs, rf falls (reactants used) and rr rises (products form). Equilibrium is reached when rf = rr.
  • At equilibrium macroscopic properties (concentrations, pressure, temperature) no longer change, but the reaction continues at the molecular level — it is a dynamic equilibrium.
  • Equilibrium is only reached in a closed vessel.

Characteristics of equilibrium: - Reaction is reversible; no net change in concentrations; forward and reverse rates are equal; closed vessel; dynamic; concentrations of reactants and products are generally not equal (only the rates are equal).

✍️ Must-know: at equilibrium the rates of forward and reverse are equal — NOT necessarily the concentrations. Example: 2NO₂(g, dark brown) ⇌ N₂O₄(g, colourless) — the brown colour stops changing once equilibrium is set, yet NO₂ ⇌ N₂O₄ interconversion continues.
