12.1 What is Biodiversity?

◌ Not started
  • Biodiversity = the range of all forms of life on earth — plants, animals, humans, bacteria and other organisms — including the different species, sub-species and communities in a given habitat (e.g. rainforest, grassland).
  • Components of biodiversity:
  • the number of species (plants, animals, other organisms);
  • the genetic diversity of different species in an area;
  • the population of each species;
  • the diversity of habitats & landscapes (forests, grasslands, etc.).
  • An area's biodiversity shows how rich it is in total species, their populations and habitats.
  • Ethiopia's high geographic & climatic diversity → high diversity of plants/animals across agro-ecologies. Many are found nowhere else — called endemic plants/animals. How biodiversity is managed determines healthy development of species & ecosystems.
