2.5 Applications

◌ Not started

Example 1: 530 flower pots in rows of 21 → 530 = 21×25 + 5 → 25 rows, 5 pots left over.

Example 2: Cover a 50 cm × 20 cm board with largest square tiles → GCF(50,20) = 10 cm tiles (50 = 2·5², 20 = 2²·5, GCF = 2·5 = 10).

✍️ Must-know: Word problems with "largest equal pieces / rooms / teams" use GCF; problems with "when will they meet again / smallest common" use LCM.
