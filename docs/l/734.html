  1. Equations of motion: v = v₀+at, s = v₀t+½at², v² = v₀²+2as.
  2. Free fall: replace a→g (9.8 m/s²), s→h.
  3. s–t graph is a parabola; slope of tangent = instantaneous velocity.
  4. v–t graph slope = acceleration; area under = displacement.
  5. a–t graph is a horizontal line; area under = change in velocity.
  6. Relative velocity same direction = |v_A − v_B|; opposite = v_A + v_B.
  7. g is maximum at Earth's surface, decreases with altitude.
