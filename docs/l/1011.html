1.3 Phrasal Verbs: "fall"

Phrasal verbs = verb + particle (preposition/adverb) whose meaning often differs from the verb alone.

Phrasal verbMeaningExample
fall apartbreak into pieces; failHis plan fell apart at the last minute.
fall backmove back / retreatThe troops had to fall back.
fall back onrely on (as a last resource)I can fall back on my savings.
fall behindfail to keep upHe fell behind in his studies.
fall downdrop to the groundThe old building fell down.
fall forbe deceived by; fall in love withShe fell for his trick.
fall offdecrease; drop offSales fell off in winter.
fall outquarrel; lose hair/teethThey fell out over money.
fall throughfail to happenThe deal fell through.
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Distinguish the two senses: fall out = (1) quarrel, (2) hair/teeth come out. fall through = a plan/agreement fails. fall back on needs an object (the resource).
