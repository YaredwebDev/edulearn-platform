3.6 Observing Cells under a Microscope

  • A microscope magnifies the image of small objects (even cells) through at least one lens.
  • Plant cell activity (onion): peel epidermal tissue from an onion, place on slide, add iodine solution for staining (makes the nucleus/cell wall more visible), cover with cover slip at 45°, view under microscope, draw and label cell wall, cell membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus.
  • Animal cell activity (cheek cell): collect cheek cells with a cotton bud, place on slide, add methylene blue dye for staining, cover slip at 45°, view, draw and label cell membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus. (Use disinfectant/autoclave on used slides; precautions due to small risk of AIDS transmission.)
