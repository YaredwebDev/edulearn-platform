7.2 Vocabulary of the Unit (matric-ready)

Key vocabulary from the "Community Service" passage:

WordMeaningExample
dedicatedcommitted; giving all one's energyThe volunteers were dedicated to their work.
devotedvery loyal and caringShe is devoted to helping others.
completewhole; total; to finishThe task took a complete day.
fortunateluckyWe felt fortunate to help.
betteredimprovedThe experience bettered our lives.
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: dedicated and devoted are near-synonyms (loyal/committed). bettered is the past form of the verb better = "to improve."
