3.3 Graphs of Motion

◌ Not started
  • Displacement–time (s–t) graph: shows how displacement changes with time. Slope = velocity.
  • Velocity–time (v–t) graph: shows how velocity changes with time. Slope = acceleration; area under the curve (between curve and time axis) = displacement.
  • Acceleration–time (a–t) graph: shows how acceleration changes with time.

✋ Trap: the slope of a v–t graph gives acceleration, while the area under a v–t graph gives displacement; the slope of an s–t graph gives velocity. Don't mix slope and area.

The textbook works a complex example of a car moving in four intervals, reading velocities/accelerations and computing displacement from the v–t graph area.

✍️ Must-know (summary statements): - Displacement-time: slope = velocity. - Velocity-time: slope = acceleration; area = displacement. - Acceleration-time: slope relates to jerk; area = change in velocity.
