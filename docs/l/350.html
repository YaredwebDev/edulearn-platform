2.3 Phrasal Verbs (fill / fix / flip / float / follow / fool / freak / find)

◌ Not started
Phrasal verb Meaning Example
fill in complete (a form); give details Please fill in this application form.
fill out complete (a form) Fill out the registration card.
fill up make completely full Fill up the tank with petrol.
fix up repair; arrange; decorate We fixed up the old house.
flip out become very angry/excited He flipped out when he lost his phone.
float around be circulating loosely A rumour is floating around the office.
follow up take further action; pursue Let's follow up the complaint.
fool around waste time; behave playfully Stop fooling around and work.
freak out panic / become very upset She freaked out at the exam.
find out discover (a fact) I want to find out the truth.

✋ Trap: "fill in" and "fill out" both mean complete a form (mostly interchangeable). "fill up" = make full. "find out" = discover; "follow up" = continue after initial action.
