5.2 Vocabulary of the Unit (matric-ready)

Key vocabulary from the "Health Benefits of Gardening" passage:

WordMeaningExample
relieveto reduce/remove (pain, stress)Gardening relieves daily stress.
unwindto relaxAfter work she goes to the garden to unwind.
contributeto give/help towardsFresh vegetables contribute to good health.
consumeto eat/drink; to use upWe should consume more fruit.
enhancingimproving/increasingEating well is enhancing my energy.
instantaneousimmediate; happening in an instantThe relief was almost instantaneous.

Word formation — the prefix ex-:

  • ex- can mean "former" (ex-president, ex-teacher) or "out of" (extract, exit, export).
  • Examples: ex-husband, export, extract, exchange.
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: "instantaneous" is the adjective for instant. Know that ex- = former / out.
