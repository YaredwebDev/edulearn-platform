Word formation — compounding & back-formation

  • Compounding: two words joined to form one. Native: mailman (mail+man), fireplace (fire+place). Borrowed: email (e-mail). Compound words divide into morphemes: replaces = re|place|s; befriended = be|friend|ed; psychology = psych|ology; retroactive = re|tro|act|ive; televise = tele|vise; endearment = en|dear|ment; changeable = change|able; previewer = pre|view|er.
  • Back-formation: a reduction — a noun is shortened to form a verb: editor→edit, television→televise, babysitter→babysit, mathematics→maths, diplomatic→diplomat.

✍️ Must-know: Compounding = joining two words (mailman, fireplace). Back-formation = reducing a noun to make a verb (editor→edit, television→televise, babysitter→babysit). Distinguish from clipping (which keeps the same word, e.g., exam from examination).
