5.1 Introduction

◌ Not started

The musculoskeletal system gives humans the ability to move, gives the body shape and form, and protects vital organs. It binds organs together, supports stability, allows bodily movements, produces blood cells (in the bone marrow) and stores minerals.

  • Musculoskeletal system = bones of the skeleton + muscles + cartilage + tendons + ligaments + joints + connective tissues.
  • Its parts and their jobs: 1. Muscles keep bones in place and enable movement. 2. Joints and cartilages connect bones to bones and prevent bones rubbing against each other. 3. Tendons connect muscles to bones. 4. Ligaments are fibrous connective tissues that attach bone to bone. 5. Connective tissue keeps internal skeletal parts in position and favours movement.

✍️ Must-know: tendons = muscle→bone; ligaments = bone→bone. (Review: "fibrous connective tissue that attaches bone to bone" = ligament; "muscles attached to bones by" = tendon.)
