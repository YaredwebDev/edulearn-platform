8.7 Apologising (functional language)

◌ Not started

Making apologies: I do apologize for…; I must apologize for…; I'd like to apologize for…; I'm so sorry for…; I shouldn't have…; It's all my fault; I'm ashamed of…; Please forgive me for…; I'm terribly sorry for… Accepting apologies: That's all right; Never mind; Don't apologize; It doesn't matter; Don't worry about it; Don't mention it; That's OK; I quite understand; You couldn't help it; Forget about it; No harm done. - An apology = recognise you were wrong + take full responsibility + (often) promise to do better.

✋ Trap (multiple-choice): "I'm so sorry I'm late" (not "apologize"). "Please forgive me for interrupting" (forgive + person). "I would like to apologize for my fault." "Please accept my sincere apologies."
