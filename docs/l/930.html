📚 Unit 5 Summary (Key Facts)

  • Food functions: growth, energy, replacement of damaged tissues, protection from disease.
  • Nutrients: carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, vitamins, minerals, water, fiber.
  • Energy: carb 16 kJ/g, protein 17 kJ/g, lipid 37 kJ/g.
  • Balanced diet = all nutrients in correct amount/proportion.
  • Deficiency diseases: marasmus (carb), kwashiorkor (protein), anemia (iron), rickets (vitamin D), scurvy (vitamin C).
  • Malnutrition: undernutrition (wasting, stunting, underweight, micronutrient deficiency) and obesity; BMI.
  • Substance abuse: tobacco (tar, CO, nicotine), alcohol, heroin, cannabis (THC), khat (cathinone), doping.
  • Diseases: infectious (HIV/AIDS, TB, malaria, COVID-19) vs non-infectious.
