  1. Asexual = one parent, no gametes; Sexual = two parents, gametes + fertilization (zygote).
  2. Fission = divides into 2 (binary) or many (multiple) equal parts; Budding = unequal parts (yeast, hydra); Fragmentation = breaks into parts (Spirogyra); Parthenogenesis = unfertilized egg develops (bees → drones).
  3. Primary sex characteristics (present at birth): males = penis, scrotum, testes; females = vagina, uterus, ovaries.
  4. Puberty hormones: FSH (follicle/sperm), LH (testosterone / ovulation), estrogen (female secondary), progesterone (thickens uterine lining).
  5. Testes produce sperm + testosterone; sperm mature in epididymis; travel via vas deferens + urethra.
  6. Seminal vesicles = fructose-rich; prostate = alkaline; bulbourethral = lubricating mucus.
  7. Menstrual cycle phases: flow → follicular → luteal.
  8. Corpus luteum secretes progesterone; menopause = end of cycles.
  9. Fertilization usually in the oviduct; implantation in the uterus; embryo → fetus at 8 weeks.
  10. Identical twins = one zygote (same sex); fraternal twins = two zygotes.
  11. Abstinence = most effective natural method; condoms = dual protection.
  12. Vasectomy (male), laparotomy/tubal ligation (female) = permanent sterilization.
  13. HIV infects T-lymphocytes → AIDS; bacterial STIs treated with antibiotics, viral STIs are not.
