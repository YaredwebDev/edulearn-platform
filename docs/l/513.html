📝 Writing focus: Communicable-Disease Report

◌ Not started

Write a report on a communicable disease (e.g. HIV/AIDS, TB, malaria). Include: 1. What the disease is (causes, transmission). 2. Its impact (numbers affected, deaths). 3. Prevention and treatment. 4. A conclusion/recommendation.

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) give key HIV/AIDS facts (top-3 killer, 117–120M deaths, WHO 2030, Japan 88.5-yr life expectancy), (2) define all 7+ vocabulary words, (3) use 9+ phrasal verbs with come, (4) form the present perfect and present perfect continuous, (5) distinguish present perfect from simple past and use since/for, (6) write a communicable-disease report?
