5.2 Classification of Feed Resources

◌ Not started

Feeds are broadly classified as roughages and concentrates.

5.2.1 Roughages

  • Bulky feeds, high in fibrous carbohydrates; low digestible-energy content; low digestibility. Contain >18% crude fibre and <60% TDN. TDN = sum of digestible fibre, protein, lipid and carbohydrate components. Natural feed for herbivores; constitute >50% of feed fed to livestock.
  • Dry roughages: hay, straw, stover, husks, sugarcane bagasse (~80–90% dry matter).
  • Green/succulent roughages: green growing pasture, high water & low dry matter (10–30%); silage is produced from green roughage.

5.2.2 Concentrates

  • Rich nutrient source, higher nutritive value & energy than roughages; protein 2–80%; contain <18% crude fibre and >60% TDN; highly digestible.
  • Energy-rich concentrates: high carbohydrate, medium protein (<18% CP), low fat; low fibre & moisture. All cereal grains, roots & tubers; agro-industrial by-products (bran, middlings, molasses).
  • Protein-rich concentrates: >18% CP; plant origin (oilseed cakes/meals e.g. soybean meal, cotton-seed cake) or animal origin (meat meal, dried blood meal, fish meal).

✍️ Must-know: roughage = bulky, >18% crude fibre, <60% TDN, less digestible (hay, straw, stover, silage, pasture); concentrates = <18% crude fibre, >60% TDN, highly digestible, rich (grains, roots/tubers, oilseed cakes, molasses; protein-rich >18% CP).
