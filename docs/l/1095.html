4.1 Reading: National Parks in Ethiopia

(the core "story"/text of the unit)

The passage describes Ethiopia's famous national parks and their wildlife and landscapes.

National parks mentioned:

  • Awash National Park
  • Bale Mountains National Park
  • Omo National Park
  • Simien Mountains National Park — home of Ras Dejen, Ethiopia's highest peak at 4,620 m; the park is a UNESCO World Heritage site.

Key ideas:

  • Parks preserve wildlife, scenery and ecosystems (endemic animals, birds, heath vegetation).
  • They protect extraordinary scenery and endangered species.
  • Visitors travel by ferry/vehicle to reach areas; peaks soar high.
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Questions test (a) which parks exist and what they protect, (b) facts such as Ras Dejen 4,620 m and UNESCO status, and (c) vocabulary in context. Learn the park names and the unique facts.
