2.5 Describing People (speaking/writing vocab)

◌ Not started

Organise descriptions of a person by category: - Hair: long, short, curly, straight, wavy, bald, black, blond. - Forehead: high, broad, narrow. - Nose: long, flat, pointed, broad. - Eyes: big, small, round, almond-shaped, brown, dark, sharp. - Face: round, oval, square, long, pale, dark-skinned. - Height: tall, short, medium, of average height. - Weight: thin, slim, plump, overweight, slender. - Age: young, middle-aged, elderly, a teenager. - Clothing: neatly dressed, smartly dressed, casual. - Personality: friendly, generous, shy, honest, hard-working, cheerful. - Hobbies / likes & dislikes: He enjoys…; She is fond of…; He dislikes…

✍️ Must-know: When describing a person in an exam, cover appearance (hair, face, height, build, clothes) AND character (personality, hobbies, likes/dislikes). Use varied adjectives, not just "good."
