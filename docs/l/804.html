6.5 Population regulation

◌ Not started

Growth ends at carrying capacity due to two kinds of resistance: - Density-dependent factors — affect the population based on its density (living/biotic factors): predation, competition (intra- and interspecific), accumulation of waste, and diseases. The denser the population, the higher the mortality rate; diseases spread quickly in dense populations. - Density-independent factors — affect growth regardless of population density (non-living/abiotic factors): weather, natural disasters (earthquakes, floods), and pollution. An earthquake kills organisms whether density is high or low.
