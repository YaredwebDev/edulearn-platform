3.4 Phrasal Verbs: "get"

◌ Not started

The exact Unit 3 list (Activity 3.15) is:

Phrasal verb Meaning Example
get ahead succeed / make progress Hard work helps you get ahead.
get along be on friendly terms; manage I get along well with my classmates.
get around to finally do something you postponed I finally got around to writing the essay.
get away escape / leave I need to get away from the city.
get back return I'll get back to you next week.
get back at take revenge on He tried to get back at his rival.
get back to return to someone/contact later I'll get back to you with the answer.
get behind fall behind; support They couldn't get behind the project.
get on by survive on what you have They get on by their monthly salary.
get down depress; write down The bad news got him down.

✍️ Must-know: This unit's ten "get" phrasal verbs are exactly: get ahead, get along, get around to, get away, get back, get back at, get back to, get behind, get on by, get down. (More "get" verbs — get in, get off, get on, get out, get over, get over with, get through, get to — are taught separately in Unit 4; don't mix the two lists.)
