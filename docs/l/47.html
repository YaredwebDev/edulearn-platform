8.4 Effects of Diseases and Parasites on Animal Production

◌ Not started

Effects on animal, farmer and country: - Production loss: sick animals fail to produce/reproduce to ultimate capacity (loss for farmer). - Mortality: death of the animal (loss of animal & income). - Economic loss: reduced productivity & mortality → less product to sell and/or fewer animals; external parasites degrade hide/skin/wool market value; diseases/parasites limit export of live animals & products; control/prevention/diagnosis/treatment are expensive; outbreaks affect food and job security. - Public health concern: some animal diseases are transmittable to humans → called zoonotic diseases (transmission often via food from infected animals). More than 150 such diseases known; examples: brucellosis, anthrax, salmonellosis, taeniasis.

✍️ Memorise the four effect categories: morbidity, mortality, economic loss, zoonotic/public-health effects.
