2.2 Flowering and Non-Flowering Plants

◌ Not started
Feature Non-flowering: Gymnosperms Flowering: Angiosperms
Vascular tissue Yes (root, stem, leaf) Yes
Flower No — reproductive organ is a cone Yes — flowers present
Seeds Naked (no cover, only seed coat) Enclosed within a fruit
Examples Pine, juniper Flowering plants (e.g., bean, maize)

Non-vascular lower plants: mosses and liverworts — small, no conducting vessels, restricted to moist/shaded areas, seedless, no flowers or fruits.

✍️ Must-know: Flower is a strong marker of classification. Gymnosperms = naked seeds in cones; Angiosperms = seeds inside a fruit.
