  • Prism: LSA=ph, TSA=LSA+2BA, V=BA·h.
  • Cylinder: LSA=2πrh, TSA=2πr(h+r), V=πr²h.
  • Regular pyramid: LSA=½pl, TSA=BA+½pl, V=⅓(BA)h.
  • Cone: LSA=πrl, TSA=πr(l+r), V=⅓πr²h; l=√(h²+r²).
  • Sphere: SA=4πr², V=4/3πr³.
  • Cross-section area ratio = k²/h².
  • Frustum: cone LSA=πl(r+r′); volume=⅓πh(r²+r′²+rr′)=⅓h(A+A′+√(AA′)).
  • Composed solids: sum volumes; sum exposed areas.
