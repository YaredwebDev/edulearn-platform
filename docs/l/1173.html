7.5 Equation of a Circle

Definition (Def 7.2): A circle is the set of all points in a plane at a fixed distance (radius r) from a fixed point (center).

Standard form: (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r², center (h,k), radius r.

Center at origin: x² + y² = r².

Equation of a circle

General form (Def 7.3): x² + y² + lx + my + n = 0, with l²+m²−4n>0. Complete the square to find center/radius.

Examples:

  • Center (3,4), r=2 → (x−3)²+(y−4)²=4.
  • x²+y²−12x−16y+19=0 → (x−6)²+(y−8)²=81 → center (6,8), r=9.
  • Center (3,4) through P(5,6): r²=(5−3)²+(6−4)²=4+4=8 → (x−3)²+(y−4)²=8.

Special cases: l²+m²−4n=0 → point circle (radius 0); <0 → imaginary (no real circle).

✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Circle equation needs center (h,k) and radius r. Complete the square to convert general form to standard form.
