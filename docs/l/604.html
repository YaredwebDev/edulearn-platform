7.6 Construction of Similar Figures

◌ Not started

To construct a triangle similar to a given △ABC with scale factor 5/3: 1. Draw a ray BX making an acute angle with BC; mark 5 equal segments B₁…B₅. 2. Join B₃C; draw B₅C′ parallel to B₃C. 3. Draw a line through C′ parallel to AC to meet BA produced at A′. 4. △A′B′C′ is similar to △ABC with scale factor 5/3.

  • Scale factor > 1 → larger figure; < 1 → smaller; = 1 → congruent.
