8.1 Reading: HIV/AIDS

◌ Not started

(the core "story"/text of the unit)

The passage discusses HIV/AIDS as a major global health problem.

Key facts in the passage: - HIV/AIDS is among the top three causes of death over the last ~25 years. - It has caused an estimated 117–120 million deaths. - The WHO has set targets/projections for 2030 (an AIDS-free generation / end of AIDS as a public-health threat). - Life expectancy examples: women in Japan have an average life expectancy of about 88.5 years.

✍️ Must-know: Questions test (a) the scale of the disease (top-3 killer, ~117–120 million deaths), (b) WHO 2030 projections, (c) life-expectancy figures, and (d) vocabulary in context. Learn the numbers — they are commonly asked.
