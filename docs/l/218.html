6.8 ⚡ Quick Revision Box (Unit 6)

◌ Not started
  1. Ecology = study of interactions among organisms and with their environment.
  2. Abiotic = non-living (energy, light, temperature, water, nutrients, salinity); Biotic = living (predators, herbivores, parasites, competitors).
  3. Levels: organism → population → community → ecosystem → biosphere.
  4. Population = same species in same area; community = different species interacting; ecosystem = community + environment; biosphere = all of Earth's living zone.
  5. Quadrats used to sample immobile/slow-moving populations.
  6. Tropical rain forest = highest animal diversity; desert = <10 cm rain/year; savanna = scattered trees + grasses (Africa); taiga = coniferous; tundra = permafrost.
  7. Freshwater < 1% salt; marine ≈ 3% salt; aquatic biomes cover ~75% of Earth.
  8. Primary succession = lifeless area (lichens/mosses → soil → climax); secondary succession = after disturbance.
  9. Pioneer species = first to colonize; climax community = stable.
  10. Relationships: Competition (−/−), Predation (+/−), Herbivory (+/−), Parasitism (+/−), Mutualism (+/+), Commensalism (+/0).
  11. Estuaries = transition between river and sea, among the most productive biomes.
