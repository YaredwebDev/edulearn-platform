⚡ Quick Revision Box (Unit 6)

  1. Trophic level 1 = producers; level 2 = primary consumers (herbivores); level 3 = secondary consumers (carnivores).
  2. Only 10% energy transferred per level; 90% lost as heat, work, egestion.
  3. Energy pyramid always upright; number & biomass pyramids can be inverted.
  4. Water cycle: evaporation, transpiration, condensation, precipitation, runoff.
  5. Carbon: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O ⇌ C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ (photosynthesis →, respiration ←).
  6. Nitrogen fixation: Rhizobium (symbiotic, nitrogenase); Azotobacter, Klebsiella (free-living).
  7. Nitrification: Nitrosomonas (NH₃→NO₂⁻), Nitrobacter (NO₂⁻→NO₃⁻).
  8. Denitrification: Pseudomonas, Thiobacillus, Micrococcus (NO₃⁻→N₂); N₂O ≈ 300× CO₂ warming.
  9. Eutrophication = excess phosphate/nitrate → algal bloom → O₂ depletion.
  10. Earthworms = "friends of farmers"; biofertilizers/vermicompost replace chemical fertilizers; legumes fix N (crop rotation).
