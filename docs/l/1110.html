6.6 Grammar: Obligation, Prohibition, Absence of Obligation

ModalMeaningExample
mustobligation (from speaker's own view/strong)We must work to end poverty.
have toobligation (external rule/general law)People have to pay taxes.
mustn'tprohibition (not allowed)You mustn't waste food.
don't have tono obligation (not necessary)You don't have to donate; it's optional.
need tonecessityWe need to find solutions.
Note: ✋ Trap — the big difference: mustn't = it is forbidden ("You mustn't steal"). don't have to / need not = it is not necessary ("You don't have to attend"). They are NOT the same.

📝 Writing focus: Poverty-Alleviation Paragraph

Write a paragraph on mechanisms to reduce poverty: education, job creation, fair wages, social protection, and economic growth. Use the passage's vocabulary and a clear topic-sentence → supporting details → conclusion structure.

Note: ✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) list the three consequences of poverty and the forms of modern slavery, (2) define all 10+ vocabulary words, (3) form words with suffixes -ate/-ise/-ize/-ment/-tion, (4) use 9+ phrasal verbs, (5) read a bar graph/table and compare values, (6) correctly use must/have to/mustn't/don't have to/need to?
