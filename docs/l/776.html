  1. c = λf; c = 3.00×10⁸ m/s.
  2. EM spectrum (increasing frequency): Radio, Micro, IR, Visible, UV, X-ray, Gamma.
  3. Reflection: θᵢ = θᵣ, same plane.
  4. Snell's law: n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂; refractive index n = c/v.
  5. Total internal reflection: dense→rare and θ > θ_c, where sin θ_c = n₂/n₁.
  6. Dispersion order: VIBGYOR; red least refracted, violet most.
  7. Plane mirror image: virtual, erect, same size, laterally inverted.
  8. R = 2f; mirror & lens formula 1/f = 1/u + 1/v; m = h'/h = −v/u.
  9. Convex mirror & concave lens → always virtual, erect, diminished.
  10. Near point 25 cm; far point infinity. Myopia → concave; hypermetropia → convex.
  11. Additive primaries RGB: RG=yellow, RB=magenta, BG=cyan, RGB=white.
  12. Subtractive primaries CMY: yellow absorbs blue, magenta absorbs green, cyan absorbs red.
