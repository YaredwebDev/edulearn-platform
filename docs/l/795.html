5.8 Positive & negative feedback controlling the menstrual cycle

◌ Not started
  • A feedback mechanism returns the body to its normal internal state (homeostasis).
  • Positive feedback (days 12–14): estrogen stimulates the hypothalamus to release GnRH, which makes the anterior pituitary secrete LH and FSH → ovulation.
  • LH — regulates testes (men: stimulate testosterone/sperm) and ovaries (women: weeks 1–2 stimulate follicles to make estradiol; around day 14 an LH surge causes ovulation; weeks 3–4 remnants form the corpus luteum producing progesterone).
  • FSH — stimulates ovaries to grow follicles & eggs. Women are born with ~1–2 million eggs; ~400,000 reach puberty; ~400 ovulate across the reproductive years.
  • Negative feedback leads to menstruation: if no fertilization, the corpus luteum dies, progesterone drops, uterine lining breaks down → period. Positive feedback leads to ovulation.
