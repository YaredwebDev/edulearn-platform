📚 Unit 4 Summary (Key Facts)

  • Reproduction: asexual (one parent, no gametes) vs sexual (two parents, gametes, fertilization).
  • Asexual: fission (binary/multiple), budding, fragmentation, sporulation, vegetative propagation, parthenogenesis.
  • Human reproduction: primary/secondary sexual characteristics, puberty hormones (FSH, LH, testosterone, estrogen, progesterone).
  • Male system: testes, epididymis, vas deferens, urethra, accessory glands, penis.
  • Female system: ovaries, oviducts, uterus, cervix, vagina, genitals; menstrual cycle (flow, follicular, luteal).
  • Fertilization, implantation, embryo → fetus, placenta, umbilical cord.
  • Birth control: natural (abstinence, fertility awareness, withdrawal) and artificial (condoms, diaphragm, IUD, pill, implant, injection, vasectomy, laparotomy).
  • STIs: trichomoniasis, chlamydia, gonorrhea, syphilis, HIV/AIDS.
