6.2 EM Spectrum

◌ Not started

The EM spectrum = the complete range of EM wave frequencies and wavelengths. At one end: low frequency, long wavelength, low energy (radio waves). At the other end: high frequency, short wavelength, high energy (gamma rays).

Order (decreasing wavelength / increasing frequency): Radio waves → Microwaves → Infrared → Visible → Ultraviolet → X-rays → Gamma rays

  • Gamma rays have the highest energy and are most penetrating; radio waves have the lowest energy and are least penetrating.
Type Uses Dangers
Radio waves Communications (police, military, TV) Large doses → cancer, leukaemia
Microwaves Cooking, mobile phones, radar, speed cameras Cataracts (eye), brain effects
Infrared Remote controls, heat lamps, night vision, weather satellites Makes you hot
Visible light Seeing Too much → retina damage
Ultraviolet (UV) Sun tan, detecting forged notes, sterilizing, vitamin D Sunburn, skin cancer, retina damage
X-rays Medical imaging, airport security, astronomy Cell damage, cancer
Gamma rays Radiotherapy (kills cancer cells), sterilizing food/equipment, tracers Cell damage, cancers, mutations

Specific values: Visible light frequency ~4×10¹⁴ to 7×10¹⁴ Hz; wavelength ~400–700 nm. UV from 400 nm down to 0.6 nm; most UV is absorbed by the ozone layer (~40–50 km altitude).

✍️ Must-know: Order: Radio, Micro, Infrared, Visible, Ultraviolet, X-ray, Gamma. Gamma = highest energy/penetration; Radio = lowest. Ozone layer screens most UV.
