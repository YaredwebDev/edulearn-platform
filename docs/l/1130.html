10.3 Phrasal Verbs: cut, do, dress

Cut + particle (from the unit's exercise — choose the right particle):

Phrasal verbMeaningExample
cut down (on)to reduce/consume lessCut down the amount of food you eat.
cut upto chop into piecesShe cut up the meat for her brother.
cut back (on)to reduceThe doctor told him to cut back on salt.
cut offto interrupt/disconnectMy phone call was cut off.
cut outto stop doing (a habit)He had to cut out smoking.

Do + particle:

Phrasal verbMeaningExample
do withoutto manage withoutThere is no sugar, so I'll have to do without it.
do overto do againI'll do it over because my first attempt failed.
do away withto abolish/removeMany countries did away with the death penalty.
do upto fasten; to repair/renovateHe did up his coat.
do withto need/want; to deal withPeople were employed to deal with the backlog.

Dress:

Phrasal verbMeaningExample
dress upto put on formal/elaborate clothesIt's not customary for men to dress up as women.
dress downto dress informally; to scoldShe dressed down for the picnic.
Note: ✋ Trap: cut down / cut back on = reduce; cut off = disconnect/interrupt; cut out = stop a habit; do away with = abolish; do without = manage without; do over = repeat. Learn the particle changes meaning.
