1.3 Major Discoveries That Revolutionized Biology

◌ Not started
# Scientist Dates Key contribution
1 Aristotle 384–322 BC First classification system: "Ladder of Life"; classified organisms as herbs, shrubs, trees, and by ability to move
2 Galen 129–161 AD Transformed medical science; distinguished veins and arteries; recognized larynx produces voice
3 Antonie van Leeuwenhoek 1632–1723 Built strong lenses (up to ~500×); discovered bacteria and the cell vacuole
4 Carl Linnaeus 1707–1775 System for naming/classifying organisms; genus + species (e.g., Homo sapiens)
5 Charles Darwin 1809–1882 Theory of evolution by natural selection; On the Origin of Species (1859)
6 Gregor Mendel 1822–1884 Laws of genetic inheritance using peas; coined "dominant" and "recessive"; father of genetics
7 Louis Pasteur 1822–1895 Father of medical microbiology; germ theory of disease; pasteurization; anthrax and rabies vaccines
8 Robert Koch 1843–1910 Father of modern bacteriology; identified causes of tuberculosis, cholera, anthrax
9 Jane Goodall 1934– Ethologist; studies of chimpanzees (omnivores, tool users); animal rights leader
10 Barbara McClintock 1902–1992 Geneticist; studied maize chromosomes; discovered transposition (jumping genes); Nobel Peace Prize 1983
11 Watson & Crick 1928– /1916–2004 Discovered the structure of DNA (double helix); Nobel Prize 1962
12 Wilmut & Campbell 1944– /1954–2012 Cloned the first mammal, Dolly the sheep (1996), by nuclear transfer

✍️ Must-know: Mendel = father of genetics; Pasteur = father of medical microbiology / germ theory; Koch = father of modern bacteriology.
