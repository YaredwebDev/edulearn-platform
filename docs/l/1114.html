7.3 Phrasal Verbs: drop, dry, eat

Phrasal verbMeaningExample
drop into visit briefly (informally)She dropped in on her grandmother.
drop offto deliver/to leave (someone/something)He dropped off supplies at the hospital.
drop outto quit a course/activityHe dropped out of school.
dried offbecame dry (on the surface)The clothes dried off in the sun.
dried outbecame completely dryThe soil dried out in the heat.
eat upto finish eating; to consumeThe children ate up all the food.
Note: ✋ Trap: drop in = pay a short visit; drop off = deliver/leave; drop out = quit/withdraw. dried off (surface) vs dried out (completely/throughout).
