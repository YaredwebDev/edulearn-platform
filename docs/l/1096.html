4.2 Vocabulary of the Unit (matric-ready)

Key vocabulary from the "National Parks" passage:

WordMeaningExample
amazescauses wonder/surpriseThe scenery amazes every visitor.
stunningextremely beautiful/impressiveThe park has stunning landscapes.
scenerythe natural features of a landscapeThe Bale Mountains offer lovely scenery.
extraordinaryvery unusual/remarkableIt protects extraordinary wildlife.
heathan area of open low-growing shrubsThe highland heath shelters birds.
inhabitingliving in (a place)Animals inhabiting the park are protected.
expansea large open areaA vast expanse of grassland stretches ahead.
ferrya boat carrying passengers/vehicles across waterWe crossed the river by ferry.
peakthe pointed top of a mountainWe reached the peak of Ras Dejen.
soarto rise high/fly highThe eagles soar over the valley.
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: "soar" = rise quickly/high (birds, prices, temperatures). "peak" = mountain top; "expanse" = a wide open area.
