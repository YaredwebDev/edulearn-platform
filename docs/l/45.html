8.2 Major Diseases of Farm Animals

◌ Not started

Major diseases in Ethiopia include FMD, anthrax, tuberculosis, brucellosis, plus CBPP, mastitis, Newcastle disease, coccidiosis, trypanosomiasis.

Disease Type / cause Animals Transmission Symptoms Prevention/Control
Foot & Mouth Disease (FMD) Highly contagious, acute viral cattle, sheep, goats, pigs contact with infected urine, faeces, milk, nasal discharge sores on feet/lips/tongue/cheek; fever, rough coat, salivation, foaming, inability to feed, weakness, lameness, death quarantine, vaccination, good sanitation, elimination of infected animals
Contagious Bovine Pleuropneumonia (CBPP) viral, respiratory cattle, goats, sheep, pigs contact with infected body fluids (milk, urine) dry & painful cough, loss of appetite, difficulty breathing, lung inflammation; high mortality & morbidity vaccination, isolation, slaughter of infected
Brucellosis infectious bacterial (Brucella abortus); = contagious abortion cattle, goats, sheep, camels, pigs direct contact with infected animals/tissue/fluids; ingestion abortion & infertility, intermittent fever, reduced milk flow, enlarged testicles good herd management; burn/bury dead animals; zoonotic risk via raw/unpasteurised dairy
Anthrax infectious bacterial (Bacillus anthracis) ruminants (also humans) contact with infected animals/materials; inhalation of spores high fever, convulsion, rapid breathing, sudden death isolation of infected, disposal of dead
Newcastle disease (NCD) viral poultry airborne; contaminated vaccines, feeders/drinkers no appetite, respiratory difficulty, twisted neck, paralysis of legs/wings, soft-shelled eggs, watery greenish diarrhoea culling sick birds, quarantine, vaccination
Taeniasis tapeworm infection (parasitic) cattle, pigs through soil during feeding anaemia, vomiting, discomfort regular deworming, good sanitation

✍️ Key terms: Viruses = very small acellular structures, only visible with electron microscope, multiply only within a living cell, transmissible. Bacteria = microscopic single-celled organisms found in most habitats. Protozoa = single-celled organisms that live within the body.
