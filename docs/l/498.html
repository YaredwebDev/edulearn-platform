6.2 Vocabulary of the Unit (matric-ready)

◌ Not started

Key vocabulary from the "Impact of Poverty" passage:

Word Meaning Example
perpetuating making something continue (especially something bad) Cheap labour is perpetuating poverty.
entrenched firmly established and hard to change Poverty creates entrenched inequality.
reverse to change to the opposite; the opposite direction Education can reverse poverty.
robust strong and healthy; sturdy A robust economy reduces poverty.
prevalence how common something is The prevalence of disease is high in poor areas.
accustomed used to; in the habit of Poor families are accustomed to hardship.
mercy kindness/forgiveness shown to someone He begged for mercy.
pledge a solemn promise The government made a pledge to fight poverty.
wages money paid for work (by the hour/day/week) Workers demanded fair wages.
recognize to identify; to accept/acknowledge We must recognize the causes of poverty.
