📚 Unit 2 Summary (Key Facts)

◌ Not started
  • Plants are multicellular, autotrophic, sessile, eukaryotic, with a cellulose cell wall, and show both asexual and sexual reproduction.
  • Gymnosperms = naked seeds in cones; Angiosperms = seeds in fruits.
  • External structure: shoot system + root system. Internal leaf layers: epidermis + mesophyll (palisade, spongy).
  • Pollination → fertilization → seed & fruit formation → dispersal → germination.
  • Seed = matured ovule; fruit = matured ovary; angiosperms show double fertilization.
  • Photosynthesis: light reaction (granum, photolysis, ATP) + dark reaction (stroma, carbon fixation). Overall: 6CO₂+6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆+6O₂.
  • Transport: xylem (water/minerals), phloem (food); transpiration pull, cohesion, adhesion; active translocation.
  • Tropisms: phototropism (light), hydrotropism (water), geotropism (gravity), mediated by auxin.
