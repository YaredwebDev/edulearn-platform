5.5 Heterogeneous equilibria — omit pure solids and pure liquids

◌ Not started

When reactants/products are in more than one phase (e.g. CaCO₃(s) ⇌ CaO(s) + CO₂(g)), do NOT include pure solids or pure liquids in the KC/KP expression (their concentrations are constant). - CaCO₃(s) ⇌ CaO(s) + CO₂(g): KC = [CO₂]; KP = P_CO₂. - NH₄Cl(s) ⇌ NH₃(g) + HCl(g): KC = [NH₃][HCl]; KP = P_NH₃·P_HCl. - CO₂(g) + H₂O(l) ⇌ H⁺(aq) + HCO₃⁻(aq): KC = [H⁺][HCO₃⁻]/[CO₂] (H₂O omitted).
