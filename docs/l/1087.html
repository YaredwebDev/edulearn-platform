2.2 Vocabulary of the Unit (matric-ready)

Key vocabulary from the "Learning Strategies" passage:

WordMeaningExample
recoursea source of help; the act of turning to something for helpThe dictionary is a good recourse for learners.
sessiona period of time spent doing one activityWe have a two-hour study session tonight.
boostto improve/increase; a helpful increaseShort breaks boost your concentration.
achievableable to be reached/doneSet achievable goals, not impossible ones.
hugevery largeDon't be frightened by a huge syllabus.
boredomthe state of being bored/wearyFrequent breaks prevent boredom.
favouritemost likedReading is her favourite habit.
flexibilitythe ability to change/adapt easilyFlexibility in your plan helps you cope with surprises.
rewardsomething given for good work/achievementGive yourself a reward after studying.

Collocations (words that commonly go together with "big/heavy/rich/strong"):

  • big: big deal, big problem, big decision
  • heavy: heavy traffic, heavy rain, heavy responsibility
  • rich: rich vocabulary, rich culture, rich food
  • strong: strong argument, strong coffee, strong wind
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Collocations sound natural; mixing them (e.g. "heavy wind") is wrong. Learn them as chunks.
