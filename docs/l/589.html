4.5 Applications of Inequalities

◌ Not started

Example 1: Kebede has Birr 6,000, saves Birr 1,000/month until > 80,000. 6000 + 1000m > 80000 → 1000m > 74000 → m > 74. So more than 74 months (6 years 2 months).

Example 2: Chaltu charged Birr 0.20/message, has Birr 50, bill ≤ Birr 80. 0.2m + 50 ≤ 80 → 0.2m ≤ 30 → m ≤ 150. Max 150 messages.

Example 3: Normal pregnancy 37 to 41 weeks inclusive: 37 ≤ w ≤ 41. Abnormal: w < 37 or w > 41.

Example 4: Dendir: food service Birr 10/hr + tutoring Birr 15/hr, needs ≥ Birr 240. 10x + 15y ≥ 240 → y ≥ −(2/3)x + 16. Shade the upper region; e.g., (15,10), (0,16), (24,0) are solutions.

✍️ Must-know: "at most" → ≤; "at least / more than" → ≥ or >; "between, inclusive" → compound a ≤ x ≤ b.
