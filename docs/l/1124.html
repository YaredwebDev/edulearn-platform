9.3 Phrasal Verbs: count, crack, cross, cut

Phrasal verbMeaningExample
count onto depend on / rely onYou should count on yourself to improve your English.
count upto add together (a total)Please count up the number of tickets sold.
crack down onto take strong action againstThe police crack down on drink-driving offences.
cross offto delete from a listShe crossed him off her Christmas-card list.
cut backto reduceThe firm cut back production because sales were slow.
cut downto reduce/consume lessI'm trying to cut down the coffee I drink.
Note: ✋ Trap: count on = rely/depend on; count up = add a total; crack down on = punish strongly; cross off = delete from a list; cut back / cut down = reduce. These are the exact phrasal verbs in the unit's matching exercise.
