5.4 Excretory System

5.4.1 Structure

Organs: Kidneys (2) → Ureters → Urinary bladder → Urethra.

  • Kidney: bean-shaped; has cortex (outer), medulla (inner), and pelvis.
  • Functional unit of the kidney = the nephron (~1 million per kidney).

Nephron parts

Renal corpuscle (Bowman's capsule + glomerulus) → Proximal convoluted tubule (PCT) → Loop of Henle → Distal convoluted tubule (DCT) → Collecting duct.

5.4.2 Function of the Kidney (Urine Formation)

  1. Glomerular filtration: blood pressure forces water, salts, glucose, urea, amino acids (filterable) out of the glomerulus into Bowman's capsule (filtrate). Blood cells and proteins stay in blood.
  2. Tubular reabsorption: PCT reabsorbs useful substances (glucose, amino acids, most water, salts) back into blood.
  3. Loop of Henle: concentrates urine (reabsorbs water, maintains salt gradient).
  4. Tubular secretion: DCT actively secretes wastes (H⁺, K⁺, creatinine, drugs) into the filtrate.
  5. Collecting duct: final water reabsorption under ADH (antidiuretic hormone) control.

Other kidney functions: produces erythropoietin (EPO) (85% of the body's) which stimulates red-cell production; completes vitamin D activation; regulates blood pH, blood pressure, and blood volume.

Excretion is the removal of metabolic wastes (urea, CO₂, excess water/salts); vs egestion (undigested food) and defecation.

5.4.3 Excretory Disorders

  • Kidney stones: crystallized minerals in the kidney/ureter causing pain.
  • Nephritis: inflammation of the kidney (infection).
  • Kidney failure: kidneys stop filtering → dialysis or kidney transplant needed.
  • Uraemia: accumulation of urea in blood due to kidney failure.
  • Diabetes insipidus: ADH deficiency → excessive dilute urine.
  • Glycosuria: glucose in urine (diabetes mellitus, when blood glucose high).
✍️ Must-know: Urine formation = filtration (glomerulus) → reabsorption (PCT) → secretion (DCT). Kidney makes EPO (85%) and completes vitamin D.
