1.2 The Relationship Between Chemistry and Other Natural Sciences

Chemistry is one branch of science and is called the "central science" because it links all fields that involve matter.

  • Biology (study of living things) + Chemistry = Biochemistry (chemical processes in living matter).
  • Geology (study of rocks/earth) + Chemistry = Geochemistry (processes controlling abundance, composition, and distribution of chemical compounds and isotopes in geologic environments).
  • Physics (nature and properties of matter and energy) + Chemistry = Physical chemistry and Chemical physics.
  • Medicine + Chemistry = Medicinal chemistry (design, development, and synthesis of pharmaceutical drugs).
✍️ Must-know: Chemistry is the "central science." Overlaps: Biochemistry, Geochemistry, Physical/Chemical physics, Medicinal chemistry.
