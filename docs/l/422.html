2D.1 Vocabulary learning strategies

  • Guessing meaning from context (word/phrase before & after) · using a dictionary · written repetition · studying the spelling · asking other people. Most-used (Schmitt, 1997): bilingual dictionary, verbal & written repetition, spelling, guessing from context, asking classmates.

2D.2 Phrasal verbs

  • Phrasal verbs = main verb + a particle, together making one meaning different from the separate words (e.g. take off = leave; look after = take care of; name after = give the name of someone).
  • Practised in this unit:
  • take after (resemble), bring up (raise a child), carry on (continue), call up (telephone), call back.
  • let off (not punish; let leave) · lay off (dismiss) · lead up to (cause) · leave behind (abandon; go faster than) · leave off (stop/cease) · leave out (omit) · lay down (put down; surrender) · let down (disappoint) · let in (allow to enter).
  • Telephone phrasal verbs (word map): hang on (wait), hang up (end call, put phone down), cut off (interrupt a call), get through (succeed in speaking to sb), call back (return a call), pick up (answer), put through (connect), hold on (wait a short time), speak up (speak louder), break up (become inaudible).
