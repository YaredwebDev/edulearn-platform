2.4 Wheat (Triticum aestivum)

◌ Not started
  • Origin: Asia (China, Pakistan, India). Globally the most important cereal, then rice, then maize. Makes bread, macaroni, spaghetti.
  • Production: needs cool weather; altitude ~1800–3000 m in Ethiopia; well-drained loamy fertile soils (black, red, brown); rotated with lentils, pea, teff, potato.
  • Land prep: 2–3 cultivations.
  • Planting: broadcast or rows (25 cm spacing), depth ~5 cm; usually June–July.
  • Fertilisation: ~45 kg N + 25 kg P per ha.
  • Weed/pest/disease: manual weeding from ~1 month after planting; 2,4-D for broad-leaf weeds; fungal rust and smut in warm humid areas → crop rotation, resistant varieties, adjusting planting time.
  • Harvesting: when dry golden-yellow with no green parts. National average ≈ 24.5 quintals (2.45 t)/ha. Source of carbohydrates, proteins, fats, minerals, fibre. Wheat contains gluten.
