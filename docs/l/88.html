14.2 Types of Farm Tools and Equipment

◌ Not started

Farm equipment ranges from big combine harvesters to a hypodermic syringe for animal medication. Categories by function: - Soil cultivation implements — for ploughing & preparing soil: tillers, disk harrows, moldboard ploughs. - Planting machines — for planting saplings/seeds after cultivation: e.g. seeder. - Harvesting equipment — to gather mature crops: combine harvester, diggers, trailers, pickers. - Irrigation machinery — for watering crops on large farms: central-pivot irrigation systems, pump units. - Feed processing equipment — weighing scale, shovel, wheelbarrow, baler, hacker, chopper, millers/grinders, mixers. - Dairy machinery — buckets, milk containers, automatic milking machines, coolers, pasteurizers; also homogenizer, cream separator, batch mixers, continuous freezers, butter churner, cheese molder. - Miscellaneous agricultural equipment — for hay-making, shredding, loading; forestry machines, aerosols, remote sensing, fruit pickers.
