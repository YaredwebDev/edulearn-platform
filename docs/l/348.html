2.1 Reading: Travel Behaviors (the core "story"/text)

◌ Not started

The passage has three paragraphs:

Paragraph 1 — Travel is everyday human behaviour: - Travelling is an everyday human behaviour; but some people find it an ordeal (they get travel sickness, feel discomfort). - People travel for business, pleasure, or education. - Travel broadens the mind; it is part of curricula (some countries fund student travel).

Paragraph 2 — Travel behavior is complicated decision-making: - Travel behavior is complicated decision-making about mode, route, time, and destination. - It is interdependent with people's beliefs, attitudes, and values. - These predetermine your purpose, how much money you take, and what items you carry.

Paragraph 3 — Travelling in big cities: - Follow traffic rules; pedestrians keep to the left-hand side; use sidewalks/pavements. - Avoid displaying valuables; beware pick-pockets in crowds; take maximum care.

✍️ Must-know: Key idea — travel choice (mode/route/time/destination) is shaped by personal beliefs/attitudes/values and by purpose/money/items. City-safety tips: traffic rules, left-hand side, sidewalks, hide valuables, watch for pick-pockets.
