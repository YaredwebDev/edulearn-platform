2.10 Medicinal Plants

◌ Not started

More than 60% of the world's population depends on traditional plant medicines. Some Ethiopian medicinal plants (Table 2.2):

Plant (scientific) Amharic name Part used Disease treated
Ruta chalepensis Teenadem Leaf Abdominal pain
Zingiber officinalis Jinjibil Rhizome Tonsillitis, abdominal pain, cough
Hagenia abyssinica Ye-kosso zaf Female flower (seed) Tapeworm
Artemisia absinthium Aritii Leaf Stomach ache
Nigella sativa Tikur azmud Seed Intestinal parasites
Ocimum lamifolium Damakesse Leaf Headache, general body illness
Allium sativum Nechshinkurt Bulb Abdominal pain, toothache, tonsillitis, cold
Curcurbita pepo Dubba Seed Tapeworm
Trigonella foenumgraecum Abish Seed Asthma, gastritis

Safety: wear gloves when collecting; do not taste, ingest, or inhale volatile substances; wash hands afterward.
