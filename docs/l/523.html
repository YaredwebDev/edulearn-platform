10.4 Grammar: Tense Revision (Summary Table)

◌ Not started

This unit revises four tenses. Master their positive, negative and question forms.

Tense Form Function Example
Simple past V2; did + V1 Completed action at a definite past time I played basketball yesterday.
Past continuous was/were + -ing Action in progress for a longer time in the past I was playing basketball the whole evening.
Present perfect have/has + V3 Past action (result/experience); no definite past time I have played basketball.
Present perfect continuous have/has + been + -ing Action that started in the past and continues to now I have been playing basketball for three hours.

Choosing between present perfect simple and continuous: - Present perfect simple focuses on a completed result/experience: "I have written three letters." - Present perfect continuous focuses on the duration/ongoing action: "I have been writing letters all morning."

Time clues: - Simple past: yesterday, last week, in 2010, ago. - Past continuous: while, when, the whole evening, at that moment. - Present perfect: since, for, just, already, yet, ever, never. - Present perfect continuous: for, since, all morning/day, lately.

✋ Trap: "I was writing for three hours" (past, finished) vs "I have been writing for three hours" (still writing). "I have been to X" (experience) vs "I have gone to X" (current location/result).
