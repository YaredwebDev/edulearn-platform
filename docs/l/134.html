4.1 The Genetic Material

◌ Not started
  • Genetics = the branch of biology that studies the genetic material of organisms and how traits pass from one generation to the next through genes.
  • Genetic material = material carrying genetic information passed from generation to generation to perpetuate life. In almost all organisms this is DNA; in some viruses (HIV, COVID-19) it is RNA.
  • DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid) = hereditary material of humans and other organisms; a double helix of base pairs attached to a sugar–phosphate backbone.
  • RNA (ribonucleic acid) = genetic code of some viruses; involved in protein synthesis.
