3.8 Enzyme Kinetics

◌ Not started

Enzyme kinetics describes the rates of enzyme-catalysed reactions and binding affinities. Enzymes speed reactions by lowering activation energy; enzyme and substrate concentrations set the rate.

Michaelis–Menten model/formula: V = (Vmax [S]) / (Km + [S]) - V (V₁) = initial velocity/rate of reaction - Vmax = maximal velocity/maximum rate - [S] = substrate concentration - Km (Michaelis constant) = substrate concentration at half-maximal velocity (a measure of affinity)

The reaction scheme: S ⇌ ES ⇌ ES* ⇌ EP ⇌ E + P (substrate → enzyme-substrate → transition state → enzyme-product → enzyme + product).

✍️ Must-know (MCQ): the enzyme kinetic reaction formula/scheme = S ⇌ ES ⇌ ES* ⇌ EP ⇌ E + P. Km = substrate concentration at half Vmax.
