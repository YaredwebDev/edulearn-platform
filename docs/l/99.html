UNIT 15 · LESSON 8 OF 8 · GRADE 11 AGRICULTURE

🧮 Key facts to memorise

◌ Not started
  • Six nutrients: carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, vitamins, minerals, water.
  • Macro (CHO, fat, protein) vs micro (vitamins, minerals).
  • Energy: 1 g CHO = 4 kcal; 1 g fat = 9 kcal; 1 g protein = 4 kcal.
  • Daily calorie shares: CHO 45–65% (text summary ~55%), fat 20–35% (~30%), protein 10–35% (~15%).
  • Complete proteins from animals; incomplete from plants. 20 amino acids.
  • Fat-soluble vitamins A,D,E,K (stored); water-soluble B,C (not stored).
  • Major minerals (RDA>150 mg/day): Mg, Ca, P, K; trace (RDA<150): Fe, Se, Zn, Mn.
  • Food groups: vegetables, fruits, legumes & nuts, animal foods, fats & oils, staples. Eat from ≥4 food groups.
  • Four pillars: availability, access, utilization, stability.
  • Food vs nutrition security (quantity vs nutritious + water/sanitation/health/care).
  • Forms of malnutrition: obesity (>20% over recommended weight), stunting (height-for-age), wasting (weight-for-height <-2 SD), underweight (weight-for-age <-2 SD), micronutrient deficiency.
  • Key deficiencies: iron (anaemia), vitamin A (night blindness), iodine (goitre), zinc, calcium, folic acid.
  • BMI = kg/m²; normal 18.5–25, overweight 25–30, obese >30, underweight <18.5.
  • Malnutrition causes: immediate, underlying, basic; 1/3 child mortality worldwide attributed to malnutrition.
  • Review answers: nutrients giving calories = carbohydrates & fats (A); food group mainly providing carbohydrates = staple foods (C); adequate dietary intake + ability to absorb/use nutrients = Utilization (D); children below average height-for-age = Stunted (B); a varied diet with enough calories & nutrients = Balanced diet (D); micronutrient supplemented to adolescents & pregnant women = Iron folic acid (C); malnutrition from long-term food shortage = Both stunting & underweight (D, B and C).
