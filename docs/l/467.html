10A.2 Listening 2: Robotics in Ethiopia (iCog Labs / Sophia)

  • iCog was part of a team of scientists that developed the software for Sophia, the world's first robot citizen (a humanoid). Sophia has learnt some Amharic. Designed with high cheekbones & a slender nose, Sophia — who is also a citizen of Saudi Arabia — can display 60 types of facial expressions and carry a conversation.
  • Match actors to functions: Assefa = a person who is co-founder of iCog Lab in Ethiopia; iCog = team of scientists that developed the software; Sophia = shows 60 facial expressions & carries a conversation; Hanson Robotics = created Sophia.
  • Research & report industries/organisations where robots perform activities.
