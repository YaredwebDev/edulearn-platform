6.8 Color Addition of Light

◌ Not started

The additive color system reproduces colors by adding the primary colors of light: red, green, blue.

Additive combinations: - Red + Green = Yellow - Red + Blue = Magenta - Blue + Green = Cyan - Red + Green + Blue = White - Absence of all three = Black

Yellow, magenta, and cyan are the secondary colors of light (produced by adding equal intensities of two primaries).

Applications: color TV, computer monitors, on-stage theater lighting, digital projectors.

✍️ Must-know: Red+Green=Yellow; Red+Blue=Magenta; Blue+Green=Cyan; RGB=White.
