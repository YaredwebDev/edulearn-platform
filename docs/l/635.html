UNIT 3 · LESSON 7 OF 7 · GRADE 11 PHYSICS

🧮 Key facts to memorise

◌ Not started
  • a = Δv/Δt; SI unit m/s²; instantaneous acceleration as Δt→0.
  • Five equations of motion (see §3.2).
  • s–t graph slope = velocity; v–t graph slope = acceleration and area = displacement.
  • Free fall: a = g ≈ 9.8 m/s² downward; v=0 at top of a vertical throw but a = g still.
  • Terminal velocity: acceleration = 0 (weight balanced by drag).
  • Reaction distance = reaction time × speed.
  • Circular motion: v = rω; a_c = v²/r = rω²; F_c = mv²/r = mrω²; ω = 2π/T = 2πf.
  • Centripetal force acts toward centre, perpendicular to velocity.
  • Banked road: tan θ = v²/(rg). Roundabout skidding = friction insufficient.
  • 1 km/h = 1000 m/3600 s; convert by dividing by 3.6.

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) define acceleration and uniform accelerated motion and state SI units; (2) write and apply the five kinematic equations; (3) read s–t, v–t, a–t graphs (slopes and areas) and analyse a multi-interval v–t graph; (4) solve free-fall/vertical-throw problems including reaction-time and stopping-distance situations; (5) explain terminal velocity; (6) define angular vs tangential displacement/velocity and centripetal acceleration/force; (7) apply v=rω, a_c=v²/r and F_c=mv²/r to wheels, centrifuge, merry-go-round, string & car-on-curve problems; and (8) use the conical pendulum and banking of roads relations? Re-answer the end-of-unit questions (speed vs velocity constancy, variable speed constant velocity, projectile-like reasoning, police/traffic and Ethiopian banks tower free-fall problems, kangaroo jump, circular motion of a 0.525 kg ball, tension in a string, and skidding distance questions).
