10.1 Reading: The Digital versus Satellite Television (the core "story"/text)

The passage has seven paragraphs comparing cable/digital and satellite TV.

Paragraph 1 — Definitions & history:

  • Cable TV (digital television) comes to your home through a cable wire; satellite TV is beamed down to a dish from a satellite.
  • Both allow you to "tune in" to specific channels within a signal.
  • Big difference: cable comes through a wire, satellite through the air.
  • Cable began in the 1940s (analog signal); satellite channels began in the 1990s (digital signal — higher-quality sound and picture).
  • By 2009 all cable systems had converted to digital; both now offer high-definition options.

Paragraph 2 — The old days:

  • Simple analog TV sets are long gone; technology gave birth to innovative, cutting-edge siblings; the contrast between cable and digital TV was the most confusing.

Paragraph 3 — Transmission differences:

  • They share a lot in common but transmission differs. Satellite: data transmitted from powerful antennas to satellites in orbit, then down to personal satellite dishes in homes. Cable: transmitted through coaxial cables from the service provider to coaxial outlets in homes.

Paragraph 4 — Competitors & quality:

  • Cable companies: AT&T, Comcast, Cox cable, U-Verse TV. Analog sets didn't support as many channels (esp. movie channels) and image quality was poor. Digital TV put a 180° shift — digital compression conveys more refined, high-quality content.

Paragraph 5 — Cost:

  • Satellite TV has a higher one-time cost (unless free equipment/installation for a yearlong commitment); cable TV has higher infrastructure maintenance cost → heavier monthly bills. In the long run, satellite offers more channels per dollar.

Paragraph 6 — Subscription:

  • Some satellite providers require a mandatory ~12-month subscription; most cable companies have no minimum subscription period. Some satellite/cable packages are similar nationwide; each local cable company may offer its own features and channel lineup.

Paragraph 7 — Conclusion:

  • Satellite seems to lead, but not by much, in innovation and picture quality; for entertainment/content it's about personal taste; there is no definite answer — ask both suppliers about current packages.
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: cable = via wire (1940s, analog→digital by 2009); satellite = via dish/air (1990s, digital). Companies: AT&T, Comcast, Cox, U-Verse (cable). Satellite = higher one-time cost but more channels/dollar; cable = higher monthly bills; satellite often mandatory 12-month contract. These are the True/False test points.
