1.6 General Laboratory Safety Rules

Safety = the condition of being protected from harm or danger; control of recognized hazards.

Important general laboratory safety rules:

  1. Follow the instructions — read/listen to the procedure and be familiar with all steps; know how to use all equipment.
  2. Know the location of safety equipment — fire extinguisher and first-aid kit must be readily available; know how to use them; review lab safety signs.
  3. Dress for the laboratory — lab coat, safety goggles, gloves, hearing protection, long pants, long-sleeve shirt, leather shoes/boots (no sandals). Wear a disposable respirator mask when handling toxic chemicals.
  4. Never eat or drink in the laboratory.
  5. Never taste or sniff chemicals — label containers and read before use.
  6. Act responsibly — never randomly mix chemicals (could cause explosion, fire, or toxic gas).
  7. Clean the experiment area and store waste properly.
  8. Handle chemicals properly — never allow chemicals to contact bare skin.
  9. Know what to do with accidents — if burned or exposed to chemicals, immediately flood the area with cold tap water for several minutes.
  10. Always emphasize safety and chemical hygiene — never leave chemical containers open; all containers must be labeled; never use unlabeled chemicals.
