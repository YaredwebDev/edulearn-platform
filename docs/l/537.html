  • Polynomial: exponents are non-negative integers; degree, leading coeff, constant term.
  • Domain of any polynomial = ℝ.
  • deg(fg)=deg f + deg g; sum/diff/product of polynomials are polynomials.
  • Polynomial Division: f=dq+r, r=0 or deg r<deg d.
  • Remainder Theorem: remainder of f÷(x−c) = f(c).
  • Factor Theorem: f(c)=0 ⟺ (x−c) factor.
  • Zero = root = x-intercept. Multiplicity k.
  • Location Theorem: sign change between a,b → zero between them.
  • Rational Zero Test: zeros = ±(factors of a₀)/(factors of aₙ).
  • Graph: smooth & continuous; degree n → at most n−1 turning points, at most n x-intercepts.
  • End behaviour: odd degree → ends opposite; even → same. Sign of leading coeff sets up/down.
