2.7 Renowned Taxonomists in Ethiopia

  • Ethiopia has rich biodiversity but few trained taxonomists. Over the past six decades, a few renowned taxonomists have contributed volumes on the Flora of Ethiopia:
  • Dr. Mesfin Taddese
  • Professor Sebsebe Demissew
  • Professor Ensermu Kelbessa
  • Professor Silesh Nemomissa
  • Professor Abebe Getahun (zoologist — animal taxonomy)
  • Professor Sebsebe Demissew has published books/articles on Ethiopian and African vegetation, served as Chair of the Biological Society of Ethiopia, Secretary-General of AETFAT, and director of the Flora of Ethiopia and Gulelle Botanical Garden.
