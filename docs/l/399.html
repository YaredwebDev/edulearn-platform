9.1 Reading: Cognitive Benefits of being Multilingual (the core "story"/text)

◌ Not started

The passage has an intro + five benefits and argues that speaking multiple languages improves the brain.

Intro: - Multilingualism has social and career benefits, plus huge cognitive benefits. - Over half the world's population is multilingual to some extent. - Speaking more than one language: memory and learning abilities change; the brain becomes more efficient and physically restructures itself.

Benefit 1 — Better innate understanding of how language works: - Learning a second/third/fourth language brings attention to the mechanics of language (grammar, conjugations, sentence structure). - Multilinguals pick up the structure of any language more quickly. - They tend to be more effective communicators, more exact editors, more compelling writers — even in their native language.

Benefit 2 — Less mental decline in old age: - The more cognitive energy elderly people expend, the less cognitive decline. - Bilingualism can delay the onset of dementia and Alzheimer's by an average of five years. - Bilingual patients who develop Alzheimer's show less cognitive decay / less brain degeneration than monolinguals.

Benefit 3 — A more efficient, better-developed executive control system: - Multilinguals constantly switch between languages without thinking. - The executive control system is the brain part controlling attention-switching and working memory. - Better performance on tasks requiring high-level thought, multitasking, sustained attention.

Benefit 4 — Greater cognitive flexibility and problem-solving skills: - Learning a language teaches the brain that the same thought can be expressed in multiple ways. - More creative at solving problems; perceive situations in different ways.

Benefit 5 — Improvements in learning abilities: - Inhibition (an executive function) = the ability to discard irrelevant stimuli and focus on key ones; key to learning. - Multilinguals have better inhibition → learn a third/fourth language more quickly and develop any learned skill faster.

Conclusion: The cognitive benefits can outweigh the massive effort of learning a new language.

✍️ Must-know: >50% of the world is multilingual; benefits = understanding of language mechanics, less mental decline (delay Alzheimer's ~5 yrs), better executive control (multitasking, attention), more cognitive flexibility & creativity, and better learning ability (via inhibition).
