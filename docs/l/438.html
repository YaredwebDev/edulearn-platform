5B.2 Dialogues: accepting/rejecting ideas & turn-taking

  • Identify expressions used to make suggestions, accept suggestions, reject suggestions (e.g. How about…? What if you…? / That sounds a good idea. Definitely. / I'm not really sure I agree with that; I think it's premature). Produce a dialogue on "ways of increasing agricultural productivity in Ethiopia".

5B.3 Debate

  • Debate = an argument/discussion expressing different opinions, with two sides: one supporting a resolution and one opposing it, governed by agreed rules.
  • Planning a debate: decide your position (for/against); establish rules & timing; choose a leader; collect facts/data; frame arguments in complete, persuasive sentences backed by reliable sources; anticipate counter-arguments & prepare refutation.
  • For debaters: greet, announce the resolution, state your position & number of points; attack the opponent's case, not the opponent; use body language & humour; persuade the audience/judges.
  • Proposition to debate: Agriculture should remain at the centre of the Ethiopian economy.

5B.4 Public speaking

  • Difference from casual conversation: public speaking is prepared and audience-directed.
  • Give a ~10-minute speech on "the importance of technology use in agriculture": research; determine the message & intent; organise intro–body–conclusion; rehearse; check movement/body language; check volume & rate; keep key-word notes; use visual prompts.
