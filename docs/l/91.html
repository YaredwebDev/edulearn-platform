UNIT 14 · LESSON 5 OF 5 · GRADE 11 AGRICULTURE

🧮 Key facts to memorise

◌ Not started
  • Mechanization = machinery replacing human/animal labour.
  • Advantages: saves time/labour, less difficulty & health hazards, large-scale farming, higher output/income, farmer cooperation.
  • Disadvantages: cost, pollution, soil compaction, harms social capital (Debo/Guza), less manual work, needs energy.
  • Levels: low (manual >33%), fair (animal 34–66%), high (mechanical 67–100%).
  • Combine harvester = reaping + threshing + gathering + winnowing.
  • Baler makes bales; seed drill plants at equal distance/depth; subsoiler loosens deep soil.
  • Cool milk below 4 °C within 2 hours; incubator 21 days.
  • Review answers: NOT a soil cultivation tool = Baler (C); 34–66% animal power = Fair (A); most popular/essential crop equipment = Tractor (B); a limitation of mechanization = requires continuous supply of energy (D); primary function of subsoiler = to loosen soil during deep tillage (C).

✅ End-of-unit self-check: Can you (1) define mechanized farming and state the three levels & three power sources; (2) summarise the advantages and disadvantages of mechanization; (3) classify farm tools & equipment by function; and (4) name the uses of the main tools (tractor, plough, subsoiler, cultivator, cultipacker, harrow, seed drill, sprinkler, combine harvester, baler, feed miller/mixer, milking machine, cooler, churner, incubator, sprayer, pruning shears)?
