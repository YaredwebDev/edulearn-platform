2.5 Common Ethiopian Animals and Plants

Ethiopia has high biological diversity (biodiversity) due to its geographical location, topographical diversity, and diverse climatic features — a hot spot for endemic species. Ethiopia is a primary centre of diversity for noug (Guizotia abyssinica), tef (Eragrostis tef), and Ethiopian mustard (Brassica carinata); also barley, sorghum, durum wheat, finger millet, faba bean, chickpea, lentil, cowpea.

Note: Dichotomous keys (note box): used to identify unfamiliar organisms. "Dichotomous" = two branches; each key is made of pairs of contrasting features, starting with general characteristics and progressing to more specific ones. By following the key and making appropriate choices, you identify the organism correctly. Example: poikilothermic (cold-blooded) vs homoiothermic (warm-blooded) → then further choices.
