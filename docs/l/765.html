◌ Not started
  1. B = μ₀I/2πd; μ₀ = 4π×10⁻⁷ T·m/A.
  2. F = qvB sin θ (moving charge); F = ILB sin θ (current-carrying wire).
  3. Unit of B = tesla (T); 1 G = 10⁻⁴ T; Earth's field ≈ 5×10⁻⁵ T.
  4. Max magnetic force when v (or I) ⊥ B; zero when parallel.
  5. Right-hand rule: thumb = current, fingers = field (wire); for moving charge, palm = force direction.
  6. Magnetic field lines: closed loops, N→S outside, S→N inside, never cross.
  7. Parallel wires: same direction → attract; opposite → repel.
  8. Poles always in pairs (no magnetic monopole observed).
  9. Earth's "magnetic North" is actually a bar-magnet South pole; field tilted ~11°.
  10. Demagnetize permanent magnets by heat, hammering, or striking.
