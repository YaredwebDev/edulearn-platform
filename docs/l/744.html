4.3 The Electroscope

◌ Not started

An electroscope is a sensitive instrument used to: - detect the presence/type of electric charge, - measure the quantity of charge, - tell whether an object is a conductor or insulator.

It consists of a glass container, a metal rod, two thin pieces of gold foil (or leaves), and a metal plate on top. When a charged object is brought near, the induced charge pushes the gold leaves apart (like charges repel).

Key: Detecting a charge = observing the diverging (or collapsing) of the electroscope's gold leaf.
