5.1 Reading: Health Benefits of Gardening

(the core "story"/text of the unit)

The passage explains how gardening/horticulture benefits physical and mental health.

Health benefits of gardening:

  • Relieves stress — gardening unwinds the mind.

  • Provides physical exercise.

  • Gives access to nutritious food (improves nutrition).

  • Aids recovery and rehabilitation.

  • Boosts creativity.

  • Teaches patience.

✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: Questions test (a) each benefit named, (b) why gardening is good for health, and (c) vocabulary in context.
