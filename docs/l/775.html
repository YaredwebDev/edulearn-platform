📚 Unit 6 Summary (Key Facts)

◌ Not started
  • EM waves are transverse; no medium needed; c = 3.00×10⁸ m/s = λf.
  • EM spectrum: Radio, Micro, Infrared, Visible, UV, X-ray, Gamma (increasing frequency/energy).
  • Reflection: θᵢ=θᵣ, same plane. Refraction: Snell's law n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂; n = c/v.
  • Total internal reflection: dense→rare, θ > θ_c (sin θ_c = n₂/n₁); optical fibres, endoscopes.
  • Dispersion: VIBGYOR; red least, violet most refracted.
  • Plane mirror: virtual, erect, same size, laterally inverted, same distance.
  • Concave mirror / convex lens: converging. Convex mirror / concave lens: diverging.
  • Mirror & lens formula: 1/f = 1/u + 1/v; m = h'/h = −v/u. R = 2f.
  • Human eye: retina, cornea, iris/pupil; near point 25 cm; myopia (concave), hypermetropia (convex), presbyopia (bi-focal).
  • Microscope and telescope (refracting & reflecting).
  • Additive primaries: RGB (RGB→white; RG=yellow, RB=magenta, BG=cyan).
  • Subtractive primaries: CMY (cyan absorbs red, magenta absorbs green, yellow absorbs blue).
