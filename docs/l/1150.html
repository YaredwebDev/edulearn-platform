3.5 Relation Between Exponential and Logarithmic Functions

y=aˣ and y=logₐx are inverses of each other (a>1, and the same holds for 0<a<1 with (½)ˣ and log(½)x).

Key relations:

  • Domain of aˣ = range of logₐx.
  • Range of aˣ = domain of logₐx.
  • x-axis is the horizontal asymptote of aˣ; y-axis is the vertical asymptote of logₐx.
  • (0,1) is the y-intercept of aˣ; (1,0) is the x-intercept of logₐx.
Family of logarithmic functions
✍️ Must-know: ✍️ Must-know: The log function is the mirror image (reflection in y=x) of the exponential function with the same base. Domain/range and intercepts/asymptotes swap.
