9.3 Phrasal Verbs: count, crack, cross, cut

◌ Not started
Phrasal verb Meaning Example
count on to depend on / rely on You should count on yourself to improve your English.
count up to add together (a total) Please count up the number of tickets sold.
crack down on to take strong action against The police crack down on drink-driving offences.
cross off to delete from a list She crossed him off her Christmas-card list.
cut back to reduce The firm cut back production because sales were slow.
cut down to reduce/consume less I'm trying to cut down the coffee I drink.

✋ Trap: count on = rely/depend on; count up = add a total; crack down on = punish strongly; cross off = delete from a list; cut back / cut down = reduce. These are the exact phrasal verbs in the unit's matching exercise.
