2.7 Pulse Crops Production and Management

◌ Not started

2.2.1 Faba bean (Vicia faba)

  • Origin: Mediterranean; produced for edible seeds and as green vegetable. Altitude ~1800–3000 m; well-drained clay soils.
  • Planting: broadcast or rows 40 cm between rows, 5–10 cm between plants.
  • Weed/pest/disease: weeds, fungal diseases, insects (African ball worm, aphids) → integrated control: hand weeding (twice within 6 weeks of planting), crop rotation, clean seedbed, chemicals.
  • Harvesting: when leaves fall off and pods dry & change green→black. If for vegetable, harvest at green (immature) stage. National average ≈ 19 quintals (1.9 t)/ha.

2.2.2 Field pea (Pisum sativum)

  • Produced for protein-rich edible seeds; also animal feed. Mono crop or mixed with faba bean; rotated with teff, wheat, barley. Altitude ~1800–3000 m.
  • Planting: broadcast or rows 30 cm between rows, 5–10 cm between plants.
  • Weed/pest/disease: weeds, insects (aphids, African ball worm), fungal diseases → integrated control with manual weeding (~from 4 weeks after planting), rotation, chemicals.
  • Harvesting: when pod mature and seeds hard; delayed harvest causes seed loss (shattering). National average ≈ 14 quintals (1.4 t)/ha.

2.2.3 Haricot bean / common bean (Phaseolus vulgaris)

  • Origin: Mexico. Edible seeds; immature pods as vegetable; animal feed. Grows in most soil types, altitude ~1400–2000 m. Mono crop or mixed with maize/sorghum; rotated with teff, maize, sorghum. Seed colour: red, brown, white or grey. Important export crop of Ethiopia.
  • Planting: broadcast or rows 40 cm between rows, 10 cm between plants; in intercropping the haricot rows go between maize/sorghum rows (10 cm spacing maintained). Manure usually used for fertilisation.
  • Weed/pest: clean seedbed, manual weeding within 4 weeks; susceptible to storage pests (weevils) → dry grain to <12% moisture and treat chemically.
  • Harvesting: mature in 3–4 months; harvest when leaves fall and pods turn yellow & dry; green (immature) pods for vegetable use. National average ≈ 14 quintals (1.4 t)/ha.

✍️ Must-know (pulses): all three are dicots + nitrogen fixers; faba bean (Mediterranean, highland clay), field pea (protein, grown in rotation with cereals), haricot bean (Mexico, export crop, weevil storage pest, moisture <12%). Compare with cereal management (minimal cultivation, no mineral fertiliser in Ethiopia → low yields).
